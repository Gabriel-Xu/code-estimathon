ZebuZebu ("Bos taurus indicus"), a type of domesticated bovine cattle-like animal found in India, East Africa and China.

Zebu have humps on the shoulders, large Dewlaps and droopy ears. They are adapted to survive disease, intense heat, sun and humidity.

Zebu can begin reproducing at around 44 months old. However, early reproduction can place stress on the body and possibly shorten lifespans.
