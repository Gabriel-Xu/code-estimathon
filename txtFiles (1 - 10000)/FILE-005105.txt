Toner particles are melted by the heat of the fuser, and bind to the paper.

Since toner is a fine dust, it can remain in the air for a long time, and is considered to have health effects similar to inert dust.

Many companies selling laser printers will also provide a way to return empty toner cartridges. These are commonly re-used, with new toner. Today, many toners have chips which tell the printer how much toner is left. These chips also need to be re-programmed, when the toner is re-filled.
