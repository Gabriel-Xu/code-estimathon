Joseph MascoloJoseph Mascolo (March 13, 1929 – December 8, 2016) was an American musician and former dramatic actor.

Mascolo was born in West Hartford, Connecticut. He studied at the University of Miami and at the United States Military Academy. He began acting in 1957.
