Neonatal progeroid syndromeNeonatal progeroid syndrome, also known as Wiedemann-Rautenstrauch syndrome is an autosomal recessive progeroid syndrome. The disease results from damage to the FBN1 gene.

There have been over 51 cases of NPS. Rarely do they reach adulthood. The average life expectancy is 7 months. The syndrome was first described in 1979 by Hans-Rudolf Wiedemann, who previously described two cases of the disease: one in 1966 and another in 1977. It is associated with abnormalities in bone maturation, lipid and metabolism. It iss very rare - less than 1 in million births.
