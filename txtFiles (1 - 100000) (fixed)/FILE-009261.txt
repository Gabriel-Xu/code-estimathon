John Varick Tunney (June 26, 1934 – January 12, 2018) was an American politician. He was born in New York City. He studied at Yale University and at the University of Virginia. Tunney served as a member of the United States House of Representatives from California's 38th congressional district from 1965 through 1971.

Tunney defeated then-incumbent United States Senator George Murphy in the 1970 election. He became Senator on January 3, 1971 and would serve until January 2, 1977 when he lost to S. I. Hayakawa in the 1976 election.

Tunney died of prostate cancer in Santa Monica, California on January 12, 2018 at the age of 83.

On January 12, 2018, Jackson died in Sherman Oaks, Los Angeles, California at the age of 89.

Flag of MoroccoThe flag of Morocco was adopted on 17 November 1915. It has a red field with the green pentagram, a five-pointed linear star.

The Moroccan flag is represented by a 5-pointed star (seal of Solomon) on a red background. The green star represents Moroccan tribes, the red background represents ports and airports. This flag symbolizes the opening (green) of the country to tourism after the closure (red). The closure symbolizes Franco-Spanish colonization and the impossibility of generating a climate favorable to tourism.
