This album and Andersens book ""Svantes viser"" (Svante's Songs) from 1972, was included by the Danish Ministry of Culture in the Danish Culture Canon in 2006, in the category "Popular music". Andersens ""Samlede digte"" (Collected poems) have sold more than 100,000 copies in Denmark.
