Spencer founded a Scrabble club after her husband's death. She continued to drive until the age of 99, however, she did not drive in the dark or on motorways.

Spencer is a Christian. She said: "I'm not, sort of, deeply into religion, but I do have faith."

Spencer celebrated her 100th birthday on 14 June 2019. Speaking on her longevity, she said: "If you're born with a sense of humour, that's a good help. An awful lot depends on your outlook on life. You're born either with your glass half full or half empty. But with old age, all sorts of things wear out and you can't get yourself the spares!" She also said: "Obviously one's body deteriorates rapidly - I'm still on my feet, just. But apart from that I have the same outlook on life as I've always had. I'm an optimist. I enjoy life. I enjoy all the beautiful things that are still there for me."

Spencer had calls to be made a Dame in 2019.

Spencer was appointed Officer of the Order of the British Empire (OBE) in the 1991 Birthday Honours by Queen Elizabeth II, for her services to performing arts. Her husband and her daughter accompanied her to Buckingham Palace to collect this honour. She was appointed Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in the 2017 Birthday Honours by Queen Elizabeth II, for her services to drama and charity.

Spencer was given the Freedom of the City of London in June 2010.

Spencer was awarded an honorary degree from the University of Nottingham as a Doctor of Letters for her services to broadcasting in July 2012.

Spencer was awarded the GDST Exceptional Contribution Award from Nottingham Girls' High School in July 2022.
