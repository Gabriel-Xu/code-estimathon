Unintentional second-degree murder means he was committing another felony crime, in this case, assault. It means he was not necessarily trying to kill Floyd, but he killed him while assaulting him. Chauvin was also convicted of third-degree murder and second-degree manslaughter.

While serving his prison sentences at the FCI Tucson prison, Chauvin was stabbed by another inmate at about 12:30 p.

The Majority Leader was Republican Kevin McCarthy of California.

The Minority Leader was Democrat Nancy Pelosi of California.

The Majority Whip was Republican Steve Scalise of Louisiana.

The Minority Whip was Democrat Steny Hoyer of Maryland.

There were 51 Republicans and 47 Democrats, as well as 2 Independents who sided with Democrats.

There were 235 Republicans and just 193 Democrats, plus 7 empty seats.

Javelin frogThe javelin frog, pygmy rocket frog or cairns tree frog ("Litoria microbelos") is a small frog from northern Australia. It lives in northern and central Queensland and in Kimberly.

The frogs are less than 2 cm long, some of the smallest in Australia. They are light brown with dark brown stripes.

They lay eggs in areas that are temporarily flooded and not in permanent bodies of water like ponds. Adults hide in the grass or under rocks or logs.
