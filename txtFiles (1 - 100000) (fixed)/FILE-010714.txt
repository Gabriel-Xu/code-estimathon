Manuela BenelliManuela Benelli (born 10 March 1963 in Ravenna) is an Italian volleyball player and coach. She played at the 1990 FIVB Volleyball Women's World Championship and 1989 Women's European Volleyball Championship, winning a bronze medal.

She played for Cermagica Reggio Emilia and Olimpia Teodora Ravenna.

She coached for Roma Pallavolo, Vicenza Volley, and Volley 2002 Forlì.
