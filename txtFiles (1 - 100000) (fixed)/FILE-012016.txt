FC Juniors OÖ. is an Austrian association football club, from Pasching, Upper Austria. It was newly founded after the move of ASKÖ Pasching to Carinthia and the renaming in SK Austria Kärnten in 2007 as "FC Superfund Pasching". The greatest success of the club is winning the ÖFB-Cup as the first third division club in history.

Since the 2014–15 season they played under the name LASK Juniors OÖ playing together with the second men's team of LASK Linz in the Regionalliga Mitte. With the return to the 2nd League for the 2018–19 season the club is again an independent team and named FC Juniors Oberösterreich.

After the 2021-22 season LASK decided to go back to the Regionalliga and form again a second team of LASK Linz.

Exerodonta abdivitaThe Río Aloapan tree frog ("Exerodonta abdivita") is a frog that lives in Mexico. It lives in rainforests. It lives mostly below 1600 meters above sea level, but scientists have also seen it at 1900 meters above sea level.

Scientists used to think this frog was the same as "Hyla melanomma", but it is not.

This frog is small and has brown spots on its skin.

This frog is not endangered, but there are fewer of them than there were. This is because human beings change the places where it lives from forests into farms or other things.
