Mukesh KhannaMukesh Khanna (born 23 July 1958) is an Indian actor and producer who works in Hindi films and television. He is known for his portrayal of Shaktimaan in Doordarshan's "Shaktimaan" and Bhishma in the Mahabharata. He was also the chairman of Children's Film Society, India, Children's Film Society India, from which he resigned in February 2018. He is the founder and director of MK Films. He is currently running "The Mukesh Khanna Show" on his own YouTube channel named "Bheeshm International".
