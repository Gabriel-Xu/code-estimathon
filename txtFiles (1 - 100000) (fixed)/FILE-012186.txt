This frog used to be the only species in the genus "Anotheca", but then scientists moved it to "Triprion".

The female frog lays her eggs in holes in trees. First the male frog sits in the hole and sings. Then the female frog comes to him.

The female feeds the tadpoles by laying unfertilized eggs in the water inside the tree hole. Scientists have also seen the same female lay a second clutch of fertilized eggs in the same hole as her first clutch. Then the younger tadpoles disappear. The scientists believe that the older tadpoles eat them.

The tadpoles are white when they first hatch. Later they turn dark brown and blue-gray.
