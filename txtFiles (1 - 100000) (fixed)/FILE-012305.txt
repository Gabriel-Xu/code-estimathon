Vance AmoryVance Winkworth Amory (22 May 1949 – 2 April 2022) was a Saint Kitts and Nevis politician and cricketer. He was the Premier of Nevis, from 1992 to 2006 and from 2013 to 2017. He also was the Minister of Sports in the Nevis Island Administration.

He founded and led the Concerned Citizens' Movement. The Vance W. Amory International Airport is named after him.
