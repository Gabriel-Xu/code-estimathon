"Peaches" is a song by Canadian singer Justin Bieber featuring fellow singer-songwriter Daniel Caesar (also Canadian) and American singer-songwriter Giveon. It was released on March 19, 2021, as the fifth single from Bieber's sixth studio album, "Justice", on the same day. The song debuted at number one on the "Billboard" Hot 100. It became Bieber's seventh US number one, while it became both Caesar and Giveon's first.
