Governor of Bagmati ProvinceThe governor of Bagmati is the Constitution head of province executive of Bagmati Province. The governor is appointed by the President of Nepal on recommended by federal cabinet for a tern of five years, and office at the President's pleasure. The role was first created in 2018.

The current governor is Yadav Chandra Sharma who was appointed and took office on 20 August 2021. The position has been held by three people in a permanent capacity since its creation.

The governor is de jure head of the provincial government; all its executive actions are taken in the governor's name. However, the governor must act on the advice of the popularly elected council of ministers, headed by the chief minister, which thus holds de facto executive authority at the province-level. The Constitution of Nepal also empowers the governor to act upon his or her own discretion, such as the ability to appoint or dismiss a ministry, recommend president's rule for the president's assent.

Article 164 of the Constitution of Nepal states that:1. being qualified for being a2. member of the Federal Parliament,3. having completed the age of thirty five years, andnot being disqualified by any law.
