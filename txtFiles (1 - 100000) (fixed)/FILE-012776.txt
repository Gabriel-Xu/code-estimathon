The Center for Civil Liberties () is a Ukrainian human rights organization led by the Ukrainian lawyer Oleksandra Matviichuk. It was founded in 2007. It was created to pressure Ukraine's government to make the country more democratic. The organization was awarded the 2022 Nobel Peace Prize, with Ales Bialiatski and Russian organization Memorial.

Oleksandra MatviichukOleksandra Vyacheslavivna Matviichuk (; born 8 October 1983) is a Ukrainian human rights lawyer and civil society. She is in charge of the non-profit organization Centre for Civil Liberties. She is an activist for democratic reforms in Ukraine and the OSCE region.

In 2022, she is awarded Right Livelihood Award with Centre for Civil Liberties.

Ales BialiatskiAles Viktaravich Bialiatski (; born 25 September 1962) is a Belarusian pro-democracy activist and prisoner of conscience. He is known for his work with the Viasna Human Rights Centre. He is a well known activist for Belarusian independence and democracy since the early 1980s.

Bialiatski is a founding member of Viasna and the Belarusian Popular Front. He is also a member of the Coordination Council of the Belarusian opposition.

In 2022, Bialiatski was awarded the 2022 Nobel Peace Prize, along with the organisations Memorial and Centre for Civil Liberties.

Bialiatski has been in prison twice. The first time was from 2011 to 2014. His second time has been since 2021 because of charges of tax evasion, but many see his arrest as politically motivated. On 3 March 2023, a Belarusian court sentenced Bialiatski to prison for 10 years.

Svante PääboSvante Pääbo (; born 20 April 1955) is a Swedish geneticist. His works focused on evolutionary genetics. He is one of the founders of paleogenetics. He has worked on the Neanderthal genome. He is also an adjunct professor at Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology, Japan.

In 2022, he won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for his works about genomes of extinct hominins and human evolution.

Amasra mine explosionOn 14 October 2022, an explosion happened in Amasra coal mine in Amasra, Bartın Province, Turkey. The explosion killed 41 people and injured 11 others. It was one of the deadliest industrial incidents in Turkey.

Hurricane Julia (2022)Hurricane Julia was a deadly 2022 tropical cyclone. There were seventy-six deaths or more from the storm. It struck northern Venezuela and Colombia as a tropical disturbance. The storm later struck Nicaragua, Honduras and El Salvador as a hurricane or tropical storm. Julia also affected Mexico in several different areas.

Julia struck the island of San Andres on October 8, 2022 and Nicaragua to El Salvador the following day. The storm later headed into the Eastern Pacific Ocean. Winds were 85 to 90 miles per hour when it struck San Andres and eastern Nicaragua on October 9, 2022.
