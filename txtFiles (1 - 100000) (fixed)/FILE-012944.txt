"Tell Her About It" is a 1983 song by Billy Joel. It is the leading single from his ninth studio album "An Innocent Man". It went to number 1 in the United States, number 2 in Ireland, number 3 in South Africa, number 4 in the United Kingdom, number 5 in Canada, number 9 in Australia and number 12 in New Zealand.

Sweetest Pie"Sweetest Pie" is a pop rap song by American rapper Megan Thee Stallion and British singer Dua Lipa, released on March 11, 2022. It is from Stallion's second studio album Traumazine, as it is the lead single in that album. Comercially, the song has peaked through the top 10 in Canada, Hungary, The Netherlands, and The United States.
