Leandro NegreLeandro Negre Carrió (born 15 June 1946) is a Spanish former field hockey player and field hockey administrator.

Negre played for the Spain men's national field hockey team. He won with the team the silver medal at the 1963 Mediterranean Games in Napoli. He was also part of the Spanish team at the 1968 Summer Olympics, but didn't play.

Negre was the president of the International Hockey Federation between 2008 and 2016.

Negre received the silver Olympic Order in 2016.
