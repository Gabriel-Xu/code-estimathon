Means testA means test is a check that people are poor enough to get help. Social security, education and healthcare are often free only to people who can show that they are poor.

Poverty is generally judged in a family or household. The members are expected to support each other. There may be tests both of regular income and of capital. People who are thought to be deserving are more likely to be helped without a means test.

Means testing can be found in ancient Rome, where 200,000 adult male citizens who passed the test were given cheap grain.
