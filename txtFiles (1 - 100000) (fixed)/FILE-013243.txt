Bluey: The AlbumBluey: The Album is an album released on 22 January 2021 by Demon Records. The album has music from "Bluey".

Man in the Box"Man in the Box" is a 1991 song by Alice in Chains. It is the second single from their debut studio album "Facelift". It went to number 18 on the Billboard Mainstream Rock chart. It was nominated for a Grammy Award in 1992. It was later included on their greatest hits compilation albums.

Little L"Little L" is a 2001 song by English funk band Jamiroquai. It is the leading single from their fifth studio album "A Funk Odyssey". It went to number 1 in Spain, number 2 in Italy, number 5 in Finland, Portugal and the United States, number 6 in Hungary and Scotland, number 7 in Canada, number 14 in Australia, number 16 in Ireland, number 17 in Switzerland, number 20 in Norway and number 22 in France.

Emergency on Planet Earth (song)"Emergency on Planet Earth" is a 1993 song by British funk band Jamiroquai and is the title track to their debut studio album of the same name. It went to number 14 in the United Kingdom and number 4 on the Billboard Dance Club Songs chart in the United States.

Fear Factor: Khatron Ke KhiladiFear Factor: Khatron Ke Khiladi () also known as Khatron Ke Khiladi is an Indian Hindi-language stunt based reality television series based on the American series "Fear Factor". It was initially launched as "Fear Factor India" on Sony TV, later it was sold to Colors TV and was relaunched as "Fear Factor: Khatron Ke Khiladi" on 21 July 2008. A spin-off of the main-series was launched on 1 August 2020.
