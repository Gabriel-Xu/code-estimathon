One of the defining characteristics of Gascony is its warm and welcoming people. The Gascons, known for their friendly nature and relaxed way of life, take great pride in their regional heritage. The Gascon language, Occitan, still survives in pockets of the region, adding to the cultural richness and diversity. Visitors will find themselves immersed in a community that cherishes its traditions, from the lively festivals celebrating local cuisine and wine to the age-old rituals passed down through generations.

Speaking of cuisine, Gascony is a gastronomic paradise. Renowned for its hearty and flavoursome dishes, the region boasts a culinary heritage that is deeply rooted in its rural traditions. Gascon cuisine celebrates the use of locally sourced ingredients, particularly duck, foie gras, and Armagnac brandy. Cassoulet, a slow-cooked casserole of white beans, sausages, and duck confit, is a signature dish that epitomizes the region's rustic flavors. Food enthusiasts can explore bustling farmers' markets, sample artisanal cheeses, and savour the delicate flavours of renowned wines like Madeiran and Jurançon.

Beyond its delectable cuisine, Gascony offers breath taking landscapes that showcase the region's natural beauty. From rolling hills and vineyards to picturesque medieval towns and fortified villages, Gascony is a haven for those seeking tranquillity and a slower pace of life. The Pyrenees Mountains provide a majestic backdrop for outdoor enthusiasts, offering opportunities for hiking, skiing, and exploring charming mountain villages. The Atlantic coastline, dotted with pristine sandy beaches and charming seaside resorts, is perfect for those seeking sun, sea, and relaxation.

Gascony is also a haven for history and culture buffs. The region is home to numerous castles, churches, and historic sites that bear witness to its rich past. The picturesque town of Auch, with its magnificent Gothic cathedral and historic centre, is a must-visit. The fortified village of Larressingle, known as the "Carthage of the Gers," transports visitors back to medieval times. Additionally, Gascony is renowned for its connection to the musketeer legend, as the birthplace of the famous d'Artagnan, one of the legendary characters from Alexandre Dumas' novel "The Three Musketeers."

In conclusion, Gascony is a captivating region that offers a unique blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. Its warm and welcoming people, delectable cuisine, and stunning landscapes make it an ideal destination for travellers seeking an authentic French experience. Gascony invites visitors to immerse themselves in its rich heritage, savour its culinary delights, and embrace its laid-back way of life. Whether exploring medieval towns, indulging in hearty meals, or simply enjoying the tranquillity of the countryside, Gascony promises an unforgettable journey through the heart of southwestern France.

Anna ShayAnna Erika Shay (January 30, 1961 — June 1, 2023) was an American socialite, businesswoman, philanthropist, and television personality. She was best known as a cast member on the Netflix reality television series "Bling Empire".

Anna Erika Shay was born in Tokyo, Japan, on January 30, 1961, as the eldest of two children to Edward Shay, an American businessman, and Ai Oizumi Shay, a Japanese-Russian businesswoman who came from Russian aristocracy. Her younger brother was Allen Shay. Her older half-brother from her mother's first marriage was Jun Oizumi.

Shay graduated from the Buckley School, a private school in Sherman Oaks. She attended the University of Southern California and other colleges.

Shay made her television debut on January 15, 2021, as an original cast member on the Netflix reality television series "Bling Empire". She quickly became a breakout star and was often named a fan-favorite. When Jeff Jenkins, the show's executive producer, approached her with the opportunity to join the show, Shay thought she was being asked to help in a behind-the-scenes consultant capacity. She appeared in 22 episodes.

Shay was a board member for the George Lopez Foundation; raising awareness about kidney disease and organ donation.

Shay had an estimated net worth of $600 million at the time of her death. Shay's date of birth was January 30, 1961, however, the media have consistently reported her birth date to be December 31, 1960.

Shay had been married and divorced four times. One of her ex-husbands was from Ireland, with whom she visited Ashford Castle once.

Shay's marriage to Kenneth John Kemp, a third generation firefighter with the LAFD, lasted three years and resulted in the birth of her only son, Kenneth Edward "Kenny" Shay-Kemp, on October 5, 1993. She raised her son as a single mother after the couple divorced. Her legal name during this marriage was Anna Erika Kemp, later switching back to Shay. She had one granddaughter.

Shay died from a stroke on June 1, 2023. She was 62.
