"Opp Stoppa" is the first single off of American rapper YBN Nahmir's first studio album "Visionland". The song was released on June 10, 2019. A remix for the song featuring British-American rapper 21 Savage was released on January 22, 2021. Nahmir said the song was about his life but said that it didn't really relate to him anymore. He also described an "Opp Stoppa" as "Something to use against another human being. Someone that's not on your side, I guess. Like, it could be like a gun, a sword, anything."

A second remix featuring American rapped Lil Eazzyy was released on April 23, 2021.

Mammas Don't Let Your Babies Grow Up to Be Cowboys"Mammas Don't Let Your Babies Grow Up to Be Cowboys" is a song off of American country musician Ed Bruce's third self-titled studio album. The song was released on November 15, 1975. The song was able to make it to #15 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles chart.

The song was named as one of the Top 100 Western songs of all time by the Western Writers of America.

A very popular cover by Waylon Jennings and Willie Nelson was released in January 1978. It appeared on their 1978 duet album "Waylon & Willie". Jennings and Nelson's version able to make it to #1 on the Hot Country Singles chart and #42 on the "Billboard" Hot 100. Their version won "Best Country Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocal" at the 1979 Grammy Award.

The song is about warns mothers to not let their children grow up to be cowboys because of the hard way of life. It tells them that they should let their children be doctors, lawyers or some other job.

Septic tankA septic tank is an underground chamber made of concrete, fiberglass, or plastic through which sewage flows for basic sewage treatment. Settling and anaerobic digestion processes reduce solids and organic material. The treated liquid effluent is commonly disposed of in a septic drain field. This provides further treatment. Nonetheless, groundwater pollution may occur and can be a problem.

The term "septic" refers to the anaerobic bacterial environment that develops in the tank that decomposes or mineralizes the waste discharged into the tank. Septic tanks can be coupled with other onsite wastewater treatment units such as biofilters or aerobic systems involving artificially forced aeration.

A septic tank has one or more concrete or plastic tanks between 4,500 and 7,500 liters (1,000 and 2,000 gallons). One end is connected to a pipe coming from a building and the other to a septic drain field. These pipe connections are made with a T-shaped pipe, allowing liquid to enter and exit without disturbing anything on the top of the water. New tanks usually have two chambers. Each has an access opening and cover and is separated by a dividing wall with openings located about midway between the floor and the roof of the tank.
