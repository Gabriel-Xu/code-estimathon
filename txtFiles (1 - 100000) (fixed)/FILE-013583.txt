Austin Matelson (born March 10, 1985) is an American professional wrestler. He currently wrestles for All Elite Wrestling (AEW) and competes under the ring names Luchasaurus and currently Killswitch.

He is also known for his time wrestling in WWE where he competed in "NXT" and wrestled under the ring name Judas Devlin and in Lucha Underground where he wrestled under the ring name Vibora.

During his time in AEW, Luchasaurus has been a one-time AEW TNT Champion and a one-time AEW World Tag Team Champion.

Matelson was a contestant on season 17 of the American reality television show "Big Brother".

On May 25, 2019 at Double or Nothing, Luchasaurus made his debut with All Elite Wrestling (AEW). He competed in the Casino Battle Royal and made it to the final three but lost to Adam Page. On May 28, it was announced that Luchasaurus had signed a contract to be a member of All Elite Wrestling (AEW). Luchasaurus would team with Jungle Boy as A Boy And His Dinosaur. They would later be joined by Marko Stunt and would create a stable called Jurassic Express.

On the November 20 episode of "Dynamite", Luchasaurus would get his first AEW win when he defeated Peter Avalon in 27 seconds.

On the August 18, 2021 episode of "Dynamite", Jurassic Express would face The Young Bucks for the AEW World Tag Team Championships but lost. On the January 5, 2022 episode of "Dynamite", Jurassic Express won the AEW World Tag Team Championship after defeating the Lucha Brothers (Pentagón Jr. and Rey Fénix). This was Luchasaurus' first championship in AEW. On June 15 at Road Rager, Jurassic Express would lose the championships to the Young Bucks. After the match, Luchasaurus and Christian Cage would turn heel. Christian would attack Jungle Boy and Luchasaurus would leave with him.

On the June 17 debut episode of "Collision", Luchasaurus would defeat Wardlow to win the AEW TNT Championship. On the September 23 episode of "Collision", Luchasaurus lost the title to Christian Cage in a three-way match that also featured Darby Allin. Cage had claimed that Luchasaurus' TNT Championship was his own in the months leading up to the match.

On the November 22 episode of "Dynamite", Christian Cage renamed Luchasaurus and began calling him Killswitch after Cage's old finisher name.

National Soccer LeagueThe National Soccer League (NSL) was a professional soccer league in Australia and New Zealand. It had 28 seasons and existed from 1977 until 2004, when it was replaced by the A-League. It was run by Soccer Australia.

The NSL was actually one of Australia's oldest nationwide sports leagues, being older than the Australian Football League (AFL) in Australian rules football and the National Rugby League (NRL) in rugby league.

Throughout its history, the NSL had 42 teams, 41 based in Australia and one based in New Zealand. In all 28 seasons, 13 clubs have won the grand final at least once.

While the clubs were not restricted to people of a certain background, many of the teams had strong connections to multicultural communities in Australia. Many of the clubs were founded by migrants from Croatia, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Malta, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom and Yugoslavia, and one club was founded by the Jewish community.

Throughout its history, clubs came from Adelaide, Auckland, Brisbane, Canberra/Queanbeyan, Melbourne, Morwell, Newcastle, Perth, Sydney and Wollongong.

Miriam Korber-BercoviciMiriam Korber-Bercovici (née Miriam Korber; born ) is a Romanian-born Jewish oncologist who kept a diary in which she documented life during the Holocaust. A Holocaust survivor, she gained some popularity through the 1995 publication of her diary "Jurnal de Ghetto" (Romanian for "Diary from a Ghetto"), in which she documents her life in a ghetto in Romania from 1941 to 1943, during World War II.

Miriam was born in in Campulung, Moldovenesc, Kingdom of Romania (in modern-day Romania). In 1937, when she was 13 or 14, Octavian Goga and his government came into power and they began to establish anti-Semitic laws and began persecuting Jews all over Romania. As the anti-Semitic laws and the persecution of the Jews increased in October 1941, the Korber family lived in a ghetto (They paid an German civilian living in Romania to sent them to live in the ghetto). The family lived in a apartment in the ghetto. Miriam kept a diary which she wrote in it regularly until October 1943 when the family were deported. Following their deportation, the Korber family were sent to live in labor camps. The family successfully managed to escape the labor camp (they were in) and move back to the ghetto. In March 1944, following the Soviet occupation of Romania, the family and other Jews were freed from the ghetto by the Soviets. In April of that same year, the family started going on a journey back home. They reached their homes in May. Miriam fulfilled her greatest wish and became a physician (she was a oncologist). Miriam married Israil Bercovici in 1951 (she got the married name Miriam Korber-Bercovici) and their child (daughter) named Ada was born two years later in .

Miriam published her diary under the title, "Jurnal de Ghetto" (English: "Diary from a Ghetto") in 1995. Miriam is still alive to this day.
