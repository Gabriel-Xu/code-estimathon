At Full Gear on November 7, 2020, the Bucks defeated FTR (Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler) to win the AEW Tag Team Championships. This was their first championships in AEW.

On the April 7 episode of "Dynamite", after months of being berated by Don Callis and questions about wether they would join Kenny Omega, the Bucks would eventually join Omega and turn heel.

At All Out on September 5, 2021, the Bucks would lose the titles to the Lucha Brothers in a steel cage match. Both Dave Meltzer and Bryan Alvarez said that their match was the best cage match that they had ever seen, and that it was one of the best AEW matches in the companie's history. Meltzer would rate the match 5.

75 stars.

At Road Rager on June 15, 2022, the Bucks would win the AEW World Tag Team Championships again when they defeated Jurassic Express (Jungle Boy and Luchasaurus) in a ladder match. On July 13, during Night 1 of Fyter Fest, the Bucks would lose the titles to Swerve in Our Glory (Swerve Strickland and Keith Lee) in a Triple or Nothing match that also featured Team Taz (Ricky Starks and Powerhouse Hobbs).

On the August 17 episode of "Dynamite", the Bucks revealed their mystery partner for the tournament to crown the first AEW World Trios Champions. Their partner was revealed to be Kenny Omega, who returned after a long time away while healing from injuries. They would win the Championships against Adam Page and The Dark Order (Alex Reynolds, and John Silver) at All Out. However, they were later suspended and stripped of them because of a real-life backstage fight that took place between Omega, the Young Bucks, CM Punk, and Ace Steel.

The Young Bucks and Omega would return from their suspension on November 19, 2022 and faced Death Triangle at Full Gear but lost.

When they were feuding with the Blackpool Combat Club, the Bucks and Omega said that they would they would have a mystery partner join them for their fight at Blood & Guts. On the July 13 episode of "Dynamite", the partner was revealed to be Kota Ibushi. They would defeat the Blackpool Combat Club, Konosuke Takeshita, and Pac.
