The SAM Labs were tasked with finding a way to separate out this rare isotope, which made up only 0.

Eventually, the team was able to successfully enrich uranium to 23 percent, which was used in the Little Boy bomb dropped on Hiroshima. Libby's work on the gaseous diffusion process was essential in the development of the atomic bomb.
