Adrian James Croce is the self-titled fifth album by American singer-songwriter A. J. Croce, released in 2004 (see 2004 in music).

Adrian James Madise (born March 23, 1980) is a former American football wide receiver.

Adrian James Toole (born 12 June 1965) is an Australian former professional rugby league player of the 1980s and 1990s.

Adrian Jardine (born 23 August 1933) is a British sailor.
