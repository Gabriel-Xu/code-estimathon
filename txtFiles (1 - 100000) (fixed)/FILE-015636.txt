After curacies in Forest Gate and Squirrels Heath he was Priest in charge of St Michael, Gidea Park from 1931 to 1938; and Vicar there from 1938 until his appointment as Archdeacon.

After Curacies in Gillingham and Felixstowe he was Rector of Komga from 1946 to 1949.

After curacies in Glasgow and Perth he was Priest in charge of Lochgelly from 1947 to 1954.

After curacies in Guelph and Toronto he was the incumbent at St John Norway before his appointment as Dean.

After curacies in Halesowen and Heywood he was Team Vicar of Hereford from 1987 to 1993.

After curacies in Harrogate and Bramley he was Vicar of St Mary, Harrogate from 1972 to 1978; then St Mary with St David, Beeston until 1992.

After curacies in Hereford and Shoreham by Sea he was with BBC Local radio from 1988 to 1992.

After curacies in Holyhead, Llanaber and Aberystwyth he was a Minor Canon at Bangor Cathedral.

After curacies in Hunslet and Burley he held Incumbencies in Wibsey, Bradford, Drypool, Routh, Wawne and Micklegate.

After curacies in Kentish Town and Lower Edmonton he was Chaplain at Parel during 1915 then a Chaplain to the British Armed Forces in Mesopotamia from 1916 until 1919.

After his years as Archdeacon he held Incumbencies at East Barnet and Woburn.

After curacies in Kettering and San Fernando he was at Queen's Royal College from 1911: eventually becoming its Principal in 1938.

After Curacies in Kileevan and Drumreilly he was the incumbent at Kilmactranny from 1893 until 1910.

After curacies in King's Lynn and Croydon he was Companion Team Priest at St Barnabas, Christchurch, New Zealand from 1996 to 1998.

After curacies in Kinsale and Clonmelhe held incumbencies at Desertserges and Cork.

After curacies in Lambeth and Ashford he was Vicar of Boxley from 1943 to 1954; and then of Thanet from 1954 to 1968.

After curacies in Lampeter and Brecon he held incumbencies in Wrexham and Bistre.

After curacies in Leeds he was chaplain at the University of Cape Town from 1966 until 1972.

After Curacies in Leeds, Uffington and High Ercall he went out to South Africa in 1874.

After curacies in Leyton and Orpington he was a tutor at Oak Hill Theological College from 1962 to 1972.

After curacies in Linwood and Ashburton he held incumbencies at Marchwiel, Burwood and Merivale.

After curacies in Lisburn and Finaghy he held incumbencies in Ballynure and Skerry.

After curacies in Llandeilo and Swansea he was Vicar of Brwyngwyn from 1963 to 1970.

After curacies in Llandyfrydog and Carnarvon he held incumbencies at Llangadwaladr and Welshpool.

After curacies in Llangefni and Abergavenney he held incumbencies in Goytre and Llwynypia.

After curacies in Llanilid and Pontypridd he was Vicar of Tonyrefail then Rector of Neath until his appointment as Archdeacon.

After curacies in Llanrhos and Aberystwyth he became Rector of Llanllyfni in 1938, a post he held for 11 years.

After curacies in Lockwood and Narberth he went as a Convict Chaplain to Fremantle in 1853; and Rector of York from 1855 to 1858.

After curacies in London he was on the staff of St Stephen's House, Oxford from 2001 until 2009; then Vicar of Clay Hill until his appointment as Archdeacon.

After curacies in London he went to Australia to join the Bush Brotherhood and served in the Anglican Diocese of Carpentaria from 1958 to 1963.

After curacies in Maryborough and Preban He held incumbencies at Schull, Ballydehob and Templebreedy.

After Curacies in Mexborough, Doncaster and Hong Kong he went to South Africa in 1929.

After curacies in Middlesbrough and New Earswick he held incumbencies at Brayton and Saltburn by the Sea.

After curacies in Middlesbrough and Torquay he became Vicar of All Saints, Cambridge, a post he held until 1922.

After curacies in Middleton Tyas and Bath he went as a Chaplain to Madras.
