A general election was held in Mississippi on November 4, 2003 to elect to 4 year terms all members of the state legislature (122 representatives, 52 senators), the offices of Governor, Lieutenant Governor, Attorney General, State Treasurer, State Auditor, Secretary of State, Commissioner of Agriculture and Commerce, and Commissioner of Insurance, plus all three members of the Transportation Commission and all three members of the Public Service Commission.

A general election was held in Myanmar on 7 November 2010, in accordance with the new constitution which was approved in a referendum held in May 2008.

A general election was held in Nicaragua on November 4, 1984, to elect a president and parliament.

A general election was held in Nicaragua to elect a president and parliament on February 25, 1990.

A general election was held in Nicaragua to elect a president and parliament on October 20, 1996.

A general election was held in Samoa on 4 March 2011, in which the people elected the 49 seats of the Fono for its 15th term.
