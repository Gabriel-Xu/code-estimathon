A keen motorsport enthusiast and driver from a young age, he earned a first class honors degree in mechanical engineering from the New South Wales University of Technology.

A keen music fan, Peach has recorded backing vocals on songs for Elton John and Hall & Oates.

A keen naturalist, he also manages a Local Nature Reserve near his home in St Leonards for the Sussex Wildlife Trust.

A keen photographer and researcher, he conducted research and wrote several books on a variety of topics.

A keen sailor, Laura Duncan won the Scottish ladies' single-handed dinghy championships in 1990.

A keen scuba diver and photographer, he has also made a series of documentaries with world champion freediver, Tanya Streeter.

A keen skier both downhill and cross country; a cyclist and Qigong teacher.

A Keen Soldier was published by Vintage Canada in 2002.
