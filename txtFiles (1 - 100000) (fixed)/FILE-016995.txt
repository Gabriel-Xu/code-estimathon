Although her career as a student remained in Michigan, Weiske has taught at various colleges and universities all over the U.

S. including Penland School of Crafts in Penland, North Carolina, Arrowmont School of Arts and Crafts in Gatlinburg, Tennessee, and Haystack School of Crafts in Deer Isle, Maine.

Although her career in London was cut short by illness, she was one of the first Scottish women artists to make a career from painting, and according to Colin Russell "her importance remains in her struggle against gender prejudice".

Although her career lasted well beyond the 1930s, she is sometimes grouped with writers of the Harlem Renaissance.
