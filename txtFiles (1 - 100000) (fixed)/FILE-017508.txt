Amrit Maan is a Punjabi singer, lyricist and actor.

Amrit Maghera is a London based professional model turned actress, working in Hindi, English and Punjabi films.

Amrit Manthan (English: The Changes of Amrit) is an Indian television soap opera, which premiered on 26 February 2012 and ran through 2 August 2013.

Amritpal Chotu is Punjabi and Bollywood comedian and actor.

Amritpal Singh (born 6 October 1983 in Sangrur, Punjab) is an Indian track and field athlete from Punjab who specializes in long jump.

Amritpal Singh (born January 5, 1991) is an Indian professional basketball player who last played for the Sydney Kings of the National Basketball League (NBL).
