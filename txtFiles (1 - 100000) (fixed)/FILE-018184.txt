Antitermination is the prokaryotic cell's aid to fix premature termination of RNA synthesis during the transcription of RNA.

Antitermination is used by some phages to regulate progression from one stage of gene expression to the next.

Antitermination provides a mechanism whereby one or more genes at the end of an operon can be switched either on or off, depending on the polymerase either recognizing or not recognizing the termination signal.
