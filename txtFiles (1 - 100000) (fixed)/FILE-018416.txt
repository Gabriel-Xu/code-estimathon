Applications were open from 11 October 2011 to 31 October 2011.

Applications were submitted through the Intel AppUp developer program.

Applications were to contain samples of drawings, photographs of paintings and a resume.

Applications were written by ICL partner companies and use of the development tools was not mainstream.

Applications where multi-agent systems research may deliver an appropriate approach include online trading, disaster response and social structure modeling.

Applications which allow cuing of weapon systems are referred to as helmet-mounted sight and display (HMSD) or helmet-mounted sights (HMS).
