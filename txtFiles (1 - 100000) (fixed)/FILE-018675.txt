Arkansas's musical heritage includes country music and various related styles like bluegrass and rockabilly.

Arkansas (sometimes spelled Arkansaw) is an unincorporated community in Hardy County, West Virginia.

Arkansas's per capita income for 2010 was $36,027.

Arkansas Sports Hall of Fame is the hall of fame and museum for sports in Arkansas, United States.

Arkansas's Senate seats were declared vacant in July 1861, due to its secession from the Union.

Arkansas State had 5 victories forfeited by the NCAA during the 2005 season.

Arkansas State has played a SEC school for the past three years.

Arkansas State has Sun Belt rivalries with all West Division schools (Little Rock, Louisiana, Louisiana-Monroe, Texas State, and UT Arlington).

Arkansas State Hospital, originally known as Arkansas Lunatic Asylum, is a public psychiatric hospital in Little Rock, Arkansas, established in 1883.

Arkansas State joined Division I in 1974.
