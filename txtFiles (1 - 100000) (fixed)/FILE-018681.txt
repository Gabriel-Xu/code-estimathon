Arlinda Dudaj (born 7 October 1977, in Tirana) is an Albanian publisher who currently serves as the president of "Dudaj" Publishing House.

Arlind Afrim Ajeti (born 25 September 1993) is an Albanian professional footballer who plays as a center-back for Italian club Crotone on loan from Torino and the Albania national team.

Arlinda Locklear (born 1951) is an American lawyer of Native American origin from the Lumbee tribe.

Arlindo Cruz (born September 14, 1958, birth name Arlindo Domingos da Cruz Filho) is a Brazilian musician, composer and singer, working in the genre of samba and pagode.

Arlindo dos Santos Cruz (born 26 April 1943) is a Brazilian former footballer.
