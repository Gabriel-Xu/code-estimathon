As a head coach, Williams has coached in a total of six NCAA championship games (1991, 2003, 2005, 2009, 2016, and 2017).

As a head coach with the Denver Nuggets in the National Basketball Association (NBA), he was named the NBA Coach of the Year in 1988.

As a head director, he directed 2nd Unit on "Pitch Perfect 3", an episode of Clueless and the pilot episode of Shasta McNasty, both in 1999.
