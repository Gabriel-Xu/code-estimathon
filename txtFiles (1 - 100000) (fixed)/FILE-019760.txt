As with other members of the Field Elm group, the taxonomy of Plot Elm has been a matter of contention, several authorities recognizing it as a species in its own right.

As with other members of the genus, A. glaesaria was most likely trophobiotic.

As with other members of their family, Termitidae, they have lost most of their cellulose-digesting protozoa.

As with other members of the large diterpene family of natural products, forskolin is derived from geranylgeranyl pyrophosphate (GGPP).

As with other members of the mint family their upper petal (or lip) is divided into two lobes.
