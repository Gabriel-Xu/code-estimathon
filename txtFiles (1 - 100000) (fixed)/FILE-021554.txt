Biogeographic realms correspond to the floristic kingdoms of botany or zoogeographic regions of zoology.

Biogeography is the study of the distribution of species (biology), organisms, and ecosystems in geographic space and through geological time.

Biogeomorphology and ecogeomorphology are the study of interactions between organisms and the development of landforms, and are thus fields of study within geomorphology and ichnology.

Biogeomorphology is a subdisclipline of Geomorphology.

Biogeophysics is a subdiscipline of geophysics concerned with how plants, microbial activity and other organisms alter geologic materials and affect geophysical signatures.

Biogeosciences is an open-access peer-reviewed scientific journal publishing research within Earth science.

Biogerontologists have continued efforts to find and validate biomarkers of aging, but success thus far has been limited.
