Cannabis in Afghanistan has been cultivated for centuries, and experienced relatively little interference until the 1970s, whereafter it became an issue both in international politics and in the finance of the series of wars which occurred in Afghanistan for forty years.

Cannabis in Alabama is illegal for all purposes since 1931; first-time possession of personal amounts is a misdemeanor crime, but repeated possession or possession with intent to sell is a felony.

Cannabis in Alaska is legal for recreational use, following a successful 2014 ballot initiative.

Cannabis in Albania is illegal, but the drug is widely cultivated.
