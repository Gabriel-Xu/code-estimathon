Coronosaurus means "crowned lizard", coming from "corona", Latin for crown, and "sauros", Greek for lizard; this name refers to the unique, crown-like shape of the horns on the top of its frill.

Coronostomatoidea is a superfamily of nematodes.

Coron School of Fisheries is a public vocational high school in Coron, Palawan.

Coronula diadema is a species of whale barnacle known to live on humpback whales, and possibly on sperm whales and finback whales.
