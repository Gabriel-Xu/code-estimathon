Cytokine release syndrome is a form of systemic inflammatory response syndrome that arises as a complication of some diseases or infections, and is also an adverse effect of some monoclonal antibody drugs, as well as adoptive T-cell therapies.

Cytokines are a family of secreted proteins involved in immunoregulatory and inflammatory processes.

Cytokines are small proteins that have an essential role in the immune and inflammatory responses.

Cytokinesis in green algae occurs via a diverse range of mechanisms, including cleavage furrows in some algae and cell plates in others.
