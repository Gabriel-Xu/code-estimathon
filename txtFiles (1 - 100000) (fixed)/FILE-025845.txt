Devathai is an Indian Tamil-language soap opera that airs on Sun TV from 1 July 2013 to 20 August 2016 at 12:00PM IST for 924 episodes.

Devatha is a 1965 Indian Malayalam film, directed by K Padmanabhan Nair and produced by Bharathi Menon.

Devathe (translation: Village Deity) is a 2001 Indian Kannada religious film written and directed by Sai Prakash.
