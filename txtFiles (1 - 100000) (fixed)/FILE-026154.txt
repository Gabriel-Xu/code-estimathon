Dockers is a brand of khaki garments and other accessories from Levi Strauss & Co. Levi Strauss & Co., then specializing in denim, introduced the Dockers brand in 1986.

Dockers makes belts, pants, leather wallets, shoes and other apparel.

Dockery continues to serve the game of football by co-organizing a youth football camp with Joe Namath that is in this 44th year.

Dockery has also been a member of the St. Louis Rams and Pittsburgh Steelers.

Dockery has served as Chair of the Board of Directors for the Council for Christian Colleges and Universities.
