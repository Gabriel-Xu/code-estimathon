Donoghue graduated from the University of California, Santa Cruz, with honors degrees in Russian Studies and in Biology, in 1978, and the Boalt Hall School of Law, of the University of California, Berkeley, with her Juris Doctor, in 1981.

In the 1980s, Donoghue acted as attorney-advisor for the U.

S. in Nicaragua v. United States.

Donoghue has been praised throughout the characters run, being labeled a "hunk" and a "hottie".

Donoghue is a member of the Health and Government Operations Committee.

Donoghue received the United States' highest award for bravery during combat, the Medal of Honor, for his action during the Battle of Fredericksburg in Virginia on 13 December 1862.

Donoghue represented the United States at the 1983 Pan American Games, where he finished second overall in the men's 1500 meters.

Donoghue stayed with the role until he quit in late 2011.

Donoghue v Folkestone Properties Limited (2003) (3 All ER 1101; 2 WLR 1138) is an English court case heard in the Court of Appeal of England and Wales concerning the tort of occupiers' liability from the Occupiers' Liability Act 1984.

Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] UKHL 100 was a landmark court decision in Scots delict law and English tort law by the House of Lords.

Donoghue was a member of the Naomh Conaill side that won their first ever Donegal Senior Football Championship in 2005.
