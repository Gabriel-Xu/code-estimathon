Edward Alexander Anderson, known as Ed Anderson, is an organic chemist based at the University of Oxford; in 2016, the University awarded him the title of Professor of Organic Chemistry.

Edward Alexander (born 1936) is an American essayist and professor emeritus of English at the University of Washington.

Edward Alexander Bott (nickname, Ned; April 11, 1887 - 1974) was a psychologist at the University of Toronto.
