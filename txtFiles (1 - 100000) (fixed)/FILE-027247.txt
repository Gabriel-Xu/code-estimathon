Ekeren is a northern district of the municipality of Antwerp in the Flemish Region of Belgium.

Ekeren is home to the Jozef Pauly municipal academy for music and word, which has around 2000 pupils as of 2004 (also counting branches in some other districts of the city of Antwerp, and one in the nearby municipality of Kapellen).

Ekeren used to be the home town of the Germinal Ekeren football team until Germinal merged with K. Beerschot V.
