Emanuele Guidi (born September 16, 1969) is a Sammarinese professional archer.

Emanuele "Lino" Saputo, CM OQ (born June 10, 1937) is an Italian-Canadian businessman.

Emanuele Liuzzi (born 22 December 1990) is an Italian rower.
