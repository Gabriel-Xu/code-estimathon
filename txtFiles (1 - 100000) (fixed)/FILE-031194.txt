Hartshill Castle was a castle in the village of Hartshill on the outskirts of Nuneaton, Warwickshire (grid reference SP325942).

Hartshill is a large village in the borough of North Warwickshire, England.

Hartshill is a township within the city of Stoke-on-Trent in the English county of Staffordshire.

Hartshill is notable because of the writer Michael Drayton who knew William Shakespeare.
