He has been the Dean of Christ Church, Oxford, since October 2014 and was previously Principal of Ripon College Cuddesdon, Oxford.

He has been the Dean of Dunedin since March 17, 2009.

He has been the dean of engineering at Concordia University, the chair of the Chemical Engineering Department at the University of Saskatchewan, and the Professor of Chemical and Mechanical Engineering at Saskatchewan and Concordia Universities respectively.

He has been the defensive coordinator for the Montreal Alouettes, Calgary Stampeders, and Toronto Argonauts.

He has been the Deputy Director at the Russian Center for APEC Studies since 2010.

He has been the Deputy Prime Minister of Papua New Guinea since August 2017.

He has been the Deputy Prime Minister of Swaziland since 2013, succeeding Themba Nhlanganiso Masuku in his position.

He has been the Deputy Speaker of the Assembly since 2006.

He has been the director and chief choreographer of Hamburg Ballet since 1973.

He has been the Director at Eyebeam Art + Technology Center since July, 2015.

He has been the Director of Chatham House (the Royal Institute of International Affairs) since January 2007.

He has been the Director of Golf at The Summit G&CC since 2013.

He has been the director of TG1 (from 1981 to 1982), a news program on Raiuno, Studio Aperto (from 1991 to 1993), a news program on Italia 1, and TG4 (from 1993 to 2012), a news program on Rete 4.

He has been the Director of the Cambridge Theater Company and the Bristol Old Vic in England, and of the Nimrod Theater in Sydney, Australia.

He has been the director of the Center for Alcohol Policy Research at Turning Point Alcohol and Drug Center in Fitzroy, Victoria, Australia, as well as the Professor of Alcohol Policy Research at the School of Population Health of the University of Melbourne, since March 2006.
