He has received the Hugo, Locus, Campbell and Nebula Awards.

He has received the IEEE Region 3 Outstanding Engineer Award, the 1980 IEEE Emanuel R. Piore Award, the Distinguished Professor Award at the Georgia Institute of Technology, the 1992 IEEE Education Medal and the 2010 IEEE Jack S. Kilby Signal Processing Medal.

He has received the James Tait Black Memorial Prize for his memoir Experience and has been listed for the Booker Prize twice to date (shortlisted in 1991 for Time's Arrow and longlisted in 2003 for Yellow Dog).

He has received the Jamnalal Bajaj Award in 2001, the Federation of Indian Chamber of Commerce and Industry (FICCI) award in 2002 and an Energy Globe Award in the AIR category in 2004.

He has received the Lifetime Achievement in Textual Studies award from the National Foundation For Jewish Culture.

He has received the London Mathematical Society Junior Whitehead Prize and the European Mathematical Society Young Mathematicians Prize.

He has received the Max Planck Research Award in biosciences (1992; together with Ernst-Detlef Schulze) and been given the Eminent Ecologist Award for 1996 by Ecological Society of America.

He has received the National Award in the screen writing for the feature Show.

He has received the National Cartoonist Society Magazine Gag Cartoon Award for 2001 and was nominated for their Gag Cartoon Award and Magazine Gag Cartoon Award for 2002.

He has received the National ICT Award for the year 2017, for his special contribution in ICT journalism.

He has received the Oliver E. Buckley Prize in 1979, the Julius Edgar Lilienfeld Prize in 1994, the National Medal of Science in 2001, and the Dickson Prize in Science in 2011.

He has received the Order of Viesturs for his work during the 2006 Riga Summit.

He has received the Outstanding Statistical Application award from the American Statistical Association three times.

He has received the Palanca Awards for his Ilokano short stories.

He has received the Palanca Awards for his poetry collections and futuristic fiction.

He has received the Paul D. Converse Award, two Fulbright Fellowships, Society of Marketing Advances Distinguished Marketing Scholar Award, and honorary professorships on four continents.

He has received the Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers and been selected by MIT Technology Review to the TR35 list of the world's top innovators under 35.

He has received the Presidential Early Career Award in Science and Engineering from the Office of Science and Technology Policy at the White House, multiple research awards from the National Science Foundation, and the E. O. Wilson Naturalist Award from the American Society of Naturalists.

He has received the Presidential Rank Award, the highest award that can be given to a civilian federal employee.

He has received the Prix Journal de Montreal, Prix du Cercle des critiques de l'Outaouais, the Dora Mavor Moore Award for Outstanding New Play, the Floyd S. Chalmers Canadian Play Award, and nine Jessie Richardson Theater Awards for the Vancouver productions of Lilies and The Orphan Muses.

He has received the Push for Excellence Award from Rainbow/PUSH for his work on racial disproportionality in school suspensions.

He has received the S.

H. Hutner Award for Research Contributions to Protozoology (2004).

He has received the Tensing Norgay National Award for outstanding achievement from the President of India in 2004.

He has received the Tony Award for Best Play for Love!

He has received the United Nations Environment Program Global 500 Award in 1989, the Sitara-i-Imtiaz in 1990, the Ramon Magsaysay Award in 1992, the WWF Duke of Edinburgh Conservation Award in 1994, "Man of the Year" Rotary International (Pakistan) Gold Medal in 2005, Sitara-e-Eisaar and Hilal-i-Imtiaz by the President of Pakistan in 2007.

He has received thirteen National Film Awards.

He has received three Best Picture nominations for producing Pulp Fiction, Good Will Hunting and Inglourious Basterds.

He has received three Emmy nominations, winning one in 2017, for Outstanding Costumes for a Variety, Nonfiction, or Reality Programming due to his work on RuPaul's Drag Race.

He has received three Filmfare Awards, two Screen Awards and two Zee Cine Awards.
