He left Montgomery to coach at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County, where he stayed from 1981 to 1990.

He left Morocco for Montreal in the 1960s, where he led a synagogue.

He left Mossley in October 1986, joining Horwich RMI.

Away from football, Gay became a civil servant working for the Employment Department.

He left most of his estate to the Gallery.

He left Motherwell in April 2002 when they went into administration.

He left Moundsville, West Virginia in 1977 for Somerset, Kentucky to be close to family that had already left West Virginia.

He left MPACUK in September 2015 citing frustration at the lack of Muslim activism and support in the UK.
