Henning Webb Prentis Jr. (July 11, 1884 - October 29, 1959) was an American industrialist, known as president of the Armstrong Cork Company, president of the National Association of Manufacturers, and recipient of the Henry Laurence Gantt Medal in 1956.

Henning won 10 tournaments on the South African Sunshine Tour between 1963 and 1980.

Henning won the Sunshine Tour Order of Merit in two consecutive seasons, in 1974/75 and 1975/76.
