Henry married Joan, daughter of Adam de Basing, a previous mayor of London.

Henry married Matilda, daughter of Hamelin de Warenne, Earl of Surrey, and Isabel de Warenne, 4th Countess of Surrey.

Henry Marriott was Archdeacon of Bermuda from 1925 until 1951.

Henry Marshall Elmore was a Colonel for the Confederate States Army and he commanded the 20th Texas Infantry during the American Civil War.

Henry Marshall Furman was the first Presiding Judge of the Oklahoma Criminal Court of Appeals, now the Oklahoma Court of Criminal Appeals, and served as Presiding Judge from 1909 to 1916.

Henry Marshall Wallace (born September 26, 1938) is a former American football player who played with the Los Angeles Chargers.

Henry Marsh was a rugby union international who represented England in 1873.
