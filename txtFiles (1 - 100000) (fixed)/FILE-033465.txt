He painted architectural landscapes and quadratura and is recorded as having painted scenery for productions of operas in Venice.

He painted a Repose in Egypt and a Christ and Samaritan Woman for the church of San Giovanni Battista; and painted a Virgin Mary, St. Anne, and St. Joachim for the church of San Clemente.

He painted a saint at the gallery of Cesena.

He painted a Saints Augustine an Monica, and the Madonna of the Belt (1765) for the church of Sant'Agostino, Rome.

He painted a Sant'Agostino at his desk, now in the Pinacoteca of Rimini.

He painted a Sant'Andrea Avellino with the Blessed Marinonio for the church of San Vincenzo in Modena.

He painted a Scene from the Life of Santa Lucia for the church of San Martino in Anatoglia.

He painted a series of lawyers for the Ducal Palace, Venice.

He painted a Sermon of St John the Baptist for the church of San Giovanni Battista belonging to the Carmelite Teresiani at Rimini.

He painted a signed altarpiece of the Madonna and Saints in a Verona Gallery.

He painted a somewhat unusual Annunciation with a hovering ghostly angel dominating the scene.

He painted a Souls in Purgatory in the church of All Saints, at Seville in 1659.

He painted a ' St. Dominic' and ' St. Thomas Aquinas ' for the church of the Dominicans at Turin.

He painted a St Louis Gonzaga for the church of Santa Maria Maddalena de' Pazzi in Florence.

He painted a Stoning of St. Stephen for the church of San Stefano in that city; and for Santa Maria del Suffragio he painted a Holy Family.
