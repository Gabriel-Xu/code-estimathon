He played Tom Lucas, the brothers' dad, on the Disney show Jonas in season one.

He played Tony Schneider on the US MTV television teen drama Skins.

He played top-flight football for several Italian clubs in Serie A, Everton of the Premier League, and for the Montreal Impact in Major League Soccer.

He played tough drugs baron, Paul Haskew in ITV drama series The Bill.

He played trombone in an Army band, and eventually graduated from Juilliard in 1958.

He played trombone on many Frank Zappa records, as well as with Captain Beefheart and in the Fowler Brothers Band.
