Her 2013 book Hitler's Furies: German Women in the Nazi Killing Fields was translated into 21 languages and was a finalist for the 2013 National Book Award in the nonfiction category and for the National Jewish Book Award.

Her 2013 book The Bling Ring: How A Gang of Fame-Obsessed Teens Ripped Off Hollywood and Shocked the World recounts the true story behind the Sofia Coppola film The Bling Ring, which was based on a 2010 Vanity Fair piece by Sales, "The Suspects Wore Louboutins".

Her 2013 book, True Refuge: Finding Peace and Freedom in Your Own Awakened Heart, offers practices for tapping into inner peace and wisdom in the midst of difficulty.

Her 2013 collaboration with Allen & Envy, called "Always", brought her further acclaim and it received support from many DJs, including Armin van Buuren.

Her 2013 debut novel Ancillary Justice won the 2014 Hugo Award for Best Novel as well as the Nebula Award, the Arthur C. Clarke Award, and the BSFA Award.

Her 2013 exhibition in Dubai, Popcornographic, explored taboos in Middle Eastern society.

Her 2013 memoir, Wave, recounts her experiences in the tsunami and the progression of her grief in the ensuing years.

Her 2013 New Yorker article "Bones of Contention" explored the black-market fossil trade.

Her 2013 TEDMED talk addresses the many issues facing science, technology and medicine (STM) publishers.
