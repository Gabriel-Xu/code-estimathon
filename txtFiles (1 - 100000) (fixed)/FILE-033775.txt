Her books, Apenas Marta (2011) and Los Perros (2013), have been critically well-received and featured at the International Book Fair in Monterrey, Guadalajara and at the Instituto de Cervantes in New York.

Her books are also available as ebooks.

Her books are Ballet Center Work, threebythree, Ballet Barre Enchainements, and Ballet Barre and Center Combinations, the last published in either description or labanotation format.

Her books are co-authored with Gavan Naden.

Her books are currently published under Avon Books.

Her books are described as "beautiful, electrifying love stories that can make even the most pessimistic person believe in happily ever afters."

Her books are evenly divided between the series The Keltiad and The Rock&Roll Murders: The Rennie Stride Mysteries.

Her books are frequently best sellers and the UK Public Lending Rights figures often list her in the top three borrowed authors.

Her books are Jeddah Diary (2012) and Stranger (2015).

Her books are known for calling attention to the plight of Japanese Americans throughout World War II.

Her books are known for their unusual subject matter, including alcoholism, death, and domestic abuse.
