Her most significant roles are that of Joy Foy in the short lived 1992 sitcom Julie, where she starred together with Julie Andrews and James Farentino, and the main character Trudy in the 1993 independent film Losers in Love.

Her most significant victories included the open singles titles of the USA (1996), Singapore (1997), Japan (1997, 2004), Indonesia (1998), Korea (2003), the Netherlands (2001, 2002), Switzerland (2002), and Taiwan (2000, 2003).

Her most successful album, 1992's Bohemia, was an international hit, although her other albums have been successful mainly in Canada.

Her most successful and popular song is Made in India released in 1995.

Her most successful book is A Mango-Shaped Space which won the American Library Association (ALA) Schneider Family Book Award for Middle School in 2004.

Mass's novel Jeremy Fink and the Meaning of Life was adapted into a feature film in 2011.

Her most successful film to date is Finding Neverland; a J. M. Barrie biographical film made in 2004.

Her most successful foal to race was Next Move, the 1950 American Champion Three-Year-Old Filly and the 1952 American Co-Champion Older Female Horse.

Her most successful horse to date has been Mon Mome, winner of the 2009 Grand National.

Her most successful partnership was with Sven Authorsen, with whom she skated from 1992 until 1995.
