Her performance collaboration with Jim Fletcher, Mortal Kombat, was presented at Le Mouvement in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, in August 2014, and was again presented at the Whitney Museum of American Art in October 2014.

Her performance credits include appearances with The Bach Choir of Bethlehem, the Dallas Opera, the Lyric Opera of Chicago, the Metropolitan Opera, the NBC Opera Theater, the New Orleans Opera, the San Diego Opera, and the Santa Fe Opera among others.

Her performance earned her several awards and nominations, including the Best Female Debut at the 59th Filmfare Awards.

Her performance gained positive appreciations for its unexpected simplicity and naturalness.

Her performance has been video-documented by the Indira Gandhi National Center for the Arts (IGNCA) while her life story is the main feature of a journal, Madras Season: Its Genesis, published by Sruti, a magazine dedicated for performing arts.

Her performance history began at the Neo-Burlesque festival, Tease-o-rama, in 2002.

Her performance in Aashiq Awara (1993) won her the 1994 Filmfare Award for Lux New Face of the Year.

Her performance in Beauty and the Beast won her the 1989 AVN Award for Best Supporting Actress, Video and the 1989 XRCO Award for Best Supporting Actress.

Her performance in Dil Se.. also earned her a Best Supporting Actress nomination at the same ceremony, while Soldier won her three more Best Debut awards at other major ceremonies.

Her performance in drama serials Muhabbat Subha Ka Sitara Hai (2014) and Diyar-e-Dil (2015) earned her a wide spread acclaim.
