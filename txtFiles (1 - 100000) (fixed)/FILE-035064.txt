He was selected in the French squad for the 2003 Rugby World Cup but had to leave the team before the beginning of the competition due to an injury.

He was selected in their squad for UEFA Euro 2012 and UEFA Euro 2016.

He was selected in the Jersey squad for the 2015 ICC World Twenty20 Qualifier tournament and the 2016 ICC World Cricket League Division Four matches held in Los Angeles.

He was selected in the ninth round of the 1971 NHL Amateur Draft, 113th overall, by the Minnesota North Stars.

He was selected in the ninth round of the 1992 NFL Draft by the San Diego Chargers, for whom he played three seasons.
