He was the Minister of State for Overseas Indian Affairs, a position he resigned from after an official commission of inquiry (Nanavati Commission) noted the 'balance of probability' indicated he was accused of inciting and leading murderous mobs against the Sikh community in Delhi after Sikh bodyguards assassinated Indian Prime Minister Indira Gandhi during the 1984 anti-Sikh Genocide, a charge he denies.

He was the Minister of State for Police and Criminal Justice until 14 July 2014.
