He won four straight elections in 1980, 1985, 1990 and 1995.

He won four successive county senior championship medals with the club between 1891 and 1894.

He won four Super Bowls as a member of the famed Steel Curtain defense.

He won fourteen caps for Ireland and was selected to captain the British Isles 1910 tour of South Africa, and played in two of the test games.

He won fourteen graded stakes races, including eight Grade I stakes: the Santa Anita Handicap in 2011, 2013, and 2014 the only horse to have won that race three times; he also won the Goodwood Stakes twice (2011, 2012), the 2013 Pacific Classic Stakes, and the Hollywood Gold Cup twice (2012, 2013).

He won fourteen races and was placed second or third on thirty occasions.

He won fourteen Singles, two Doubles and three Mixed Doubles National titles and finished as runner up in numerous other National finals.
