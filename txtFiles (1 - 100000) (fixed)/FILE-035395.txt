Highland and Island Emigration Society was a charitable society formed to promote and assist emigration as a solution to the Highland Potato Famine.

Highland and James streets, laid out in the nineteenth century, run parallel and form a southwest-northeast axis for the district.

Highland Arts exhibited the works of the late C. John Taylor, a poet, artist, and composer; it closed in November 2017.

The local slate contains pyrites or "fool's gold".

Highland Avenue and Chester Transportation Center stations nearby are still served by SEPTA.

Highland Avenue begins at Central Park Place NE in the Old Fourth Ward and proceeds eastward past the Atlanta Medical Center and crossing Freedom Parkway a first time.
