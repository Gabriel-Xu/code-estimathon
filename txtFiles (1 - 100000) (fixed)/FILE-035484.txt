His 2016 book, The Butcher's Trail, was reviewed by The Independent, The National, and The Wall Street Journal.

His 2016 Centers was the first release on FatCat's relaunched 130701 record label, which specializes in post-classical music and popularized the music of Max Richter and Hauschka.

His 2016 collaboration with Rae Sremmurd, titled "Black Beatles", provided Gucci Mane with his first number-one single on the US Billboard Hot 100 chart.

His 2016 film Fire at Sea, a documentary focused on European migrant crisis on the Sicilian island of Lampedusa, won the Golden Bear at the 66th Berlin International Film Festival.

His 2016 film Letters from War was shown at the 66th Berlin International Film Festival.

His 2016 film United States of Love was shown at the 66th Berlin International Film Festival where he won the Silver Bear for Best Script.

His 2016 novel Praise for the Women of the Family was nominated for the Arabic Booker Prize.

His 2016 reappearance marked the first time in 27 years that he, Alf and his half-sister Roo Stewart (Georgie Parker) had been on-screen together.

His 2016 season was badly disrupted by injury and he ran only twice, failing to complete his first start and being beaten on his eventual reappearance.

His 2016 single, "B.

E.

D.", was on the Billboard charts for 7 weeks, peaking at number 69.

His 2016 war crimes convictions were overturned following an appeal on 8 June 2018; an appeal against his conviction for witness tampering is pending.

His 2017/2018 season has yet to kickstart because he has only played one game this whole season, with Mamelodi Sundowns coach Pitso Mosimane saying "He needs to prove himself to get into the starting eleven".
