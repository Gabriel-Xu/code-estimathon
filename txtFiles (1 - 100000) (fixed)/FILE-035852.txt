His robotic and bio-art installations seek to merge the organic and electromechanical seamlessly through sonification, interactivity and motion, expressing a gentle symbiosis.

His rock/ambient instrumental albums are Xmas Vol.

His rock cut tomb at Qubbet el-Hawa consists of one bigger main cult chapel and several smaller rooms.

His "rock 'n' roll comedy" (as he himself calls it) is mostly a mix of blue comedy and the production of sound effects, similar to Dutch-Moroccan comedian Najib Amhali.

His rods fetch high prices from collectors.

His role as Abhiram Shaba in the Manipuri show, Goura Lilas is reported to have earned him the nickname.

His role as a cinematographer with directors such as Julie Dash and Spike Lee has been notable, with his work on Daughters of the Dust (1991) winning the Cinematography Award at Sundance.

His role as a key player in the Liverpool Avante Garde art scene has been noted in the Tate Liverpool's 2007 exhibition Liverpool - Creative Center of the Universe.

His role as an advisor to Diageo ended in September 2014.

His role as an apprentice involved football training, but also maintenance of Easter Road, such as sweeping and painting.
