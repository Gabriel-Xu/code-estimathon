His singles and albums have charted in Flanders, Austria, Switzerland and Germany; however, Smit is the most successful in his own country the Netherlands.

His singles began attracting popularity in 2017, when his style was described as "the spitfire suburban poetry of younger Arctic Monkeys and Jamie T, fused with a healthy touch of ska and hip-hop spirit".

His singles "Deep Down Low" and "Make Some Noise" peaked at number one and fifty respectively on the "Billboard Twitter Emerging Artists" chart.

His singles discography comprises 58 singles and 34 music videos.

His single-seater racing career was interrupted several times and only took off in 1989 when he won the Benelux Formula Opel Championship in 1989.

His singles "Follow Me" and "Drift Away" were top 10 hits on the Billboard Hot 100.

His single, "Show No Mercy" (1990) was a top ten hit in both countries.

His singles include "Deliverance", "Ugly", and "Ms. New Booty", the latter of which peaked at No. 7 on the US Billboard Hot 100.

His singles included the 2003 hit "Jam", followed by "In Your Mind" and "The Bomb".

His singles include five Number Ones on the Hot Country Songs charts: "Home" (his debut single), as well as "If the Devil Danced (In Empty Pockets)," "Third Rock from the Sun," "Pickup Man" and "Bigger Than the Beatles."

His single "Solarstorms" was released worldwide on CTK Records/Corporate Ogre Records on 23 August 2012, accompanied by his US Network TV debut on "Jimmy Kimmel Live!"

His singles record was 34-67 and his doubles record was 23-55.

His singles "Release Me" and "The Last Waltz" both topped the UK music charts in 1967, and sold more than a million copies each.

His single "Standing Tall" sold 5 times platinum, making it the most sold single of all time in Norway.
