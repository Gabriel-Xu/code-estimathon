In 1969 she won the singles gold medal and the triples bronze medal at the 1969 World Outdoor Bowls Championship in Sydney, Australia.

In 1969, she won the title of Miss Utah.

In 1969 she wrote Hancock, a biography of her then recently deceased estranged husband, Tony Hancock, with the journalist David Nathan.

In 1969, she wrote the first modern book of Herbalism, Herbs & Things.
