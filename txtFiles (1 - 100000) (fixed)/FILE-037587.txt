In 1996 he won the USPRO National Road Race Championships, and a stage on the Tour of China.

In 1996, he worked for former astronaut Jon McBride, who ran unsuccessfully for governor of West Virginia, and he later played a key role in George W. Bush's 2000 victory in that state.

In 1996, he worked for the Bronx Torts division of the Corporation Counsel and in 1998, he joined the New York State Senate as counsel and deputy chief of staff to then senior State Senator Guy J. Velella.

In 1996 he worked for the pharmaceutical giant Pfizer, his work with the company included the design of a learning game that was later distributed to the sales representatives of the company for the launch of Zyrtec.

In 1996, he worked with Bill Irwin on the musical Hip Hop Wonderland.

In 1996 he wrote an acknowledged classic in the literature on Fanon entitled Fanon's Dialectic of Experience.

In 1996, he wrote a pilot episode for a new series of Poldark, The Stranger from the Sea, which became a controversial adaptation with fans, using a new cast featuring John Bowe as Ross Poldark and Mel Martin as Demelza.

In 1996 Hidding was elected to the Tasmanian House of Assembly for the multi-member seat of Lyons.

In 1996, his composition, Memento mori - An AIDS Requiem, was premiered in Atlanta, Georgia, and was released on CD in 2001.
