In 2012, he became head coach, and was a Coach of the Year finalist in the European Baseball Coaches Association voting.

In 2012 he became interested in the ergonomics of keyboards, having designed and built himself several designs.

In 2012, he became Irish National Criterium Champion.

In 2012 he became managing director of the Fridtjof Nansen Institute.

In 2012 he became one of the inaugural fellows of the American Mathematical Society.

In 2012 he became one of Vans's "Vans Custom Culture Ambassadors" with Cisco Adler and The Hold Steady.

In 2012 he became Paralympic champion in the +100kg by beating Song Wang (China) in the final.

In 2012, he became president of jazz music label Blue Note Records.

In 2012, he became President of the Campaign to Protect Rural England, taking over from Bill Bryson.

In 2012, he became the coach of the Xenics Storm League of Legends pro gaming team.

In 2012, he became the executive director of cultural engagement at the Hendricks Center at DTS.

In 2012, he became the first American football coach in history (high school, college or professional) to win 600 career games.

In 2012 he became the first American pistol shooter to qualify for Paralympics; at the 2012 London Games he placed 14th in the 10 m and 23rd in the 50 m events.

In 2012, he became the first artist to have a different album in the British charts every year for 25 consecutive years.

In 2012, he became the first former footballer to train a Melbourne Cup-winning horse.

In 2012 he became the first Olympic medalist in wrestling for Puerto Rico by winning a silver medal in the 84 kg division.

In 2012, he became the first player in the history of the European Tour to win his first four European Tour titles in the same year.

In 2012 he became the first Victorian born and bred player to play for the Melbourne Storm at NRL level.
