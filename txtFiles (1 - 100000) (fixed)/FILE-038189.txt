In 2013, the album Presente by Bajofondo became the first instrumental album to be nominated for Album of the Year.

In 2013, the album's remastered deluxe edition was released to similar acclaim.

In 2013, the album was certified gold by the British Phonographic Industry.

In 2013, the album was listed at number 10 in Ballast's list of top 50 Canadian albums of all time.

In 2013 the album was nominated for a Gardel Award.

In 2013, the album was reissued across a double LP by Asbestos Records.

In 2013, The album was reissued again, this time with two CDs in celebration of the album's 30th anniversary.

In 2013, the album was reissued as part of a CD series spanning They Might Be Giants' four Elektra releases.

In 2013, the album was re-issued through De Stijl and included "The Fate of the Human Carbine"/"Catapult" 7-inch single with the vinyl edition.

In 2013, the album was released on CD in the United States, through distribution from Alliance Entertainment.

In 2013, the album was released on vinyl for the first time.

In 2013 the album was remastered and 2 bonus tracks were added.

In 2013 the album was re-released as a double CD set under the title Electric Peace, with one disk featuring the originally released album and the second containing the entire Peace album recorded during the Manor Sessions.

In 2013, the album was re-released for Record Store Day by Polydor (in Europe) and Rhino (in the US) as a limited edition of 3,500 copies (numbered on the sleeve), pressed on marbled blue vinyl and featuring none of the 2006 bonus material.

In 2013, the ALEKS Corporation was acquired by McGraw-Hill Education.
