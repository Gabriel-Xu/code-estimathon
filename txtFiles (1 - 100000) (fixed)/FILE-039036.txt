Indian Head Park is a village in Cook County, Illinois, United States, slightly north of the intersection of Interstate 294 and Interstate 55.

Indianhead Technical College (WITC) is a two-year technical college with campuses in Ashland, New Richmond, Rice Lake and Superior, in the U.

S. state of Wisconsin.

Indian Health Service (IHS) is an operating division (OPDIV) within the U.

S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS).
