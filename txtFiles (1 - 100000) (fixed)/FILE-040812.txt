In this chapter he reproves the people and priests for their sins in the interregnum which followed Jeroboam's death; hence there is no mention of the king or his family; and in Hosea 4:2 bloodshed and other evils usual in a civil war are specified.

In this chapter, Jesus accuses the Pharisees of hypocrisy and perjury.

In this chapter, Jesus goes to Nazareth and faces the rejection of his own family.

In this chapter, Judah's King Hezekiah is approaching death, but his life is extended in answer to his prayers.
