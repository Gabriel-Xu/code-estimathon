It is the first album to feature bassist Joel Graham.

It is the first album to feature bassist Jorgen Carlsson, who joined the band in 2008, replacing Andy Hess.

It is the first album to feature bassist Kyle Zeigler.

It is the first album to feature bassist Seb Mueller and guitarist Max Becker, who previously played bass.

It is the first album to feature, bassist TJ Alford, pianist Elizabeth Kimbrough, drummer Steve Robinson, and rhythm guitarist Adam Wright.

It is the first album to feature bassist Yenz Leonhardt.

It is the first album to feature bassist Zakk Sandler, last album to feature drummer Ryan Seaman, and only album to feature lead guitarist Christian Thompson.

It is the first album to feature both Andrew Giddings on keyboards and Jonathan Noyce on bass, who would remain with the band until 2007, resulting in Jethro Tull's longest ever unchanged line-up.

It is the first album to feature Caleb Shomo on lead vocals, replacing former lead vocalist Nick Barham who had replaced original lead vocalist Austin Carlile but had not recorded an album with the band.

It is the first album to feature Casey Lewis on drums, replacing Graham Churchill due to conflicting commitments outside the band.

It is the first album to feature Cody Smith and Jon Dunn.

It is the first album to feature David Wallin on drums.

It is the first album to feature Dean Butterworth on drums, who joined the band in March 2005 after former drummer Chris Wilson departed in 2005.

It is the first album to feature Derek Bell on the harp.

It is the first album to feature Doriano Magliano, Hance Alligood, Brian Medley, and Andrew Paiano (formerly of Abandon All Ships), and the last album featuring Austin Thornton on drums.

It is the first album to feature drummer Bill Andrews and the only one to feature guitarist Rick Rozz.

It is the first album to feature drummer Jonathon Blackmore and bassist Andy Smith.

It is the first album to feature drummer Marco Pitruzzella.

It is the first album to feature drummer Mark Heaney and the second to feature vocalist Damian Wilson, his first album with the band being their 1993 debut, Wounded Land.

It is the first album to feature former Bury Your Dead vocalist Myke Terry as co-vocalist, and the first album to not feature founding co-vocalist Michael Burr.
