It was amalgamated with the 9th Battalion, Ulster Defense Regiment in 1984 to form the 1st/9th Battalion, Ulster Defense Regiment.

It was amalgamated with the Borgu Game Reserve in 1975 to form the Kainji Lake National Park.

It was amalgamated with the Caribbean Operational Hydrological Institute (COHI) in the mid-1980s to form the Caribbean Institute for Meteorology and Hydrology (CIMH), but the name was only officially changed in September 1999 to reflect the dual role of the Institute.

It was amalgamated with the civil parish of Woodhall Spa in 1987.

It was amalgamated with the Metropolitan Borough of Finsbury to form the London Borough of Islington in Greater London in 1965.
