Juan Richard Turner (born 19 October 1974) is a Manx politician, businessman, radio producer, radio presenter, television producer and freelance cameraman, he is also a Member of the Legislative Council of the Isle of Man, his current term expiring on 28 February 2018.

Turner was elected to the body in the eighth round of voting on 22 May 2007.

Juan Risso (born 3 September 1942) is an Argentine former footballer who competed in the 1964 Summer Olympics.

Juan Rivera Saavedra is an American singer and actor of Mexican heritage.

Juan R. Liwag Memorial High School (JRLMHS) is a general public type high school located in Gapan City, Nueva Ecija, The Philippines.

Juan R. Liwag was a Filipino lawyer and politician who served as senator in the 5th and 6th Congress of the Philippines.

Juan R. Luciano (born c. 1962) is a US-based Argentine businessman.
