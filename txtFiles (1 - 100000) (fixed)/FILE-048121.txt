Julien Denormandie (born 14 August 1980) is a French politician, a Secretary of State, attached to the Minister of Territorial Cohesion in the Second Philippe Government.

Julien de Sousa Bardy (born Clermont-Ferrand, 3 April 1986) is a French-born Portuguese rugby union player.

Julien Desrosiers (born October 14, 1980) is a French ice hockey player who represented France at the 2011 IIHF World Championship.

Julien De Wilde (b. Wetteren, 7 January 1944) is a Belgian businessman.
