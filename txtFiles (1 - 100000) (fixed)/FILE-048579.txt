Kill the Man is a 1999 American comedy film starring Luke Wilson and Joshua Malina as the owners of an independent photocopier shop who are in competition with a large chain of copy-shops.

Kill the Messenger is a 2014 American biographical crime thriller film directed by Michael Cuesta and written by Peter Landesman.

Kill the Messenger is a television special that premiered on HBO on September 27, 2008 starring comedian Chris Rock.

Kill the Messenger was adapted into a 2014 film by the same name.
