Kuppadi is a village near Sulthan bathery in Wayanad district in the state of Kerala, India.

Kuppadithara is a village near Padinharethara in Wayanad district in the state of Kerala, India.

Kuppam is a census town in Chittoor district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh located 100 kilometers South-East of Bengaluru and 250 kilometers West of Chennai .

Kuppam is an Assembly legislative constituency of Andhra Pradesh.
