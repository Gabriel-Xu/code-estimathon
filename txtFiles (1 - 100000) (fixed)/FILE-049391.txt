Lego Marvel Super Heroes 2 is a Lego-themed action-adventure video game developed by Traveler's Tales and published by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment for Microsoft Windows, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch and Xbox One on 14 November 2017, and by Feral Interactive for macOS on 2 August 2018.

Lego Marvel Super Heroes 2's storyline centers around superheroes from different eras and realities of the Marvel Universe as they fight against the time-traveling villain Kang the Conqueror in a battle across space and time.

Lego Marvel Super Heroes is currently the bestselling Lego video game of all time.
