Lubu is a Malayan language spoken by the Lubu people on the island of Sumatra in Indonesia.

Lubu is unattested, only recalled as the language of the grandparents of the village elders.

Lubuk China is a small town in Alor Gajah District, Melaka, Malaysia.

Lubuk Paku is a small town in Maran District, Pahang, Malaysia.

Lubukraya is an andesitic stratovolcano on Sumatra island, Indonesia.

Lubuli is a town in southeastern Swaziland.

Lubumbashi International Airport (IATA: FBM, ICAO: FZQA) is an airport serving Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo.
