Mad Men is an American television drama series created by Matthew Weiner, produced by Lionsgate Television and broadcast in the United States and Canada on the cable network AMC.

Mad Men set a new record for the largest "shutout" in Emmy history, receiving nominations for 17 awards and winning none.

Mad Men won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series, Golden Globe Award for Best Drama Series, and acknowledgement by the American Film Institute for the third year in a row.

Mad Mex Fresh Mexican Grill is an Australian chain of fast-food restaurants based in Sydney, Australia that purveys Mexican cuisine, while the Mad Group own the master franchise for the restaurant in New Zealand.

Mad Michelle (real name Ann Marie McKoy) is a Jamaican dancer who was crowned Dancehall Queen in 2003.

Mad Mimi is an email marketing service enabling users to create, send and track email campaigns without using templates.

Mad Mission 3, with Cantonese lyrics by Lin Zhenqiang and the song was one of the 1986 RTHK Top 10 Gold Songs.
