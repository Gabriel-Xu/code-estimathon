Maharao is a variation on the Indian (mainly Hindu) royal title Maharaja, also meaning 'Great king' in Hindi.

Maharao Sahib Shri Brijraj Singhi Ji of kota presents a gold medal every year to the all rounder and the best student of school on the occasion of annual function.

Maharao Shardul Singh Shekhawat was born in the year 1681 and was the ruler of Jhunjhunu (in present-day Rajasthan, India).

Maharasan is a 1993 Tamil comedy film produced, directed and co-written by G. N. Rangarajan.
