Margary's cataloging system has been criticized as being essentially arbitrary in several respects.

Margary's hierarchy of routes is not necessarily that of the original designers or users of the network.

Margary's network also largely consists of roads built by the Romans, not necessarily roads used by the Romans, who may have continued to use native British trackways.

Margary's numbering system follows similar conventions to modern road numbering systems.

Margary's system is nonetheless widely used for its practicality, and the awarding of a Margary number to a route came to be considered a hallmark of authenticity among researchers in the field.
