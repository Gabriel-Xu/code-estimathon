Milton's record includes service as Senior Minister of First Presbyterian Church of Chattanooga, Tennessee and his election as President of the Charlotte campus of RTS (2007) and then Chancellor and Chief Executive Officer of Reformed Theological Seminary (RTS) (2010).

Milton's Secret is a 2016 Canadian family-drama film directed by Barnet Bain, based on the novel of the same name by the author Eckhart Tolle, which was released in 2008.

Milton State School is a heritage-listed state school at Bayswater Street, Milton, City of Brisbane, Queensland, Australia.
