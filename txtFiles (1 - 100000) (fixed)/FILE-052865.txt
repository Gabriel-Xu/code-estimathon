Nathan Paul Broadhead (born 5 April 1998) is a Welsh professional footballer who plays as a forward for English club Everton.

Nathan Paul Harris (born 8 March 1992) is a New Zealand rugby union player who currently plays as a hooker for Bay of Plenty in the ITM Cup and the Chiefs in Super Rugby.

Nathan Paul Myhrvold (born August 3, 1959), formerly Chief Technology Officer at Microsoft, is co-founder of Intellectual Ventures and the principal author of Modernist Cuisine and its successor books.

Nathan Paulse (born 7 April 1982 in Cape Town, Western Cape) is a South African retired professional footballer who played as a striker for Ajax Cape Town and had 1 cap for South Africa.

Nathan Paulse is a well recognized public figure sharing his voice and advocacy of player development, education and brand development.

Nathan Peats (born 5 October 1990) is an Australian professional rugby league footballer who plays for the Gold Coast Titans in the National Rugby League.
