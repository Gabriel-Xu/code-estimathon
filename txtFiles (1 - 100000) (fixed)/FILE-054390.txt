One of its aims is to provide more non-English symbols, such as clicks, than earlier ASCII-mappings do (Kirshenbaum, X-SAMPA).

One of its aims is to use images of popular (as opposed to elitist) culture in art, emphasizing the banal or kitschy elements of any culture, most often through the use of irony.

One of its aims was to prevent obtention of citizenship through "marriages of convenience", by specifying that the spouse of a Samoan citizen must reside in Samoa with his or her spouse for at least five years before being granted citizenship.

One of its amendments, the 1689 Claim of Right, was recently referenced by the Scottish Assembly in debates over Scotland and Brexit.

One of its applications is ligating cDNA into a plasmid or other vectors instead of using Terminal deoxynucleotide Transferase enzyme to add poly A to the cDNA fragment.

One of its applications was the sequencing of the human genome.

One of its archbishops was the saint Torribio Mogrovejo.

One of its artists is Charles Hayward (musician) formerly of This Heat - he is a musician in the Entelechy project Ambient Jam.
