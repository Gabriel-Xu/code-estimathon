Oscar Smith (born April 5, 1963 in Tampa, Florida) is a former professional American football running back in the National Football League for the Detroit Lions.

Oscar Soetewey (born 13 January 1925) was a Belgian middle-distance runner.

Oscar Solomon Straus was Secretary of Commerce and Labor under President Theodore Roosevelt from 1906 to 1909.

Oscar Soria (born 1974) is an Argentinian political activist, social journalist, and environmental and human rights campaigner, currently serving as senior campaigner in the international activist group Avaaz.

Oscar Sorto (born August 13, 1994) is an American-Salvadoran soccer player.
