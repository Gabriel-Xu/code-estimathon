Pattanapravesham (English: Town Entry) is a 1988 Malayalam screwball comedy film directed by Sathyan Anthikkad and written by Sreenivasan.

Pattanathil Sundaran is a 2003 Malayalam film directed by Vipin Mohan, and starring Dileep and Navya Nair.

Pattanayak was awarded Padma Shri in 1987.

Pattanayak was elected in 1961, 1966 and 1978.

Pattanchery is a village in the Palakkad district, state of Kerala, India.

Pattani Airport (IATA: PAN, ICAO: VTSK) is an airport serving Pattani, a town in the Pattani Province of Thailand.
