Perfect Sting (foaled 1996 in Kentucky) is an American Thoroughbred Championracehorse and broodmare.

Perfect Sting raced from age two through five during which time she won the 2000 Breeders' Cup Filly & Mare Turf for trainer Joe Orseno and was voted that year's American Champion Female Turf Horse.

Perfect Storm: Disasters That Changed The World was a Canadian Historical Documentary which was broadcast on History and Yesterday.

Perfect Storm is a 2000 American biographical disaster drama film directed by Wolfgang Petersen and based on the 1997 non-fiction book of the same name by Sebastian Junger.

Perfect Stranger is a 2005 Independent Christian film based on the novel Dinner With a Perfect Stranger by David Gregory.
