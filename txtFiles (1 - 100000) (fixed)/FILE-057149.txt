Professor Michael F. Good AO was the Director of the Queensland Institute of Medical Research, from 2000 to 2010.

Professor Michael G. Cornelius is an American scholar, specializing in early British Literature, and an award-winning author.

Professor Michael Gelfand OBE, CBE, Knight of the Order of St. Sylvester, was one of Zimbabwe's most distinguished colonial medical practitioners, who received a Papal Order of the Knighthood of St. Sylvester.

Professor Michael Goldsmith (born 1959) is a British computer scientist, senior research fellow and Lecturer at the University of Oxford, England.
