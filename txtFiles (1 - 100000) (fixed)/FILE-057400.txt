Purple Radio was an internet and British digital radio station for a gay, lesbian and gay-friendly audience.

Purple Radio was launched in 2001 and merged with Gaydar Radio in 2003.

Purple Rain is a 1984 American rock musical drama film directed by Albert Magnoli, written by Magnoli and William Blinn, and produced by Robert Cavallo, Joseph Ruffalo and Steven Fargnoli.

Purple Rain is regularly ranked among the best albums in music history and is widely regarded as Prince's magnum opus along with his 1987 double album Sign o' the Times.
