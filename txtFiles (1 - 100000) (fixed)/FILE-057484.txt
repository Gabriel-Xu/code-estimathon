Quest for the Ancients is a 1991 space science fiction role-playing video game produced by Game Designers Workshop licensee Paragon Software and distributed by MicroProse and Empire Software.

Quest for the Future is a science fiction novel by A. E. van Vogt.

Quest for the Golden Maze is a PC game published by Infogrames and released in 2001.

Quest for the Grail is an out-of-print collectible card game by Horizon Games and Stone Ring.

Quest for the Heartstone is a 1984 adventure module for the Basic Rules of the Dungeons & Dragons fantasy role-playing game.
