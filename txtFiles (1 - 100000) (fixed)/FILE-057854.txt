Records are recognized for both males and females in the following long course (50m) and short course (25m) events: freestyle: 50, 100, 200, 400, 800 and 1500; backstroke: 50, 100 and 200; breaststroke: 50, 100 and 200; butterfly: 50, 100 and 200; individual medley (I.

M.): 100 (25m only), 200 and 400; relays: 4x50 free (25m only), 4x100 free, 4x200 free, 10x25 free (25m only), 10x50 free (50m only), 4x50 medley, and 4x100 medley.
