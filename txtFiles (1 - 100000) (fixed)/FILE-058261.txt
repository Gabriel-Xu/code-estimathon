Richard Constance von Biberstein (1 December 1859 - 11 September 1931) was an American architect who designed numerous textile mills.

Richard Constant Boer (31 January 1863, Warnsveld - 20 August 1929, Amsterdam) was a Dutch linguist who specialized in Old Norse.

Richard Conte (born June 12, 1953) is a Contemporary artist and Art Professor.
