Roberto Tamassia is an Italian computer scientist, the Plastech Professor of Computer Science at Brown University, and served as the chair of the Brown Computer Science department from 2007 to 2014.

Roberto Tamburini (born 15 January 1991 in Rimini) is an Italian motorcycle racer.

Roberto Tancredi (born 30 January 1944 in Montecatini Val di Cecina) is a retired Italian professional football player who played as a goalkeeper.
