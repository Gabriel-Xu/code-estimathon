Robertson was sworn into office on December 2, 2013, for a two-year term as mayor.

Robertson was the head coach of the team between 1936 and 1943 and between 1946 and 1950.

Robertson was the lead underwriter of some of the most prominent firms of the 1990s stock boom, including Switchboard, Mapquest, E-Trade and Vericity, as well as retailer Bebe.

Robertson was the MPP between September 3, 1867 and December 27, 1867.

Robertson was then to have the first of two spells at Meadowbank Thistle, which had stints with Cowdenbeath and Raith Rovers separating them.

Robertson was the subject of controversy after a 2013 interview he did with GQ magazine, where he said that homosexual behavior was sinful.
