"Robots," as the venue was popularly known, operated quasi-legally from a nondescript storefront and basement at 25 Avenue B, between East 2nd and 3rd Streets, from 1983 until mid-1984, when the club was shut down for fire safety violations.

Robots can be used in any situation and for any purpose, but today many are used in dangerous environments (including bomb detection and deactivation), manufacturing processes, or where humans cannot survive.

Robots can take on any form but some are made to resemble humans in appearance.

Robots capable of juggling are designed and built both to increase and test understanding and theories of human movement, juggling, and robotics.
