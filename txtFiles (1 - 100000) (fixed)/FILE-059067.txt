Sandler and Young were a singing team from the 1960s through the 1980s, composed of Belgian-born Tony Sandler and native New Yorker Ralph Young.

Sandler discussed Making Love in the 1995 documentary film The Celluloid Closet.

Sandler Foundation is a spend-down foundation as the Sandlers have signed The Giving Pledge.

Sandler founded Analytical Engineering, Inc. in 1995.

Sandler has authored several books on power supply modeling and simulation, among them SMPS Simulation with Spice 3, Switched-Mode Power Supply Simulation with SPICE, and SPICE Circuit Handbook.

Sandler is also a professor of surgery and pediatrics at George Washington University School of Medicine and Health Sciences.

Sandler is perhaps best known for her play Mustard, for which she was awarded the Dora Mavor Moore Award for Outstanding New Play in 2016.

Sandler later reworked Canteen Boy by amalgamating him with Cajun Man, another SNL character played by Sandler.

Sandler later stated that there is nothing wrong with Canteen Boy, rather he is "someone who has been in the Scouts too long".

Sandler O'Neill + Partners, L.

P., (Sandler O'Neill) is a full-service investment banking firm and broker-dealer specializing in the financial services sector.
