Shannon, MacShannon, and O'Shannon are Anglicized Irish and Scottish surnames that derive from the Gaelic word seanachaidh, which means "skilled storyteller".

Shannon Maree Fentiman is an Australian politician.

Shannon Marie McDonnell (born 28 September 1984) is a soccer midfielder.

Shannon Marie McGinnis (born August 26, 1980 in Winter Park, Florida) is an American soap opera actress.
