She has more than 500 performances as an artist and 2 as a director in 2007, 2009.

She has more than two decades of experience as an expert consultant in Internet Governance to Fortune 500 companies, foundations, and government agencies.

She has mostly acted Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada language films.

She has mostly done comedic roles, and has appeared in over 100 films.

She has mostly edited films directed by Rolf de Heer and won an Australian Film Institute Award for Best Editing for the 2006 film Ten Canoes.

She has mostly played supporting roles in television dramas such as Boys Over Flowers and Brilliant Legacy (both in 2009).

She has mostly sung songs under the direction of her father and brothers.

She has most notably modeled for Marc Jacobs.

She has most recently directed a horror parody of Re-Animator entitled Re-Agitator: Revenge of the Parody starring Aurelio Voltaire Hernandez and Alan Merrill.
