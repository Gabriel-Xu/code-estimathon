She has served as President of Division 3 (Experimental Psychology) of the American Psychological Association, as Chair of the Psychology Section of the American Association for the Advancement of Science, and as Chair of the Society of Experimental Psychologists.

She has served as president of Studio Art Centers International (SACI) since 2006.

She has served as president of the American Statistical Association, and the International Biometric Society, one of a handful of people to have led both organizations.

She has served as president of the British Psychological Society.

She has served as President of the Division for Research of the Council for Exceptional Children and as an officer or committee member for the American Educational Research Association and the American Psychological Association.

She has served as President of the Joyce Foundation, as well as Vice President for Recruitment and Public Policy at the Chicago Climate Exchange, which pioneered emissions trading and environmental markets worldwide, as well as President of CCX International.

She has served as President of the local Wolverine Bar Association, President of the National Bar Association, on the Detroit Board of Police Commissioners, and Vice President of the Detroit Branch of the NAACP.

She has served as president of the North American Millers' Association, vice president for corporate relations with the Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corporation, and assistant secretary for congressional relations at the United States Department of Agriculture.

She has served as President of the North Carolina Composers' Alliance.

She has served as President of the Realwheels Society; Ambassador for the Rick Hansen Man in Motion Foundation; a researcher and member of the advisory panel for the International Collaboration on Repair Discoveries (ICORD), a member of the Diversity Advisory Committee for Global BC and as a mentor with the YWCA.

She has served as President of the Society for Research in Child Development and of Division 7 (Developmental) of the American Psychological Association.

She has served as president of the Texas Institute of Letters.

She has served as president of two other community colleges, Northwest State Community College and Asheville-Buncombe Technical Community College.

She has served as Principal Investigator or co-Principal Investigator on over $5,000,000 worth of research grants from the National Science Foundation and the National Endowment for the Humanities.

She has served as prioress and Benedictine federation president, president of the Leadership Conference of Women Religious, and co-chair of the Global Peace Initiative of Women.

She has served as professor of English and head of the English Department at Lady Shri Ram College, Delhi University, New Delhi, and Ranchi University and Utkal University.

She has served as Program Director for the First Nations Composer Initiative in St. Paul, Minnesota.

She has served as prosecutor for the Board of Alcoholic Beverage Control of West New York from 2009 to 2012, as an attorney of the town's parking authority from 1995 to 2009, and has been the municipal prosecutor of the Borough of Ridgefield since 2011.

She has served as provost at the Cleveland Institute of Art, and was an associate dean at Rochester Institute of Technology.

She has served as Secretary in the Ministry of Law and Justice and also as Special/Additional Secretary in the Ministry of Finance.

She has served as Secretary of the Tasmanian Branch of the International Commission of Jurists, including participating in international conferences.
