She is best known overseas for her leading role in the Kim Ki-duk film Samaritan Girl, for which she won Best New Actress at the 2004 Busan Film Critics Awards.

She is best known to audiences for her role as inmate Michelle "Brumby" Tucker in the television series Prisoner.

She is best known today as a founding presenter (1999) of the Sony Gold Award-winning program Late Junction, and is internationally recognized for her wide-ranging knowledge of music including classical, world, traditional, jazz, experimental, electronica and choral music.

She is best known today as the Flying Lark, given to her by her final owners in 1957.

She is best known to English-language audiences for her roles in Rain Man, Big Top Pee-wee, and the two Hot Shots! movies.

She is best known to non-Classicists for her book, Not Out of Africa (1996), where she criticizes the Afrocentric theory that Greek civilization was "stolen" from Ancient Egypt.

She is best known to soap fans for playing the role of Bridget Reardon on Guiding Light from May 21, 1991 to June 18, 1997, in which she spawned some of the show's most-remembered storylines.

She is best known to the public because of her participation in Bigg Boss season 6.
