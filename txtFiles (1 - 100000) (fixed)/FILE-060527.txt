She portrayed Kara Souders in the Fox TV show, Red Band Society.

She portrayed Kathy Baker in the 2010 Bollywood film, My Name Is Khan.

She portrayed Kelly Pitts in the pilot episode of The Game, though the role was later recast with Brittany Daniel.

She portrayed Lacey Wilcox in the horror comedy Brain Blockers (2007), Molly in the drama Higher Ground (2011), and voiced Orro Oxslayer in the fantasy video game Guild Wars 2 (2012).

She portrayed Lois Lane in Superman Returns (2006), and had roles in 21 (2008), Straw Dogs (2011) and Still Alice (2014).
