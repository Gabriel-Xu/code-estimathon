She served as the country's Ambassador to Denmark and Lithuania from November 2006 until December 2013.

She served as the country's Minister for Youth Development during her time as a Senator.

She served as the country's Vice-President from 19 January 2001 to 1 January 2005.

She served as the credit union's assistant manager and in 1979 was appointed president and CEO.

She served as the Dean of the Law School between 2009 and 2017, and on June 30, 2017, she stepped down from her post as Morgan and Helen Chu Dean and Professor of Law at Harvard Law School.

She served as the Department of State Transition Coordinator for the 2016-17 Transition.

She served as the Deputy Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) in the George W. Bush administration from 2005 to 2009.

She served as the Deputy Chairman of Committee of Parliament on Official Language and also as the Vice-president of All India Mahila Congress and Indian Council of World Affairs.

She served as the deputy for Norris Cochran (acting), Tom Price and Don J. Wright (acting).

She served as the Deputy managing director of the National Social Security Fund (Uganda) (NSSF Uganda), from 20 March 2011, until 29 October 2017.
