Sparkles were an American garage rock band from Levelland, Texas, that were active between 1957 and 1972.

Sparkle - the national transgender charity based in the United Kingdom that is best known for organizing the annual Sparkle Weekend in Manchester, which aims to celebrate the transgender community.

Sparkle Time was a musical radio program in the United States.

Sparklies are black or white 'hard' interference dots (as opposed to the 'soft' interference patterns of terrestrial television), caused either by too weak or too strong a signal.

Sparklies do not occur on digital satellite systems; similar problems with digital signals cause MPEG artifacts.

Sparklies is a formal term, as used by British Sky Broadcasting (BSkyB) and a number of hardware makers including Amstrad and Pace, for interference on analog satellite television transmissions.

Sparklines are small enough to be embedded in text, or several sparklines may be grouped together as elements of a small multiple.

Sparkling cider is also sometimes used as a nonalcoholic version of champagne.
