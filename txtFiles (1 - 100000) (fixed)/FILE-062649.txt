Stephen Davis, born July 1950, is a British screenwriter who wrote the film Ruby (based on his stageplay Love Field), and episodes of the TV series Waking the Dead, Silent Witness and Casualty 1909.

Stephen Davis is an American music journalist and historian.

Stephen Davison Bechtel Jr. (born May 10, 1925) is an American billionaire businessman, civil engineer, and co-owner of the Bechtel Corporation, with his son Riley.
