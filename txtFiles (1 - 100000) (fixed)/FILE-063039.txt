Sukia was a vampire-themed Italian comics series by Renzo Barbieri and Fulvio Bosttoli published by Edifumetto from 1978 to 1986, for a total of 153 albums and 6 extra albums.

Sukia were an American electronic band, based in Camarillo, California, United States, that featured former child actor Ross Harris, who in the 1970s acted in shows such as CHiPS and the Little House on the Prairie.

Sukidion is a butterfly genus in the family Lycaenidae.

Sukie de la Croix (born Darryl Michael Vincent, September 16, 1951) is a writer and photographer.

Sukierae is the debut album by Tweedy, a side project formed by Jeff Tweedy and his son Spencer.
