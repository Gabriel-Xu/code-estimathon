Tanya Tucker's Live at Billy Bob's Texas is another in the series featuring various country music artists performing at Billy Bob's nightclub in Fort Worth, Texas, the club that promotes itself as "The World's Largest Honky Tonk."

Tanya Tucker, The Oak Ridge Boys, Don Williams, The Forester Sisters, John Connely, Terri Hendrix, Valerie Smith, The Tuttles with AJ Lee, the Lewis Family, Cluster Pluckers and others have recorded Pie songs.

Tanya van Graan (born 13 December 1983) is a South African actress, singer and model.

Tanya Victoria Harding (born 23 January 1972 in Brisbane, Queensland) is an Australian softball player, who has competed for Australia at four consecutive Summer Olympics, starting in 1996.

Tanya Vidal (born 1971) is an American actress, screenwriter, film director and producer of Puerto Rican descent.

Tanya Walker is a lawyer in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, practicing in the field of commercial litigation.

Tanya Warren (born 1965) is an American basketball coach who is currently the head women's basketball coach at the University of Northern Iowa.

Tanya was also a recurring supporting role in children's comedy drama series The Amazing Extraordinary Friends.

Tanya was born in Bristol, and grew up in London.
