Tellico Plains is a town in Monroe County, Tennessee, United States.

Tellico Reservoir, also known as Tellico Lake, is a reservoir in Tennessee, created by the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) in 1979 upon the completion of Tellico Dam.

Tellico stores its collection files in XML format instead of SQL databases, which makes it easy for the users to export data or visualize it.

Telligent Community is a community and collaboration software platform developed by Telligent Systems and was first released in 2004.

Telligent Community is built on the Telligent Evolution platform, with a variety of core applications running on top of it such as blogs, forums, media galleries, and wikis.
