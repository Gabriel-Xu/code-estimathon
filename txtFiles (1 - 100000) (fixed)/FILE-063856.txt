The 1937 Great Hong Kong Typhoon was an unnamed typhoon in Hong Kong on 2 September 1937.

The 1937 Hawthorn Football Club season was the club's 13th season in the Victorian Football League.

The 1937 Home Nations Championship was the thirty-third series of the rugby union Home Nations Championship.

The 1937 Hong Kong municipal election was supposed to be held on January 1937 for the one of the 2 unofficial seats in the Urban Council of Hong Kong.

The 1937 Ice Hockey World Championships were held between February 17 and February 27, 1937, in London, England.

The 1937 International Lawn Tennis Challenge was the 32nd edition of what is now known as the Davis Cup.

The 1937 Iowa Hawkeyes football team represented the University of Iowa in the 1937 Big Ten Conference football season.

The 1937 Italian Grand Prix was a "750 kg Formula" Grand Prix race held on 12 September 1937 at the Montenero Circuit in Livorno.

The 1937 Kentucky Derby was the 63rd running of the Kentucky Derby.

The 1937 Kentucky Wildcats football team represented the Kentucky Wildcats of the University of Kentucky during the 1937 college football season.

The 1937 ladies and pairs competitions took place from March 1 to 2 also in London, United Kingdom.
