The 1998 Asian PGA Tour was the fourth season of the Asian PGA Tour, the main men's professional golf tour in Asia excluding Japan.

The 1998 Atlantic 10 Men's Basketball Tournament was played from March 4 to March 7, 1998.

The 1998 ATP Tour World Championships (also known for the doubles event as the Phoenix ATP Tour World Doubles Championship for sponsorship reasons) was a tennis tournament played on indoor carpet courts.

The 1998 Auckland Warriors season was the 4th in the club's history.
