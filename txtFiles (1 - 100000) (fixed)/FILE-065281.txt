The Arab Satellite Communications Organization (often abbreviated as Arabsat) is a communications satellite operator in the Arab World, headquartered in the city of Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

The Arab satellite lists or Arab lists were Israeli Arab electoral lists formed for the purposes of electoral support of Mapai (and later the Labor Party), Mapam and the General Zionists between 1948 and the mid 1970s.

The Arabsat satellites are a series of geostationary communications satellites launched from 1985 through 2011.
