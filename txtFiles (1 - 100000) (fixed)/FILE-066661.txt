The Caribbean People's Democratic Party was a political party in Trinidad and Tobago.

The Caribbean Philosophical Association (CPA) is a philosophical organization founded in 2002 at the Center for Caribbean Thought at the University of the West Indies, in Mona, Jamaica.

The Caribbean pine, Pinus caribaea, is a hard pine, native to Central America, Cuba, the Bahamas, and the Turks and Caicos Islands.

The Caribbean Pioneer was a Bible magazine published monthly in North America by the Christadelphians.

The Caribbean Pioneer was originally published separately from The Christadelphian Tidings of the Kingdom of God (The Tidings), but merged with The Tidings in January 2005.

The Caribbean Plate is a mostly oceanic tectonic plate underlying Central America and the Caribbean Sea off the north coast of South America.

The Caribbean Policy Research Institute (CAPRI) is a public policy think tank based at the University of the West Indies, Mona, Kingston, Jamaica.

The Caribbean Premier League (abbreviated to CPL or CPLT20) is an annual Twenty20 cricket tournament held in the Caribbean.

The Caribbean Premier League chose the Brian Lara Cricket Academy as the host for the Playoff, eliminators and final matches of the 2017 edition of the league signaling the first high profile matches at the ground.

The Caribbean Professional Football League was a short-lived association football competition spanning several Caribbean nations.

The Caribbean Public Services Association is a trade union Federation in the Caribbean which links public sector unions in the region.

The Caribbean Regiment (also known as the Carib Regiment) was a unit of the British Army during World War II.
