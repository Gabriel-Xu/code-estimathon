The character of Hedwig was inspired by a German divorced U.

S. Army wife who was a Mitchell family babysitter and moonlighted as a prostitute at her Junction City, Kansas, trailer park home.

The character of Heikki Lunta is a product of the heavy Finnish-American presence in Michigan's Upper Peninsula combined with a tremendous annual snowfall.

The character of Hemlock Hoax was based on a minor character from a film story that had been rejected.

The character of Henry first appeared in 1999, and is played by actor Trent Dawson.

The character of Hershel Greene and Scott Wilson's portrayal has garnered favorable reviews from critics and fans alike.

The character of Hollis was portrayed by Canadian actor Klea Scott.
