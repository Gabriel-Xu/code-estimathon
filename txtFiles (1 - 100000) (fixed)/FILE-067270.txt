The club's male first team ceased all footballing activities in May 2010 to (unofficially) merge with KV Red Star Waasland, becoming KV Red Star Waasland-Beveren.

The club's manager is Maureen Vandrau, assisted by Lesley Coles and Teresa Jolliffe.

The club's marquee player Henrik Larsson chose to retire from professional football aged 38, taking over Landskrona as manager.

The club's mascot is a bear.

The club's mascot is a gray kangaroo, and its use dates from the middle of the 20th century.

The club's mascot is the great racehorse Bonecrusher (known as the Pride of Ellerslie).

The club's members are current and former members of the United State Armed Forces.

The club's members are students and staff from Trinity College and, occasionally, associate members from other Colleges.

The club's members are students and staff from Wadham College and Harris Manchester College.
