The Community Tool Box is a free, online resource that contains more than 7,000 pages of practical information for promoting community health and development, and is a global resource for both professionals and grassroots groups engaged in the work of community health and development.

The Community Tool Box is a public service of the University of Kansas in Lawrence, Kansas, United States.
