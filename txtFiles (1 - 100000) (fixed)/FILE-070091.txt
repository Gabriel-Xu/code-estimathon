The first evening schools opened in the first half of the nineteenth century under the pressure of the first civil strike organized by labor movements, with the main aim of reducing illiteracy.

The first event, a 35-lap feature race, was won by DAMS driver Romain Grosjean from a pole position start.

The first event, a lecture by Cundell, took place in December 1943 at the Guildhall School, shortly followed by the first concert, a performance by the Morley College Choir, in January 1944.
