The first six tracks on said edition were originally released as Lou Donaldson/Clifford Brown - New Faces-New Sounds (BLP 5030), whilst tracks #10-15 were released with the title New Star on the Horizon (BLP 5032), issued in 1954.

The first six tracks on the album are 1968 recordings of songs from Buckley's singer-songwriter period.

The first six tracks on the album are produced by Mike Stock and Pete Waterman.

The first six tracks were new studio recordings, while the final six were live recordings of some of The Exponents' hits, recorded at the Pounamu Hotel, in Takapuna in Auckland.

The first six tracks were recorded live at The Middle East in Cambridge MA on October 26, 1996.
