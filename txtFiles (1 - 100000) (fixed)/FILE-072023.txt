Their debut album, Beyond the Valley of the Murderdolls, was a studio album, consisting of 15 tracks.

Their debut album, Bitter Lullabies was released in January 2012 to a sold out album launch at The Sage Gateshead (Hall Two).

Their debut album, Black Sabbath, was released on Friday the 13th, February 1970, on Philips' newly formed progressive rock label, Vertigo Records.

Their debut album 'Bleak Strategies' was released on 1 April 2014 through the Pledgemusic platform.

Their debut album Bleibt das immer so (2013) reached No. 7 in the German albums chart.

Their debut album, "Blind Sighted Faith", produced by John Porter, was released in February 2012.
