The Museum of Arts and Design (MAD), based in Manhattan, New York City, collects, displays, and interprets objects that document contemporary and historic innovation in craft, art, and design.

The Museum of Arts and Sciences, often referred to as MOAS, is a museum in Daytona Beach, Florida, United States.

The Museum of Asian Art is located in a house on the waterfront near Fort St. Louis.

The Museum of Asian Art of Corfu is a museum in the Palace of St. Michael and St. George in Corfu, Greece.

The Museum of Automobiles is an automobile museum in the city of Morrilton, Arkansas, adjacent to Petit Jean State Park.

The Museum of Bags and Purses (Dutch: Tassenmuseum Hendrikje), is a museum devoted to the history of bags, purses, and their related accessories.

The Museum of Bakkie, located in the village Reynsdorp (now better known as Bakkie) has an impressive collection of old bottles, prints, maps, paintings and many utensils.

The museum of Battle of Gettysburg artifacts was in Rosensteel's 1884 frame home and served as the "Round Top inn" restaurant/small hotel.
