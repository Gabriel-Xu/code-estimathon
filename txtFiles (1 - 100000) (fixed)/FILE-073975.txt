The Museum Wiesbaden is a two-branch museum for art and natural history in the Hessian capital of Wiesbaden, Germany.

The museum will also display other rolling stock including a turntable from Auckland and any other suitable railway material.

The museum will also other NZR and Bush locomotives from the Canterbury Railway Society.

The museum will be based in Bradford, Yorkshire, England.

The museum will be constructed by Hyundai Construction.

The museum will be dedicated to Nuragic and contemporary art.

The museum will be operated by B.

D.

T. Smoke Shops, a head shop in Hazel Park since 1973.

The museum will be permanently housed in the USS Edson, a retired United States Navy destroyer that has recently been converted to a museum.

The museum will be skirted by the proposed Northern Distributor Road; aircraft were positioned elsewhere to accommodate this, although no museum space was lost.
