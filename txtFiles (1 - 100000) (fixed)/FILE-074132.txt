The name Toseland is Scandinavian and has connections to the Old Norse word 'lunde' translated as a type of sacred or small wood.

The name Tournesol is derived from Sunflower in French, communicating the values and direction of the organization seeking culturally awakening topics, themes and production.

The name, "Tour of Life", was not coined until after its completion, with all promotional material referring to it simply as the Kate Bush Tour.

The name Tower was a reference to the Post Office Tower now the BT Tower which stands near the geographic heart of the neighborhood.

The name "towhee" is onomatopoeic description of one of the towhee's most common calls, a short two-part call rising in pitch and sometimes also called a "chewink" call.

The name "Town Branch" commemorates the body of water that presently runs under Lexington, on which Lexington was founded.

The name Toxi became widely used as shorthand in the German media when referring to Afro-Germans and their social circumstances.

The name Toys for Bob was invented by Laurie Lessen-Reiche; it was chosen to stimulate curiosity and allude to Paul and Fred's appreciation of real toys.
