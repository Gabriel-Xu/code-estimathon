The National Traffic System (NTS) is an organized network of amateur radio operators sponsored by the American Radio Relay League for the purpose of relaying messages throughout the U.

S. and Canada.

The National Trail Conference (NTC) is a historic high school conference in east central Illinois.

The National Trail Local School K-12 complex is located on U.

S. Route 40 in Gettysburg.

The National Training Complex has four baseball fields (one Stadium Field and 3 Training Fields), with dimensions of 330 feet down the lines, 400 feet in center.

The National Training School For Boys, located in what is now known as the Fort Lincoln area of Washington, D.

C., was a Federal Government juvenile correctional institution for offenders under the age of seventeen.

The National Training School Of Cookery, founded in 1873, changed its name to The National Training School of Cookery and Other Branches of Domestic Economy in 1902 and, in 1931 became The National Training College of Domestic Science.

The National Training Service (Spanish: Servicio Nacional de Aprendizaje, SENA) is a Colombian public institution aimed to develop vocational training programs for the Colombian labor force as a means to increase the competitiveness of Colombia's enterprises.
