The Prime Minister's Office (also referred to as PMO) is the principle workplace of the Prime Minister of Pakistan, located at 44000 Constitution Avenue, Islamabad, Pakistan.

The prime minister's office announced a simplification of the Thai alphabet on 29 May 1942.
