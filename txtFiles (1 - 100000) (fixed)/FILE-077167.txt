The Rossborough Inn is a historic building facing Baltimore Avenue/United States Route 1 (also formerly known as the old Washington Boulevard and the Washington and Baltimore Turnpike) on the eastern edge of the campus of the University of Maryland at College Park.

The Rossborough Inn is listed as a historic site by the Maryland-National Capital Park and Planning Commission.

The Ross brand remains common in the retail frozen fish market.

The Ross Brook Formation is a geologic formation in Nova Scotia.

The Rossby whistle is the oscillation of sea-level and bottom pressure in the Caribbean Sea with the period of 120 days and influenced by propagating westward oceanic Rossby wave.
