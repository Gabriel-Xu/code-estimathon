The school was founded upon a principle of promoting public service by its students and graduates; each student must perform at least 30 hours of public service before graduation.

The school was founded with 15 pupils and 4 members of staff in January 1994.

The school was founded with just one building called "The White Block" in 1978, and in 1981 added the "New Block" for its additional pupils.

The school was founded with money left by John Fereby and his wife, a wealthy wool merchant, for the education of the poor boys of the town.

The school was found in 1932 by S.

K. Wijayaratnam for the Negombo Tamil people.

The school was found to be one of the most improved comprehensives in England in 2000, however St Clement's High School was judged to be inadequate after an Ofsted inspection in March 2013 and was placed in special measures.

The school was fully accredited by the American Bar Association (ABA) in August 2011.

The school was fully accredited in 1959 by the New England Association of Secondary Schools and Colleges.
