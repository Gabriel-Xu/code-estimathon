The Schrader company, for which it was named, was founded in 1844 by August Schrader.

The Schrader valve (also called American valve) is a type of pneumatic tire valve used on virtually every motor vehicle in the world today.

The Schraffts Building at 16-24 West Street was built in 1922, and housed a flagship candy store and restaurant for more than fifty years.

The Schrambach Formation is a geologic formation in Germany.
