The series was made with a very low budget for Thames Television, with a style inspired by fringe theater.

The series was made with a view to airing the show in the US, so episodes were shortened to 25 minutes rather than 30 minutes to allow for commercial breaks; and twenty-six episodes were commissioned to tie in with the American tradition of having "seasons", rather than the typical British "series" of six to eight episodes.

The series was managed by Graham Robson.

The series was managed by WC Vision and sanctioned by the Sports Car Club of America (SCCA).

The series was marked for the third anniversary installment of the Precious Hearts Romances television adaptations for giving Three Years of Many Top Rating Serials and is the first adaptation to have a Fantasy Action Adventure for Television under 'Precious Hearts Romances' television distribution.

The series was marketed as three ongoing series (G.

I. Joe: Cobra II), two mini-series (G.

I. Joe: Cobra and G.

I. Joe: Hearts And Minds) and a small series of one-shots (G.

I. Joe: Special).

The series was marketed by the BBC as "The Sunday Format for the online age", and critics have given Bigipedia favorable comparisons with its predecessor.
