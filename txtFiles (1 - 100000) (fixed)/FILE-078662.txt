The Society for Classical Reform Judaism is an advocacy group within the constituency of the American Union for Reform Judaism, promoting a return to the values and worship style of the "Classical Reform" era, stressing the universal message of Judaism as a purely religious community, the importance of belief and ethics, decorum and less practical observance.

The Society for Classical Studies (SCS), formerly known as the American Philological Association (APA), founded in 1869, is a non-profit North American scholarly organization devoted to all aspects of Greek and Roman civilization.
