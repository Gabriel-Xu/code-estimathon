The Spitfire Grill is an American musical with music and book by James Valcq and lyrics and book by Fred Alley, based on the 1996 film of the same name by Lee David Zlotoff.

The Spitfire is a 1914 American comedy film directed by Edwin S. Porter and Frederick A. Thomson, written by Edward Henry Peple, and starring Carlyle Blackwell, Violet Mersereau, Lionel Adams, Robert Cummings, William R. Dunn and Redfield Clarke.

The Spitfire is a high-performance catamaran used for training and racing.

The Spitfire is a lost 1924 silent film society drama directed by Christy Cabanne and starring Betty Blythe and Lowell Sherman.

The Spitfires Ice A team play their home games at Basingstoke Arena, the Spitfires Ice B and C team play their home games at Gosport Arena or Basingstoke Arena.

The Spitfires inflicted substantial losses on Japanese raiders as North-Western Area stepped up its attacks on Japanese positions.

The Spitfires Inline A and B team play their games at Solent Arena although the Inline B team does not currently play in league games.

The Spitfires played home games at the Windsor Arena, built in 1924.

The Spitfires were the junior team of the Stoke Potters.
