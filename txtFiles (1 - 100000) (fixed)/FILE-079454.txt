The St Ives by-election, 1937 was a by-election held in England on 30 June 1937 for the House of Commons constituency of the St Ives in Cornwall.

The St Ives by-election of 1875 was fought on 5 March 1875.

The St Ives by-election of 1881 was fought on 11 April 1881.

The St Ives School refers to a group of artists living and working in the Cornish town of St Ives.

The St. Ives Times and Echo is an independent, weekly local newspaper based in St Ives, Cornwall, England, United Kingdom.

The STIX fonts are available as fully hinted OpenType/CFF fonts.
