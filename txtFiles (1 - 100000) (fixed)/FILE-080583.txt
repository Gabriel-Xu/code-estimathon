The Tupolev Tu-16 (NATO reporting name: Badger) was a twin-engined jet strategic heavy bomber used by the Soviet Union.

The Tupolev Tu-2000 was a planned hypersonic experimental aircraft designed by the Tupolev design bureau.

The Tupolev Tu-360 was a hypersonic strategic bomber conceived by the Tupolev design bureau in the 1980s.

The Tupolev Tu-4 (NATO reporting name: Bull) was a piston-engined Soviet strategic bomber that served the Soviet Air Force from the late 1940s to mid-1960s.

The Tupolev Tu-70 (NATO reporting name: Cart) was a Soviet passenger variant of the Tu-4 bomber (which was a reverse-engineered copy of the American-made Boeing B-29 Superfortress) designed immediately after the end of World War II.

The Tupolev Tu-72 was a proposed Soviet medium bomber of the late 1940s.

The Tupolev Tu-82 was a 1940s Soviet experimental swept-wing bomber.

The Tupolev Tu-85 (USAF/DoD reporting name 'Type 31', NATO reporting name Barge) was a Soviet prototype strategic bomber based on the Tu-4, an unlicensed reverse engineered copy of the Boeing B-29 Superfortress.
