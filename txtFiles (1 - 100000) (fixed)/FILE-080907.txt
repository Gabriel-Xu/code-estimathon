The U.

S. Highways in Tennessee are the segments of the United States Numbered Highway System that are maintained by the Tennessee Department of Transportation (TDOT) in the state of Tennessee.

The U.

S. Highways in the District of Columbia comprise 10 current and former United States Numbered Highways in Washington, D.

C..

The U.

S. Highways in the U.

S. state of West Virginia are owned and maintained by the West Virginia Division of Highways.

The U.

S. Highways in Wisconsin comprise 14 current and two former United States Numbered Highways in Wisconsin.
