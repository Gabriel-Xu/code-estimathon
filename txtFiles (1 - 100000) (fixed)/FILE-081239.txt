The weir or saddle type is the most common in process applications and the seat-type is more commonly used in slurry applications to reduce blocking issues but exists also as a process valve.

The weir piles were then removed and a new footbridge built.

The weir raises the level of the Goulburn River so that water can be diverted, by gravity, along the main irrigation supply channels: Stuart Murray Canal, Cattanach Canal, East Goulburn Main Channel.

The weir runs across from the Abingdon side to the lock island, and has a pedestrian walkway across it.
