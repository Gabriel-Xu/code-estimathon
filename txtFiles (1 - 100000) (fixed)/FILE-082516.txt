This church building is one of the very few church buildings that is currently divided into two worship areas, one Catholic and one Anglican, with the western side of the church building occupied by the Anglican church of St Nicholas.

This church building remains significant because of its grand architectural elements, which have led to its designation as a historic site.

This church came to be known as the First Colored Baptist Church of Shreveport.

This church claims to be the mother church of at least seven other baptist churches in the area.

This church comes under Belthangady Varado which is a part of Roman Catholic Diocese of Mangalore.

This church comes under Deanery of Belthangady, Roman Catholic Diocese of Mangalore.
