Tower Theater is a theater located in the Terminal Square section of Upper Darby, Pennsylvania at the intersection of 69th and Ludlow Streets.

Tower Theater, Upper Darby 1975 is a live album by Bruce Springsteen & The E Street Band, released in February 2015 and was the third official release through the Bruce Springsteen Archives.

Tower (Tour) is a 1987 mural by Keith Haring covering a stairwell from the Necker-Enfants Malades Hospital in Paris, France.

Tower Transit is a Transport for London-contracted bus operator operating mainly in west and east London.
