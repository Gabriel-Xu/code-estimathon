Transgender women are defined as natal males with female identification or expression and transgender men are natal females with male identification or expression.

Transgender youth are children and adolescents who are transgender and/or transsexual.

Transgene escape has been documented for GMO crops since 2001 with persistence and invasiveness.

Transgenerational trauma is trauma that is transferred from the first generation of trauma survivors to the second and further generations of offspring of the survivors via complex post-traumatic stress disorder mechanisms.
