Wendy McElroy (born 1951) is a Canadian individualist feminist and anarcho-capitalist writer.

Wendy McMahon (born 1951 in Edmonton, Alberta) is a Canadian former politician, who was a BC Liberal Member of the Legislative Assembly of British Columbia from 2001 to 2005.

Wendy McMurdo (born 1962 in Edinburgh, Scotland) is a British artist who specializes in photography and digital media.
