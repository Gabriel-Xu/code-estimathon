Zaslow is currently heard weekdays from 6am-10am on The Ticket Miami in the station's morning drive time slot.

Zaslow originally was a producer for Craig Minervini and then became a producer for The Boog Sciambi Show hosted by Jon Sciambi.

Zaslow was raised in North Miami Beach, Florida where he attended Sabal Palm Elementary School and was a classmate of James Jones of the Miami Heat and 2011 NBA three point shootout champion.

Zaslow would later appear on One Life to Live as David Renaldi from 1983 to 1986, and again in 1998.
