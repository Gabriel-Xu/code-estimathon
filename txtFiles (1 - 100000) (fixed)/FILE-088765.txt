Obama's comments today in Berlin about 'breaking down the walls' come tinged with irony as the President delivered his remarks behind thick walls of bullet-proof glass. The safety measure has been a constant presence throughout his presidency when he makes appearances in large public spaces. The last time that Obama spoke to 200,000 people in Berlin, there was no safety glass  - but then, he was not yet president. It was put up at his victory speech in November 2008, when Obama and family waved to supporters behind the two-inch thick walls. Since then, it has been in constant use. The reported $100million cost of Obama's trip to Africa this month includes three trucks with sheets of bullet­proof glass - some of which will be used to cover hotel windows.
