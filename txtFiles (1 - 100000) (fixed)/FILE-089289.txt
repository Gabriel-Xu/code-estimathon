Doctors believe Jayden Channell survived because he has highly-flexible joints which are thought to have helped his body absorb the impact. While the car was left dented, Jayden was able to stand up immediately and walk away with only grazes and a bump to his head despite bouncing off the bonnet and being sent flying. 'He looked like a rag doll': Jayden Channell, five, was sent flying into the air after being hit by a car outside his home in Essex - but escaped unscathed thanks to a condition called hypermobility syndrome . Relief: Smiling Jayden, pictured with his mother Samantha Taplin, sustained barely a scratch when he was hit by the car on Bank Holiday Monday . Jayden has hypermobility syndrome, . which means he is double-jointed. The collagen in his skin and ligaments . is weaker than normal, making his joints looser and more stretchy so . that they can extend further than usual.
