Sam Kane Beef Processors of Corpus Christi, Texas, has recalled 90,987 pounds of ground beef products after consumers complained about finding pieces of metal inside the meat, the U.

S. Department of Agriculture said Saturday. The USDA said four consumers complained, with one person reporting a chipped tooth. The pieces of metal were reported to be about 3mm, a USDA press release said. Alfred Bausch, general manager for the company, said all the products were shipped to Texas retail outlets. "It was not a food safety issue," he said. "It was a foreign object and the foreign object was very small."
