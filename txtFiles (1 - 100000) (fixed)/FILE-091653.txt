Biden set the scene during the speech by detailing how his family would stop by a doughnut shop after Mass every Sunday. "We'd get donuts, and my dad would wait in the car. Biden then said he walked up behind the boy, who was with his parents, and "smashed his head next to the counter." When the crowd applauded, Biden said, "I'm not recommending it." After the scuffle, Biden says the boy's father grabbed him. "Now, that was a euphemism," Biden said, getting to the point of the story. "I thought I was really, really in trouble.
