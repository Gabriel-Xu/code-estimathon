Anthony Doyle, 29, was given a lethal . injection on Thursday for the death of Hyun Mi Cho, 37, who he beat with a baseball bat before stealing her car, phone and . credit cards in Rowlett in 2003. After the injection, Doyle took a few breaths and began to snore quietly before he stopped moving, the Dallas Morning News reported. He was pronounced dead 25 minutes later, at 6.

49pm. Doyle did not make a statement before his death, a Department of Criminal Justice spokesman said. Texas ended its tradition of preparing a requested last meal for death row inmates in 2011.
