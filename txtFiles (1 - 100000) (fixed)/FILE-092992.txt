Slade’s Merry Xmas Everybody has earned more than £500,000 in royalties for 2013 alone – and the total could swell by a further £300,000 by the 25th. Slade, The Pogues and Mariah Carey earn small fortunes from their festive hits every winter, thanks to radio coverage and compilation album sales. Now analysts have estimated the money earned by the most popular tracks by combining radio, internet and TV data with statistics on royalties from the Performing Right Society, which collects fees when music is played everywhere from Radio 1 to jukeboxes and shops. Their findings suggest that Holder wasn’t far off when he said that owning the rights to his band’s 1973 hit was ‘like winning the lottery every December 25 for the rest of your life’.
