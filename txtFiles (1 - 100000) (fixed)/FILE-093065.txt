A British woman was been forced to move home from Australia after discovering she was recorded as a male on her birth registry - and now claims she is technically locked in an 'illegal gay’ marriage. Mother-of-five Kim Walmsley, 49, says her world has been turned upside down after it came to light the registrar wrongly recorded her as a male when she was born in February 1965. Mrs Walmsley says when she first noticed the mistake more than a decade ago when she applied for a full-time Australian visa she 'laughed it off' and thought it would be put right straight away - but after years living in the UK unable to move back she has turned to the Prime Minister for help. But she was then told the information could not be altered because it is a legal document - which she claims has made her 23-year marriage to husband Jack, 49, invalid. The family, who were living their dream life in Australia, have since been forced to move back to the UK after Mrs Walmsley was unable to renew her passport due to the error. Mrs Walmsley says the cost of the move and the paperwork trying to fight it has cost £150,000 - and the only person left who can help is David Cameron. She said: 'The whole thing is absolutely disgusting. I’ve lived nearly 40 years not realising I was actually registered as a boy and then all of a sudden my whole life fell apart. Although the General Registry Office accepted it was an error they said it was against the law to change it and there was nothing they could do.
