The other cattle were underweight and dehydrated after being left exposed in sub-zero conditions without enough food or water. The shocking case came despite Undre's restaurant Down to Earth, in west London's Kensington High Street, boasting of its 'environmentally sound, sustainable and animal friendly' credentials.
