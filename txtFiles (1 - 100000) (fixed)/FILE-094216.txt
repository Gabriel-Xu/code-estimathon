Many of the victims had gathered to watch a cricket match on Sunday evening in a village about 30 kilometers (20 miles) southwest of the state capital, Lucknow, government official Anil Garg said Tuesday. By midday Tuesday, 28 people had died, including 11 in a nearby village, police officer Mukul Goel said.
