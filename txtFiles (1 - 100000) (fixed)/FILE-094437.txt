The seabed is being searched and those searches will intensify when the ship is gone, they said. Salvage crews began the arduous task of refloating the ship on Monday morning so they can move it from its resting place off Giglio Island to Genoa to be dismantled. On Tuesday, they began the process of lowering steel hollow boxes, or sponsons, which will be pumped full of compressed air to help lift the vessel further. Divers also began attaching more chains and cables to reinforce the bottom of the boat. Once that's done, they'll raise the ship deck by deck, clearing any debris along the way. After the ship is completely floated, a convoy of 10 boats will then begin a potentially treacherous journey.
