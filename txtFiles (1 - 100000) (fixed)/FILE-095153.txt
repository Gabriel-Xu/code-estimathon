High heels... they are the bane of most women's lives. From then onwards, both sexes turned to the heels throughout the centuries, with some reaching as high as 30 inches in Venice in the 1400s. In the 17th century, King Louis XIV of France wore shoes with five inch heels and made a rule that nobody could wear higher heels than him.
