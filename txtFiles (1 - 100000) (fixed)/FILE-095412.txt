Australians living in Britain are heading home where employment opportunities are at an all-time high. Known as the ‘boomerang effect,’ research has shown that Australian and New Zealand expats are sick of the miserable job market in the UK where unemployment was recently at a 17-year peak. The research, done by jobs website Track Me Back, found that the positive outlook at home is enticing expats to return.
