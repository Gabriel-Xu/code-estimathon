Pope Francis has condemned the West for selling chemical weapons responsible for thousands of civilian deaths to Syria. Returning from a three-day trip to Turkey the pope said the Middle East state did not have the ability to manufacture the chemical weapons that were deployed against Syrian civilians last year, adding they must have been supplied them by its 'accusers'.
