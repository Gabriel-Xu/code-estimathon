'To hear that I had a disease that could not be cured, I was so afraid... It is as if I have just been reborn,' she told reporters on Thursday outside a treatment centre in the capital Conakry, days after medics had saved her life. A view of gloves and boots used by medical staff, drying in the sun, at a centre for victims of the Ebola virus in Guekedou . Guinea has been hit by the most severe strain of the virus, known as Zaire Ebola, which has had a fatality rate of up to 90 per cent in past outbreaks, and for which there is no vaccine, cure or even specific treatment.
