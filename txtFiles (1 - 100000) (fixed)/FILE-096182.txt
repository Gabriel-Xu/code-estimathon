By . Daniel Miller . The Free Syrian Army has been accused of war crimes after video emerged appearing to show a group of rebels executing around 10 captured government soldiers. The disturbing clip, which has been branded 'shocking' by human rights group Amnesty International, shows the captured soldiers, some of whom appear wounded, being heaped together on the ground. They are heard pleading for their lives as they are taunted and kicked by the rebel troops who then step back and open fire. Scroll down for video .
