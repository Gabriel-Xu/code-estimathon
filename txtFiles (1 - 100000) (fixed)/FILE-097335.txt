If you want to check out more EKG readings related to the Apollo 11 moon mission, Jay Walker, curator and chairman of TEDMED, showed off fellow astronaut Buzz Aldrin's EKG reading in a TEDMED 2012 presentation. The reading, taken as the astronauts were attempting to find a place to park the Apollo 11 lunar module during the craft's descent onto the moon's surface, is "a bit accelerated," Walker comments."

In a thousand years this will still be an amazing artifact, because we saw, at the moment man landed on the moon  we were monitoring their vital signs back on the Earth which is something we will all be doing, as a matter of course, in just a few years for all of us and our loved ones."
