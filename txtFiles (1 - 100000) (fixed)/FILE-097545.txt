For just a moment it looked as if the brilliance that had earned Martin Kaymer a place in the record books at the halfway point of the 114th United States Open was about to unravel. Becoming the first man to open a major championship with two rounds of 65 suddenly appeared more than 24 hours ago. Now he was staring down a 15ft putt for a bogey at the fourth. Miss it and he would have been three over par at that early stage, and his six shot lead would have been halved after less than an hour’s play.
