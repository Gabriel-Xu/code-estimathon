Buses are to run 10 minutes from 07:00 to 19:00 Monday to Saturday, apart from Leeds United's home fixtures.

Electric car drivers can also make use of five electric-car charging points.

A bus priority lane at Junction 3 of the M621 means buses using the site should not have to queue in traffic.
