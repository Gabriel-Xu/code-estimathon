Argentine ants were very successful as an an invasive species, spreading across the country with their territorial behaviour and super-colonies, having arrived in the U.

Lead author of the research into this ant influx, Eleanor Spicer Rice at North Carolina University, said the Asian ants could be the next major ant invasion. The researchers believe the Asian . ants have an advantage over the Argentines because they are active in . cool weather, whereas the Argentine ant rests until spring. The Asian ants start building new nests around March, whereas the Argentine ants start in late April or May, giving them a headstart every year.
