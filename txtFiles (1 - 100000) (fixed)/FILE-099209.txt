Not because this 56-year-old Glaswegian couldn't handle the heat, but rather he had Premier League matches to watch. On the Sunday, he was at Old Trafford as Manchester United’s 'Gaalacticos' illustrated their attacking force while just about hiding their defensive frailties in the 4-0 win over Queens Park Rangers. In 48 hours, roughly 400 miles covered as Irvine sought to scout future opponents and get a measure of what his team will face this season. Rather than rely on videos or reports, he wants to witness as much football first-hand as possible.
