The aim was to have the best Slovenian-speaking footballers in Carinthia play in a team, to celebrate sporting successes with the SAK and thus draw attention to the Slovenian ethnic group throughout Austria.The biggest opponents of the SAK in the 1970s were pro-German referees and the spectators. At that time, many games were more like political events for or against the rights of the Slovene minority than football games. Nevertheless, the team was again relegated one year later to the Regional League, where they have played until 2001, from 2002 until 2003 and from 2005 until 2015.
