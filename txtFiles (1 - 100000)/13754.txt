The coronation of Charles III and his wife, Camilla, as king and queen of the United Kingdom and the other Commonwealth realms was held on Saturday, 6 May 2023, at Westminster Abbey. Charles became the king on 8 September 2022 upon the death of his mother, Elizabeth II.Charles III became king immediately upon the death of his mother, Elizabeth II, on Thursday 8 September 2022.
