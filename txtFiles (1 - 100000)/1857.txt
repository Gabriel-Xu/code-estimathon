These two operations must follow special rules to work together in a ring.Mathematicians use the word "ring" this way because a mathematician named David Hilbert used the German word "Zahlring" to describe these structures. The integers, the rational numbers, the real numbers and the complex numbers are all famous examples of rings. There are a few rules the set and the operations must obey to qualify as a ring.
