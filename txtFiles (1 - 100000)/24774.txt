Cricket Lee (born March 17, 1953 in Decatur, Alabama) is an American entrepreneur and inventor, notable for developments towards better fitting clothes.Cricket Leigh is an American actress.Cricket lingered on over the following century, with occasional visits of German players to England and British and other foreign teams touring in Germany, but only when it got a foothold in the German universities in the 1980s did the number of German cricket clubs and players start to grow again.
