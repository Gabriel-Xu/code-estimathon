Eucalyptus chapmaniana, commonly known as the Bogong gum, is a small to medium-sized tree of montane and sub-alpine eastern Victoria and far south-eastern New South Wales.Eucalyptus chartaboma, commonly known as paperbark gum, is a eucalypt that is native to Queensland.Eucalyptus chloroclada, commonly known as dirty gum, is a tree native to New South Wales and Queensland in eastern Australia.Eucalyptus cladocalyx, commonly known as sugar gum, is a species of eucalypt tree found in the Australian state of South Australia.Eucalyptus clelandii, commonly known as Cleland's blackbutt, is a species of eucalypt native to Western Australia.Eucalyptus cneorifolia is now the predominant strain used in production due to a higher oil content in new growth.Eucalyptus cneorifolia, the Kangaroo Island narrow-leaf mallee, is a native tree of Kangaroo Island, South Australia.Eucalyptus coccifera is a member of the peppermint group (series Piperitae) within subgenus Eucalyptus (formerly Monocalyptus).
