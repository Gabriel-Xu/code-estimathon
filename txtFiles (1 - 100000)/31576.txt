School of Medicine and the Otago University Medical School.He attended the Film and Television Studio International and won the role of Chris Knight in Neighbors.He attended the Film Production course worked as an assistant director on Kang Je-gyu's The Gingko Bed (1996).He attended the first foundation of Buenos Aires by Pedro de Mendoza.He attended the First International Syndicalist Congress, held in London, 27 September to 2 October, 1913, but not as delegate of the IWW.He attended the first session of the Fifth Lateran Council, before giving up the bishopric in favor of his nephew.He November 2015 with a promotion to Vice Admiral in 2017.He attended the Frankfurt University of Music and Performing Arts and graduated with a Master's degree.He attended the Frank Wiggins Trade School Frunze Academy in 1981 and the General Staff Academy in 1992.He attended the GAFCON meeting in Jerusalem, in 2008, and supported the creation of the Anglican Church in North America, in June 2009.He attended the Georgia Institute of Technology before transferring to the University of Alabama.He attended the Georgia Institute of Technology in 1917 and gave generously to both his alma mater and Emory University, including (in coordination with his brother Robert W. Woodruff) what was at the time the single largest donation ever to a school, $105 million to Emory University in 1979.He attended the Georgia Military Academy and Oklahoma State University.He attended the Gigantour with The Dillinger Escape Plan as a Massage Therapist for guitarist Ben Weinman.He attended the Glasgow School of Art and soon was contributing articles, drawings, and caricatures to newspapers and magazines including The Light Car.He attended the Glasgow School of Art where he studied mural painting.He attended the Global Anglican Future Conference in Jerusalem, also in June 2008.He attended the Goodman School of Drama in Chicago, Illinois from 1953 to 1958.He attended the graduate program in photography at Ohio University in Athens, Ohio, and received a Master of Fine Arts in Photography from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago, where he also taught for 38 years.He attended the Grand Canyon University.
