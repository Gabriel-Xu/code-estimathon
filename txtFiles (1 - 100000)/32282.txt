He has raced as a member of Team Oreca, Peugeot Sport, CD Sport Performance Engineering, Team Speedcars, Team Iconic Parts, and Backdraft Racing.He has raced exclusively in cars manufactured by the Audi marque since 1990.He has raced for many factory teams and holds several titles, back to back AMA 125 West Supercross Championships in 1996 and 1997, the 2005 Motocross des Nations team championship, and 2nd in points for the 2008 supercross season.He has raced for most of his career in Great Britain and Europe, where he currently resides in Buckinghamshire England with his partner Sheila.He has raced for the US Sailing Team since 2003.He has raced in all three of NASCAR's major series and is currently a free agent.He has raced in a variety of different series, most notably the British Touring Car Championship (BTCC), the FIA GT1 World Championship, the Le Mans Series, the FIA World Endurance Championship and the 24 Hours of Le Mans.He has raced in Denmark for Slangerup.He has raced in dirt late models, the Mid-American Stock Car Series, SCCA, and had one NASCAR Nationwide Series start in 2012.He has raced in Europe for ten years, for Amore E Vita, Mobilvetta, Mercury, Domina Vacanze and Tenax professional cycling teams.He has raced in Formula Ford as well as many Kart racing events such as the Le Mans 24 Hour, Daytona 500 and Spa Francorchamps 10 Hour.He has raced in Formula Renault series around the world for most of his career, winning the Formula Renault 1.6 Belgium championship in 2006.He has raced in France in the Mini Transat 6.50, Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race, Fastnet, Transpacific Yacht Race and other sailing races.He has raced in junior motorsport category for most of his racing career, before driving in the 2008 Superleague Formula season for Olympiacos CFP in the final rounds.He has raced in most major US and international motorcycle racing championships, in both Grand Prix and Superbike categories.He has raced in MotoGP (full-time in 250s, and part-time in 500s and MotoGP itself), the Superbike World Championship, and the British Superbike Championship, taking the British crown in 2005.He has raced in NASCAR, CART, IndyCar, Trans-Am, IMSA, IROC and Dakar Rally.He has raced in NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series and Pickup Truck Racing with TorqueSpeed.He has raced in NASCAR for four years and has competed in 16 races with one finish in the top five and two finishes in the top ten.He has raced in numerous off-road circuits, including SCORE International/Tecate Baja series and Best In The Desert (BITD).He has raced in sports cars, Indy Lights, NASCAR, and Speed Energy Formula Off-Road.He has raced in such series as Auto GP and Formula Palmer Audi.He has raced in such series as Le Mans Series, V8Star Series and the FIA GT Championship.He has raced in such series as Porsche Supercup and Stock Car Brasil.He has raced in such series as Superstars Series, where he has been a two-time runner-up, and was International Superstars Series champion in 2008.He has raced internationally for Italy.He has raced in the championship since 2008, mostly in a BMW machinery finishing 6th in 2010 with two podiums, 4th in 2011 with a 1 win and 4 podiums, and champion in 2012 and 2013, he previously raced in the European Superstock 600 championship in 2006 and 2007.He has raced in the European Rallycross Championship since 2012 and the World Rallycross Championship since its inception in 2014.He has raced in the European Superstock 600 Championship, the Moto2 World Championship and the Supersport World Championship.He has raced in the Formula Renault 3.5 Series and also in the Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters.He has raced in the Hong Kong Touring Car Championship and the Canadian Touring Car Championship.He has raced in the Hong Kong Touring Car Championship but is best known for his appearance in the World Touring Car Championship.He has raced in the Macau and Asian Touring Car Championships and will race for Corsa Motorsport in the final round of the 2011 World Touring Car Championship.He has raced in the NASCAR Busch Series and Craftsman Truck Series.He has raced in the United States Auto Club, the Auto Racing Club of American, and NASCAR.He has raced in various classes, probably known for his V8 Supercar Season in 2004, driving a privately entered ex Glenn Seton Ford Falcon AU where he entered 6 events, qualifying for only 5 of them.He has raced mostly in short track competition and as high as the NASCAR Busch Series (now Xfinity Series).He has raced motorcycles, drag cars, and stock cars.He has raced on both two and four wheels internationally.He has raced on the national level in the ARCA RE/MAX Series (now ARCA Racing Series), Camping World Truck Series, and the Xfinity Series.He has raced the #22 pickup since 2002 in the UK Pickup Truck Racing series.He has raced the #74 pickup since 2005 in the UK Pickup Truck Racing series.He has raised funds for the Evelina Children's Hospital and for a local Garden Farm.He has raised over $100 million from public and private investors.He has raised slavery issues in the United Nations, through the Working Group for Contemporary Forms of Slavery and in International Labor Organization (ILO).
