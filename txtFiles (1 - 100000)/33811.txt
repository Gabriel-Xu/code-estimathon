2001 and became a Member of the Order of Australia in 2003.He received a centennial medal for his community service from premier Ralph Klein in 2005.He received a certificate in broadcast meteorology utter disregard of personal danger in the effort to repel the attack on the Naval Air Station, Kaneohe Bay, Oahu.He received a City of Los Angeles Certificate of Service, for his chairmanship of The received a commendation at the 2003 Ian Charleson Awards.He received a commission to write the story The Visitation.He received a common school education, came to Wisconsin in 1849 and settled in Milwaukee, where he became a dealer in cattle.He received a concurrent sentence of 7 years and 11 months for possessing child pornography.He received a confirmation from the Emperor Louis II of all of
