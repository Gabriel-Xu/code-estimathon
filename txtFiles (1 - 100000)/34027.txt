Herodes Atticus and Publius Aelius Vibullius Rufus were first cousins.He rode Seabiscuit in his first race in January 1935 before the colt was sold.He rode St George II to victory in the 1938 Cheltenham National Hunt Chase, and in 1946 he won the Grand National on Lovely Cottage.He rode ten editions of the Tour de France and won 3 stages, one in 1980, one in 1982 and one in 1986.He rode the 1960 and 1961 Tour de France.He rode the 1979 and 1980 Tour de France.He rode the 1980 Tour de France.He rode the 1985 Tour de France.He rode the 1989 Grand National winner Little Polveir when completing the course in the 1986 Grand National and when falling in the 1987 Grand National.
