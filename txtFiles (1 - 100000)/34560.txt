He was a member of the New South Wales Legislative Council from 1856 until his resignation in 1858.He was a member of the New South Wales Legislative Council from 2001 until 2008, and Treasurer of New South Wales from 2006 to 2008 and held other ministerial portfolios in the governments of premiers Bob Carr and Morris Iemma.He was a member of the New Warriors.He was a member of the New Wave band Face to Face and is best known for his work with Kings of Leon.He was a member of the New York City Council from the New York City borough of Queens from 1964 to 1977 and the leader of the Queens Democratic Party from 1971 to 1974.He was a member of the New York Giants and Chicago Rush.He was a member of the New York Giants and the Oakland Raiders of the National Football League and BC Lions of the Canadian Football League.He was a member of the New York Giants during the 1969 NFL season.He was a member of the New York Giants team that won the 1927 NFL Championship.He was a member of the New York Jazz Repertory Company when it toured the Soviet Union in 1975.He was a member of the New York Renaissance basketball team, and part of the first black team to win a world championship in basketball.He was a member of the New York State Assembly (Cayuga Co.) in 1836 and 1837.He was a member of the New York State Assembly for 23 years.He was a member of the New York State Assembly from 1975 to 1978, sitting in the 181st and 182nd New York State Legislatures.He was a member of the New York State Assembly from 1977 to 1980, sitting in the 182nd and 183rd New York State Legislatures.He was a member of the New York State Assembly, representing about half of Albany County in the 104th Assembly District, from 1993 through 2012.He was a member of the New York State Commission on Judicial Conduct for 13 years until March, 2017.He was a member of the New York State Senate from 1965 to 1984, sitting in the 175th, 176th, 177th, 178th, 179th, 180th, 181st, 182nd, 183rd, 184th and 185th New York State Legislatures.He was a member of The New York Times reporting team that received the 2015 Pulitzer Prize for International Reporting for coverage of the 2014 Ebola virus epidemic in West Africa.
