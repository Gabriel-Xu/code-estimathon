He was an attorney in the Office of the General Counsel for the U.S. Government Accounting Office from 1965 to 1969.Tidwell then held a series of positions within the United States Department of the Interior, first as a staff attorney from 1969 to 1971, then as an assistant solicitor for procurement until 1974, associate solicitor for general law until 1976, and associate solicitor for energy and resources until 1977.He was an attorney of the Court of Common Pleas.He was an attorney with the firm of Cole, Wishner, Epstein & Manilow before joining Walter E. Heller & Co., a finance company, where he eventually served as president and chief operating officer.He was a natural son of King Valdemar II of Denmark by an unknown mistress.He was an auditor and examiner during the inventory of King James III's property.He was an auditor and receiver of the ransom of King James I of Scotland in 1424.He was an auditor, school principal, professor of educational administration.He was an Augustine friar before he changed vocations and became a barrister and writer.
