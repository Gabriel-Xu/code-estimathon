He was member for Campbelltown in the New South Wales Legislative Assembly between 1981 and 2001.He was member for Plymouth and Tavistock.He was Member Legislative Assembly in 2009, also as a BJP member.He was member of 10th, 11th, 12th and 13th Lok Sabha, as BJP representative from Hapur seat of Uttar Pradesh.He was member of 10th,11th & 12th Vidhan Sabha of Madhya Pradesh Assembly from Niwari between 1993-2003.He was member of 10th Lok Sabha from Badaun in 1991 and from Machhlishahr in 1998.He was member of 11th Lok Sabha, 14th Lok Sabha, 15th Lok Sabha.He was member of 12th and 14th Lok Sabha from Gonda, Uttar Pradesh as Samajwadi Party candidate.He was member of 12th Lok Sabha from Shimoga and defeated Sarekoppa Bangarappa of Congress.He was member of 13th Lok Sabha.He was Member of 15th Lok Sabha.He was member of 15th Lok Sabha from Khajuraho seat.He was member of 1st Lok Sabha from Bhusaval (Lok Sabha constituency).He was member of 2nd and 3rd Lok Sabha from Bhandara (Lok Sabha constituency).He was member of 2nd Lok Sabha from Ramtek (Lok Sabha constituency).He was member of 4th Lok Sabha from Bilaspur.He was member of 5th Lok Sabha by winning by-elections in 1974 at Janjgir Lok Sabha constituency.He was member of 7th Lok Sabh during 1980-84.He was member of Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) from 2013 to 2014.
