He was runner-up in the 2009 Formula Le Mans Cup season to DAMS teammate Nico Verdonck.He was runner-up in the 2009 Jersey Seniors Classic, losing at the third playoff hole to Delroy Cambridge.He was runner-up in the 400IH in the NAIA national championships; also setting a new Guatemalan national record in the event.He was runner-up in the Austrian International Championship in both 1909 and 1913.He was runner-up in the club best and fairest in 2014.He was runner-up in the club Best & Fairest in 1956, and club leading goalkicker a number of times between 1955 and 1965.He was runner up in the FAI Cup in 1982 and 1983 and runner up in the League of Ireland in 1984 and 1985.He was runner-up in the Jack Dyer Medal count, polling only one vote behind Matthew Richardson.He was runner up in the middleweight tournament after losing to Hector Lombard.He was runner-up in the Oceania Play-offs, however Glen Wilkinson who beat him decided not take his place on the Main Tour.He was runner up in the QAFL best and Fairest award in 1984 and also played 12 representative games for Queensland.He was runner-up in the same competition in 1995 and moved to Aidan O'Brien's Ballydoyle stable in 1996 where he was second jockey after Christy Roche.He was runner-up in the steeplechase at the 1962 NCAAs for San Jose State College coached by Lloyd (Bud) Winter.He was runner up in the UK Championships in the 100/200 in 1978 and 1980.He was runner-up on both occasions.He was runner-up six times on the European Tour, at the 2003 Madeira Island Open, 2007 Valle Romano Open de Andalucia, 2010 BMW PGA Championship, 2011 Barclays Scottish Open, 2012 UBS Hong Kong Open and 2012 Omega European Masters.
