In 1958 she became the first woman to win Olympic, European and Commonwealth titles.In 1958 she collided with the ferry Ashtabula in the harbor of Ashtabula, Ohio.In 1958, she joined the Canadian Negro Women's Association (CANEWA), later the Congress of Black Women of Canada.In 1958, Sheraton renamed the hotel the Sheraton-West Hotel.In 1958 she rescued 24 members of the crew of a British tanker that had been sunk by the CIA.In 1958 she was named an Honored Professor of Public Education in the Uzbek SSR, and in 1978 she was named an Honored Teacher of Uzbekistan.In 1958, she was rebuilt as a passenger liner.In 1958 she was renamed Montmajour and in 1963 was renamed Mantinia, serving until 1978 when she was laid up.In 1958 she was sold to a German shipping line, and in 1971 she was scrapped after 39 years' service.In 1958 she was sold to Panama and renamed Northern Venture serving until running aground off Okinawa in 1967.In 1958 she was sold to the Maldavian National Shipping Corp (Ceylon) Ltd and renamed Maldive Star, serving for a further fourteen years until scrapped in 1967.In 1958 she was sold to the Western Steamship Co Ltd, Hong Kong and renamed Sunlight.In 1958 she won the Miss Finland beauty contest.In 1958, Sir J. J. School of Art was divided, with the Departments of Architecture and Applied Art becoming the Sir J. J. College of Architecture and Sir J.J. Institute of Applied Art respectively.In 1958, small Burnham Hall was obtained to become the clubhouse.In 1958, some Masyumi members joined the PRRI rebellion against Sukarno.
