It borders the Atlantic Ocean to the north, Suriname to the east, Brazil to the south and the regions of Mahaica-Berbice, Upper Demerara-Berbice, Potaro-Siparuni and Upper Takutu-Upper Essequibo to the west.It borders the Belmont Regional Park and features much native bush and beautiful views.It borders the Bluff View neighborhood on the west and the Preston Center neighborhood on the east.It borders the borough of Gravesham to the east, Sevenoaks District to the south, the London Borough of Bexley to the west, and the Thurrock unitary authority in Essex to the north, across the River Thames.It borders the Botsford section of town, Newtown borough, and also the towns of Monroe, Southbury and Oxford along the Housatonic River.It borders the bypass (and Hermiston Gait) to the west, the Calders to the south, Sighthill to the east, and South Gyle/Edinburgh Park to the north.It borders the Canadian province of New Brunswick.It borders the Canadian provinces of Quebec and Ontario.It borders the Canterbury Region, West Coast Region, Marlborough Region and Nelson City.It borders the capital of the United States of America, Washington, D.C., situated just south of the downtown district.It borders the Catholic High School of Holy Trinity, and the multi-recreational Mill Woods Park.It borders the Chaco Core, which contains both Chacra Mesa and Chaco Canyon.It borders the Cherwell Valley Line railway a short distance south of Oxford railway station.It borders the Chitrakoot district in Uttar Pradesh, whose headquarters Chitrakoot Dham (Karwi) is located nearby.It borders the cities of Pasay and Makati in the south.It borders the city line with Delaware County at Cobbs Creek, and extends to the Schuylkill River.It borders the city of Bridgeport and towns of Trumbull, Easton, Weston, and Westport along the Gold Coast of Connecticut.It borders the city of Highland Park on the southeast and the city of University Park on the east.It borders the city of Ironton and lies across the Ohio River from Ashland, Kentucky.It borders the City of Leeds, and the City of Bradford, districts of West Yorkshire.It borders the city of Liverpool to the west.It borders the city of Philadelphia.It borders the City of Philadelphia along Cheltenham Avenue.
