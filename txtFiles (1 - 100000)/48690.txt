Klaus Peter Robert Cadsky (born 3 August 1937 in Hannover; died 11 March 2011 in Solothurn), known as Nico, was a Swiss German cartoonist.Klaus-Peter Schneider (born 9 August 1964) is a retired West German javelin thrower.Klaus Petersen (born 18 February 1951) is a Danish modern pentathlete.Klaus-Peter Siegloch (born May 15, 1946 in Hamburg) is a former German journalist and lobbyist.Klaus-Peter Thaler (born 14 May 1949 in Eckmannshausen, North Rhine-Westphalia) was a professional cyclist between 1976 and 1988, successful in road-racing and cyclo-cross.Klaus pioneered the use of the electron microprobe to study meteorite samples.Klaus Pohl (born 1960 as Klaus Mussgnug in Karlsruhe ) is a German computer scientist and Professor for Software Systems Engineering at the University of Duisburg-Essen, mainly known for his work in Requirements Engineering and Software product line engineering.Klaus Porbadnik (born 24 June 1930) is a German long-distance runner.Klaus Raffeiner (born 1977) is a male badminton player from Italy.Klaus Rainer Scherer (born 1943) is Professor of Psychology and director of the Swiss Center for Affective Sciences in Geneva.
