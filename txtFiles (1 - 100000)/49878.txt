Live at the Grand Olympic Auditorium is the second live album by the American rap metal band Rage Against the Machine, released on November 25, 2003 by Epic Records.Live at the Greek Theater 2008 is a live album released by musician Ringo Starr & His All-Starr Band in 2010.Live at the Greek Theater is a live album by the band Flogging Molly, recorded at The Greek Theater in Los Angeles, CA.
