Maggie Rose Durante (born May 19, 1988) is an American country music singer.Maggie Roswell (born November 14, 1952) is an American actress from Los Angeles, California.Maggie Roswell made her acting break-through in the 1980s with appearances in films such as Midnight Madness (1980), Lost in America (1985), and Pretty in Pink (1986), and guest appearances on television shows such as Remington Steele, Masquerade, and Happy Days.Maggie Ryder is an English singer/musician, principally known as a backing singer for bands such as Eurythmics and Queen.
