Middlefield is a village in Middlefield Township, Geauga County, Ohio, United States.Middlefield Township is one of the sixteen townships of Geauga County, Ohio, United States.Middlefield Township was established in 1858.Middlefield was designed for Henry Bond, a lecturer in Roman Law at Trinity College and Trinity Hall, Cambridge.Middle Finger Salute are a punk rock band from Blackburn, England.Middle Finger U is now out of print.Middle floodplain forests are tall occasionally flooded forests.Middleford is an unincorporated community in Sussex County, Delaware, United States.
