Most of the music from Halo: Combat Evolved is present on the CD, although some songs have been remixed by O'Donnell in medley form for "more enjoyable" listening.Most of the music from the label is from artists from the 1960s and 1970s sunshine pop, psychedelic pop era.Most of the music from the original opera is adapted as bluegrass music, although the original "Prelude" and "Introduction" were performed in an orchestral arrangement by the Mandolin Society of Peterborough and recorded at the Peterborough Folk Festival.Most of the musicians are members of the Jazz at Lincoln Center Orchestra led by Wynton Marsalis.Most of the musicians signed in Utopia Records are from southern Israel.Most of the musicians that appear on this album were featured on Monkees albums in the past.Most of the musicians went on to become top session players on the Canadian music scene throughout the 1970s and 1980s working with the likes of Bruce Cockburn, David Wiffen and Murray McLauchlan.
