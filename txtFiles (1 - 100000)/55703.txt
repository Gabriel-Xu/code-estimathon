Park Jung-Bin (born 22 February 1994) is a South Korean footballer playing as an attacker for Viborg FF of the Danish Superliga in Denmark.Park Jung-bum (born 1976) is a South Korean film director, screenwriter and actor.Park Jung-chul (born Park Chul on November 5, 1976) is a South Korean actor.Park Jung-eun (born 14 January 1977) is a Korean basketball player who competed in the 1996 Summer Olympics, the 2000 Summer Olympics, the 2004 Summer Olympics, and the 2008 Summer Olympics.Park Jung-geu (born 2 November 1983) is a South Korean handball player who competed in the 2008 and 2012 Summer Olympics.
