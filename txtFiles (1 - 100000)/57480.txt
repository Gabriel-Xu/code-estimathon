Quercus rapurahuensis, the Talamanca oak, is a tropical species of oak in the red oak group (Quercus section Lobatae).Quercus robur, commonly known as common oak, pedunculate oak, European oak or English oak, is a species of flowering plant in the beech and oak family, Fagaceae.Quercus rugosa, commonly known as the netleaf oak, is a broad-leaved tree in the beech and oak family Fagaceae.Quercus sadleriana is a species of oak known by the common names Sadler's oak and deer oak.Quercus sagraeana, the Cuban oak, is a medium-sized evergreen tree native to western Cuba in the Cuban pine forests ecoregion.Quercus setulosa is the accepted name of a tree species in the Asian sub-genus of 'ring-cupped oaks' and the family Fagaceae.Quercus shumardii, the Shumard oak, spotted oak, Schneck oak, Shumard red oak, or swamp red oak, is one of the largest of the oak species in the red oak group (Quercus section Lobatae).
