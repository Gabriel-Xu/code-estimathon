Salivary duct carcinoma, abbreviated SDC, is a rare type of aggressive cancer that arises from the salivary glands.Salivary duct stricture (also termed salivary duct stenosis) is narrowing of the duct of a major salivary gland.Salivary gland aplasia (also termed salivary gland agenesis) is the congenital absence of salivary glands.Salivary gland atresia is congenital blockage or absence of the orifice of a major salivary gland duct or part of the duct itself.Salivary gland diseases (SGD) are multiple and varied in cause.Salivary gland fistulae are almost always related to the parotid gland or duct, although the submandibular gland is rarely the origin.Salivary gland hyperplasia is hyperplasia of the terminal duct of salivary glands.Salivary glands can be classified as serous, mucous or seromucous (mixed).Salivary glands lead into the preoral chamber from both pairs of coxapophyses.Salivary gland tumors or neoplasms are tumors that form in the tissues of salivary glands.
