She taught theater studies at Columbia College in Chicago and was the artistic director of the Body Politic theater company in Chicago in the 1980s and early 1990s.She taught there from 1975 to 1982, and then worked for a time at the private Mount Scopus College.She taught there under the auspices of the YMCA Neighborhood Clubs.She taught the young Guru-to-be the value of compassion, love, kindness, bravery, humility, etc.She taught traditional Native bead working, and was certified by the Native American Arts & Crafts council.She taught voice at York University from 2000 to 2018.She taught Zhu Qiuying and Yang Zhixue in Harbin.
