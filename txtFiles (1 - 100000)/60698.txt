She was active as a political Polish agent in the court of Empress Maria Theresa in Vienna, where she was sent to influence the empress in favor of Poland.She was active as a recording artist from the late 1920s to the mid-1930s.She was active as a sculptor and modeler of mainly figurative pieces between 1920-1957.She was active as a teacher of the Dramatens elevskola from 1847 until 1884 (except for the years 1851-56).She was active at Naval Station Argentia, Newfoundland, from 1958 to 1963.She was active between 1979 and 1986, with her best performance a silver medal at the 1985 Alpine World Ski Championships in Bormio.
