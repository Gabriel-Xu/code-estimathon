The club is known as the breeding ground of New Beat.The club is known as The Bulldogs.The club is known as "the cradle of the game (lawn tennis) in the country (India)" as it nurtured many talents from the late Dilip Bose to Jaidip Mukherjee and Premjit Lal and Naresh Kumar and Akhtar Ali to Zeeshan Ali and Leander Paes to Syed Fazaluddin all of whom are Davis Cup Players for the Country of India.The club is known as "the Kangaroos".The club is known as "The Lakers" and wear red, white and black panels similar to the Saints guernsey.The club is known as 'the Pilgrims' in reference to the Pilgrim Fathers, who left England and sailed to North America and founded Boston, Massachusetts.The club is known as the 'Swans'.The club is known for developing youth players which eventually are signed by clubs in the Persian Gulf Pro League.The club is known for having hosted a huge number of now-famous artists when they were still relatively unknown, including Green Day, Death Cab for Cutie, The Flaming Lips, Blink 182, The White Stripes, Arcade Fire, Alanis Morissette, Black Rebel Motorcycle Club, Oasis, Lush, Avail, Kid Rock, Marilyn Manson, The Mighty Mighty Bosstones, Cake, Queens of the Stone Age, The Strokes, Bright Eyes, Neutral Milk Hotel, and fun.The club is known for its Futsal team, which is one of the best in Asia.The club is known for its international representation, including Swiss runners Martin Hubmann and Daniel Hubmann as well as Baptiste Rollier and veteran British runner Jon Duncan.The club is known for its political endorsements, which are often sought after by candidates in local elections; it generally supports liberal and progressive candidates in elections.The Club is known for its Youth Development Policy, and is the first Professional Football Club in India to have tied up with a major international football club in Liverpool FC along with whom they run their Academy.The club is known for playing major teams in practice games and for its "passionate" supporters, who initiated major brawls over the years.The club is known for training a number of top Russian tennis players who have gone on to win Grand Slam titles.The club is known for training some of the world's best sprinters, world champions currently training with the group include Usain Bolt and Yohan Blake.The club is known not only for its open age team but also for the younger girls' teams which wear the same uniform.
