The club's only cup final appearance came in 1956, but this match was lost 2-1 to Skeid.The club's only equipment was a single punchbag which they hung from a breadfruit tree.The club's only full season saw them have four head coaches: Geoff Copping got things underway and Erskine Douglas provided a solid structure and program for a few weeks before leaving for Chelmsford Chieftains.The club's only team withdrew from North Division 3 in 2007 due to a lack of players, but re-entered the league in 2008, however Kincraig withdrew yet again in 2009.The club's only title came in the amateur era of Guayaquil football, when it won the regional league in 1943.The club soon thereafter was relegated.The club's open age team currently compete in the top division of the National Conference League.
