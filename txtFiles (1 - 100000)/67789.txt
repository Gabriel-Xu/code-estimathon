The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship 1978 was held in Honduras.The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship 1980 was held in the United States.The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship 1982 was held in Guatemala.The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship 1986 was held in Trinidad and Tobago.The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship 1988 was held in Guatemala, although it has also been reported that the tournament was held in Trinidad and Tobago.The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship 1990 was held in Guatemala.The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship 1992 was held in Canada.The CONCACAF Under-20 Championship is the second longest running international association football event in the North America, Central America and the Caribbean region, CONCACAF, and is the qualification tournament for the FIFA U-20 World Cup.
