The episode was inspired by Don Payne, based on the area where his mother lived, where one side of town would spread rumors about the other side.The episode was inspired by Patrick Creadon's 2006 documentary Wordplay, and was written by Tim Long, and directed by Nancy Kruse.The episode was inspired by Pemberton's own chance encounter with a single shoe.The episode was inspired by the 1951 British film Scrooge, starring Alastair Sim.The episode was inspired by the Abu Ghraib detainee abuse scandal.The episode was inspired by To Catch a Predator, a series of specials of the NBC newsmagazine Dateline, which depicts the capture of pedophiles red-handed on camera after they are lured to a certain location in an online sting operation.The episode was intended to be commentary on Apartheid in South Africa, using gender role reversal.The episode was intended to make Sawyer look like less of a belligerent, as he had generally acted as the antagonist in previous episodes.The episode was intended to show guns in a neutral way, and faced some problems with the censors because of the subject matter.The episode was later adapted as part of a Level Pack for the 2015 video game Lego Dimensions.The episode was later dedicated to his memory.The episode was later nominated for an Annie Award.The episode was later nominated for a Primetime Emmy Award for Short-format Animation at the 66th Primetime Emmy Awards, but lost to the Mickey Mouse episode "O Sole Minnie".
