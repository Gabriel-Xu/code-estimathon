The game was seen as an ambitious project by a small team by the gaming media, and Murray and Hello Games drew significant attention leading to its release.The game was seen as more serious than other Maxis games.The game was self-published by its developer, Peter Hewitt, off his website and at a stall in Townville's "Cotter's Markets".The game was self-published for Microsoft Windows and macOS in October 2017, and by Humble Bundle for the PlayStation 4 and Xbox One consoles two months later.The game was sequel to Rapala Tournament Fishing and released by Activision for the Xbox 360, PlayStation 3, and Wii on September 2, 2008.The game was set in a virtual world or "Parallel Kingdom" where users claimed their territories based on their GPS location or by making friends who could invite them to travel to new places.The game was set in a world where the dead roam the earth eating the flesh of the living.
