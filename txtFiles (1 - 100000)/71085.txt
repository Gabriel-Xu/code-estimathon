The Grand Prix de Oriente (Grand Prix San Miguel) is an elite women's professional one-day road bicycle race held in El Salvador.The Grand Prix de Plumelec-Morbihan Dames is an elite women's professional one-day road bicycle race held in France and is currently rated by the UCI as a 1.2 race.The Grand Prix de Ponce is an auto race held at the Ponce Speedway in the El Tuque sector of barrio Canas, Ponce, Puerto Rico.The Grand Prix der Volksmusik (English: Grand Prix of Folk Music) was an annual regional song contest for folk music, held from 1986 until 2010.The Grand prix des lectrices de Elle is a French literary prize awarded by readers of the Elle magazine.The Grand Prix des Marbriers is a road bicycle race held annually in France.The Grand Prix de Wallonie is an annual road bicycle race held annually in Wallonia, Belgium.The Grand Prix Drivers' Association (GPDA), is the trade union of Formula One drivers.
