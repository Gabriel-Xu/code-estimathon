The London Victory Celebrations of 1946 were British Commonwealth, Empire and Allied victory celebrations held after the defeat of Nazi Germany and Japan in World War II.The London Victory Parade of 1982 was a British victory parade held after the defeat of Argentina in the Falklands War and organized by the City of London as a salute to the task force.The London Wall was the defensive wall first built by the Romans around Londinium, their strategically important port town on the River Thames in what is now London, England, and subsequently maintained until the 18th century.The London War Cup was an English football competition held with the aim of helping to fill the gaping hole left in English football by the FA Cup's cancelation.The London Warriors are a British American Football club based in Thornton Heath, London and currently play in the BAFA Premier Division South for the 2016 season.The London was a 76-gun second-rate ship of the line of the English Royal Navy, originally built for the navy of the Commonwealth of England at Chatham by Captain John Taylor, and launched in June 1656.The London was accidentally blown up in 1665 and sank in the Thames Estuary.
