The Saga Continues is the second and last album by the Bloods & Crips.The Saga Continues was mixed by Josh Gannet.The Saga Continues..., with the record labels Bad Boy Records and Arista Records.The saga describes his youth in Geldrop, his student days in Nijmegen and his life afterward in Amsterdam in the seventies and eighties.The saga ended with two of the hostages escaping and a third being rescued, Palczynski being shot to death by Baltimore County Police, and a woman being sentenced to prison for purchasing the weapons for him.The saga ends some generations later in 1890, with the death of Gideon Kent in The Americans.
