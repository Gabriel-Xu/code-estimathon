The song was Minogue's first commercial single since she was diagnosed with breast cancer in May 2005.The song was minor success peaking at #63 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Singles & Tracks.The song was mistaken to be the album's third single, after a music video of the song was released on the band's Myspace on May 20, 2009.The song was mixed at Metalworks Studios in Mississauga, Ontario.The song was mixed by co-producer Bob Clearmountain in January 1987 at AIR Studios in London.The song was mixed by Delbert Bowers and Morten Pilegaard.The song was mixed by Dr. Dre.The song was mixed by Dr. Dre and charted at number one on the US Billboard Hot 100 chart.The song was mixed by Kevin "KD" Davis.The song was mixed by Lee Sunghoo of SM Yellow Tail Studio and it was originally published by Orec Songs, with the support of BMG Music Publishing Korea, the original title of the song being "Life Couldn't Get Better."
