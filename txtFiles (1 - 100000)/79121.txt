The specific name stadelmani honors Raymond Edward Stadelman, curator at the Tela serpentarium and naturalist at the Museum of Comparative Zoology.The specific name stanleyi is in honor of explorer Henry Morton Stanley.The specific name stejnegeri honors Leonhard Hess Stejneger, a Norwegian-American herpetologist.The specific name, stenophrys, is derived from the Greek words stenos, meaning "narrow", and ophrys, meaning "brow" or "eyebrow".The specific name Sturanyella is apparently in honor of Rudolf Sturany (1867-1935), a malacologist from Austria.The specific name subvoce is Latin for "under voice" and refers to the very subdued male advertisement call of this frog.The specific name sungariensis comes from the name of the Sungari River, where this species was found.The specific name, taenia, is derived from the Greek word, tainia, meaning ribbon bandage or stripe, in reference to the slender body.The specific name tanganyicensis is derived from the name of Lake Tanganika.The specific name tankervillii is in honor of Charles Bennet, 4th Earl of Tankerville.The specific name taraiensis is derived from the noun "Tarai".The specific name "tealei" is in honor of Roy Teale, the malacologist who collected the holotype of this species.The specific name thomsoni is in honor of explorer Joseph Thomson.The specific name tigrina is from Latin word "tigrinus", that means "tiger-like", referring to the predatory nature of the slug.The specific name tigris, Latin for "tiger", refers to the many narrow dorsal crossbands, which create a pattern of vertical stripes when viewed from the side.The specific name togianensis is after the type locality Togian Island.The specific name tominangensis is named after the Tominanga River, where it lives.The specific name toradjarum is in honor of Toraja (spelling variant Toradja), an ethnic group indigenous to Sulawesi.The specific name toruputuensis is apparently according to its type locality, Toruputu Peak.The specific name towutensis is according to the lake Towuti.The specific name tridentata refers to its three-toothed leaves.The specific name tyleri honors Michael J. Tyler, Australian herpetologist who have worked extensively with Australian and New Guinean frogs.The specific name unicolor refers to the uniformly colored dorsal side of the body.The specific name uno refers to uniqueness of this species among its relatives (at the time of description, genus Eleutherodactylus).The specific name utsunomiyaorum honors Taeko and Yasuaki Utsunomiya for their contributions to clarifying the amphibian fauna of the Yaeyama Group.The specific name wadei honors Edward O.Z. Wade, an English illustrator and herpetology enthusiast who drew some of the illustrations accompanying the species description.The specific name waliesa is derived from the Dobu word waliesa that means "namesake", in honor of Fred Malesa from Fergusson Island.The specific name was given because it is the smallest member of the tribe Diplothelopsini.The specific name was given to honor the people of Bang Tawa village, the type locality of the species.The specific name was named after the Jiufotang Formation, in which the holotype was collected.The specific name was reused by Linnaeus due to Cornut's confusion with a species from Asia Minor.The specific name "wilsoni" is apparently in honor of U.K./Australian malacologist John Bracebridge Wilson (1828-1895), who collected the type specimen.The specific name "woleuensis" is derived from the Woleu River, where the specimens were originally collected.The specific name zeteki honors James Zetek, an American entomologist who worked in Panama.
