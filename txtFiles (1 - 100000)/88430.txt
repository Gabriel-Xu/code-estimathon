Authorities are investigating whether Hoffman had prescriptions for these drugs, the sources said.Timeline of Hoffman's final days, hoursThe law enforcement sources told CNN that investigators have begun to piece together where Hoffman was in the days before his death by talking to his family and friends.An assistant for Hoffman told police she saw him Friday at his apartment and there was nothing out of the ordinary. He also seemed fine when she spoke to him on Saturday at about 1:30 p.m. ET.But Mimi O'Donnell, Hoffman's ex-partner, told authorities that when she saw him near his apartment at 2 p.m. Saturday she thought he appeared to be high.
