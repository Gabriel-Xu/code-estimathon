The whole structure, including the base, is about 4 1/2 feet tall, and the arm and apple statue is about 18 inches long.Lex Manticore of Chicago, a leader and spokesman for the group, said the arm represents that of Eve in the Biblical story of the Garden of Eden, where Adam and Eve eat the forbidden fruit from the tree of knowledge."We see Satan as a hero in that story, of course, spreading knowledge," Manticore said.He called the pursuit of knowledge "the greatest individual pursuit of bettering yourself, and we believe that you should basically act with the best scientific understanding of the world when you make decisions."
