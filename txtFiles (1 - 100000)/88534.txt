The pay settlement announced by the minister last Friday afternoon falls far short of what the unions wanted.It sets out a 1% rise for the 60% of health care staff who are at the top of their pay band.That rise is non-consolidated, which means it is effectively a one-off bonus which won't necessarily be added into next year's pay.The 1% payment will equate to around £300 for most, with a maximum of £985.The other 40% of staff who aren't at the top of their pay band, will get their normal annual incremental rise.This will on average be 3.7% rise which will equate to a minimum of £1,588.The unions want the 1% rise to be consolidated, meaning it would increase annual salaries and wouldn't be a one off payment.
