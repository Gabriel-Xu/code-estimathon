A British man fell to his death on the last day of a holiday in Spain as he tried to get into his apartment after locking himself out. Father-of-four Tyrone Jones, 42, had lost his key and tried to get into the Benidorm flat by stepping on to a wall, unaware of a four-metre drop on the other side. The top bricks of the wall were loose and he toppled over, suffering fatal head wounds. His grieving relatives believe he lay on the ground for up to five hours before he was seen.
