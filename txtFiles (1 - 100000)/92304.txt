When Richard Dimbleby died of cancer in 1965, his two sons vowed to ensure his legacy would live on. Now David and Jonathan Dimbleby have donated £2million to an innovative cancer centre, to be built in honour of the distinguished broadcaster. The centre, which will accommodate a range of leading services, is under construction at Guy’s Hospital in London and is due to open in 2016. Brothers Jonathan, left, and David, right, Dimbleby have donated £2million to an innovative cancer centre, to be built in honour of the distinguished broadcaster father Richard Dimbleby . The distinguished broadcaster Richard Dimbleby died of cancer in 1965 . ‘There was a great outpouring of public grief at the time.
