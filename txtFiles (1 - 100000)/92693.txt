Some of his co-workers ran to the glass panels that surround the building’s atrium to see what was going on. Birkenbuel then barricaded them in the office for safety. And they waited.Mark Vandroff, a retired Navy captain, had arrived by Metro from his home in Bethesda at 5:50 a.m. He had already worked out in the Navy Yard’s gym, showered and read through his e-mails.
