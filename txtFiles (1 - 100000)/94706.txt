After a brief chase Mr Fox managed to catch the animal before releasing it back outside, ending its 48 hour reign of fear in the flat in Chartham, Kent. Together they launched “Prairie Home” on July 6, 1974, with a live broadcast from Macalester College witnessed by a handful of people.The show began airing nationwide in 1980 and grew into a hit that fueled MPR’s growth into one of the nation’s largest public radio operations.
