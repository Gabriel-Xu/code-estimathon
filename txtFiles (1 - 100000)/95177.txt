Teachers, social workers and NHS staff should be legally required to give police all allegations of child sex abuse, a report into the Jimmy Savile scandal will demand today. The report was commissioned by police and is based on interviews with more than two dozen of Savile’s victims. They told interviewers that professionals who come into contact with children should be required to pass on any accusations handed to them. Currently Britain lacks a ‘mandatory reporting’ law – meaning people who work with children can go unpunished if they fail to report abuse. Such laws are common in parts of the US.
