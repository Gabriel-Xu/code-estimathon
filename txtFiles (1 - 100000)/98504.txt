The mother-of-three stole the money when she worked for Way Ahead Housing, which merged into charity Independent People, Bristol Crown Court heard. Caught out: Samantha Wilding, pictured outside Bristol Crown Court, admitted stealing more than £350,000 from the homeless charity she worked at as a manager . Audacious: Wilding spent the cash on renovations to her home and trips to Marks and Spencer, Bristol Crown Court, pictured, heard .
