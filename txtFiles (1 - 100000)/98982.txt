"We write to address the misdemeanor charge recently filed by the U.S. attorney," the restaurant said in a statement posted on its Web site. The restaurant pledged to set up procedures to ensure that it complies with laws and "becomes a good corporate citizen." "We sincerely apologize. There will be minor changes to bring civil partnerships and marriages into line in the remaining areas of legal difference between them.
