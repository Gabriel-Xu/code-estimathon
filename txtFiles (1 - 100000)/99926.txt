Meanwhile, Viktor Mayer-Schönberger, Professor of Internet Governance at the University of Oxford, suggests that being able to see the online profile of people who have hurt us could actually hinder our ability to forgive. He explained: 'Psychologists tell us that forgetting and forgiving are intertwined — as we forgive somebody, our brain depreciates the memory of wrongdoing because it is no longer relevant to how we judge that person. Forgiving without forgetting is very difficult.'
