In certain cases, the receiver can correct the error, without the need for a retransmission.FEC adds redundancy to the transmitted information with a known algorithm. Each redundant bit is a function of many original information bits. The original information may or may not appear in the encoded output; codes that include the unmodified input in the output are systematic, while those that do not are nonsystematic.An extremely simple example would be an analog to digital converter that samples three bits of signal strength data for every bit of transmitted data.
