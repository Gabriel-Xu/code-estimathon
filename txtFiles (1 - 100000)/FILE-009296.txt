Priscilla Opoku-Kwarteng (16 February 1997 – 8 February 2018), known by the stage name Ebony, was a Ghanaian dancehall/Afrobeats artist widely known for her hit songs "Poison" and "Kupe". She was discovered by Bullet, a member of Ruff n Smooth fame.
