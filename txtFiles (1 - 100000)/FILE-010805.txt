Campbell's Soup Cans, which is sometimes referred to as 32 Campbell's Soup Cans, is a work of art produced between November 1961 and March or April 1962 by Andy Warhol. It is made up of thirty-two canvases, each measuring in height × . Each are of a painting of a Campbell's Soup can—one of each of the canned soup versions the company offered at the time.
