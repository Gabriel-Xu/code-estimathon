For him, that meant the end of the goalkeeper career.Ferdinand Lube, the sports club youth leader at the time, who wrote sports reports for the "Kleine Zeitung", suggested Edi Finger, who was working for the Mining Union at the time, as his successor. Finger, who went there to apply, found himself surrounded by unemployed actors, older journalist colleagues and war veterans .
