We are thelads to hunt him up his whale!ALL. Aye! aye!OLD MANX SAILOR. How the three pines shake! Pines are the hardest sortof tree to live when shifted to any other soil, and here there’s nonebut the crew’s cursed clay. Steady, helmsman! steady. This is the sortof weather when brave hearts snap ashore, and keeled hulls split atsea. Our captain has his birthmark; look yonder, boys, there’s anotherin the sky—lurid-like, ye see, all else pitch black.DAGGOO. What of that? Who’s afraid of black’s afraid of me!
