AGROVOC (a portmanteau of agriculture and vocabulary) is a multilingual controlled vocabulary covering all areas of interest to the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), including food, nutrition, agriculture, fisheries, forestry and the environment.AGROVOC is made available by FAO as an RDF/SKOS-XL concept scheme and published as a linked data set aligned to 18 other vocabularies.A growbag is a large plastic bag filled with a growing medium and used for growing plants, usually tomatoes or other salad crops.A grow box is a partially or completely enclosed system for raising plants indoors or in small areas.AgroWeb Network collaborates with the European System of Cooperative Research Networks in Agriculture.AgroWeb Network has twenty seven member countries.AgroWeb Network is maintained by institutions and individuals operating in their respective countries.A grow house is a property, usually located in a suburban residential neighborhood, that is primarily used for the production of marijuana, it may be used for the production of other plants such as Psilocybin mushrooms.
