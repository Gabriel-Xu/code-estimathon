He has competed in Canadian and international wrestling promotions including the eastern Canadian Grand Prix Wrestling, the Calgary-based Stampede Wrestling, New Japan Pro Wrestling in Japan and World Wrestling Council in Puerto Rico.He has competed in competitions such as the Arab Athletics Championships, Asian Games, IAAF World Half Marathon Championships and the IAAF World Cross Country Championships, before 2013 for Kenya and after that for Bahrain.He has competed in EliteXC, ShoXC, and Bellator, and is the former Bellator Bantamweight World Champion.He has competed in events like Monte Carlo Rally, Coupe des Alpes, 24 Hours of Le Mans and Acropolis Rally.He has competed in events such as the 2006 Commonwealth Games, the 2005 and 2006 World Triathlon Championships and many ITU World Cup races.He has competed in every Paralympics that included his sport, representing Canada five times.He has competed in every Summer Paralympic Games from 1976 to 2008, winning a total of fourteen gold, six silver, and two bronze medals.He has competed in five consecutive editions of the 24 Hours of Le Mans race, from 2007 until 2011, with a best result of seventh overall and second in class in the 2010 event.He has competed in five Paralympic Games winning gold medals in three consecutive Games.He has competed in four NASCAR Winston Cup Series (now Monster Energy NASCAR Cup Series) races, all at Sears Point Raceway (now Sonoma Raceway), most recently in 1996.He has competed in four United States Men's Curling Championships.He has competed in four World Bowls Championships in 2000, 2008, 2012 and 2016.He has competed in Freestyle Relay and Men's 200m Freestyle.He has competed in ice sledge hockey at the 2006 Winter Paralympics, in Sitting volleyball at the 2007 European Championships, and is credited with inventing the sport of Inline sledge hockey.He has competed in IMSA GT, sprint car racing, midget car racing, and the Trans Am Series.
