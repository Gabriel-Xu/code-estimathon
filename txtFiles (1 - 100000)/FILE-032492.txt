He is a former member of the group Toolshed (with Chokeules, Psyborg and Selfhelp), and a member of the groups Wolves (with Bix, D-Sisive, Ghettosocks and Muneshine), Swamp Thing (with Chokeules and Savillion) and My Giants (with Ambition and Uncle Fester).He is a former member of the group Toolshed (with Timbuktu, and Psyborg), and a member of the group Swamp Thing (with Timbuktu and Savillion).He is a former member of the Hannaford Street Silver Band with whom he performed, composed, and recorded for more than 20 years.He is a former member of the Henrico County School Board.He is a former member of the House of Commons of Canada, representing the riding of Miramichi, New Brunswick from 1993 to 2008.He is a former member of the House of Commons of Canada who represented the riding of Mississauga South for the Liberal Party from 1993 to 2011.He is a former member of the House of Representatives for GreenLeft.He is a former member of the Inatsisartut and one of the founders of the Greenlandic political party Inuit Ataqatigiit.He is a former member of the Indonesia national team.He is a former member of the International Law Commission of the United Nations; forty-ninth session (1997 to fifty-eighth session) (2006).He is a former member of the Internet Architecture Board, a past chair of numerous Working Groups of the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF), the inventor of IP Multicast, and the lead designer of the new version of the Internet Protocol, IPv6.He is a former member of the Karnataka Legislative Assembly and a former minister in the Government of Karnataka.He is a former member of the Karnataka Legislative Assembly representing the Channapatna constituency.
