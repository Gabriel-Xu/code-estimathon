He participated in the 2002 European Championship for his country, and in one match in the C&G Trophy competition of 2005.He participated in the 2002 Salt Lake City and 2010 Vancouver Winter Olympics as the chief referee in the short track speed skating events.He participated in the 2003 Cricket World Cup, and made an impressive debut in which he disposed of South Africans Herschelle Gibbs and Jacques Kallis.He participated in the 2003 World Youth Championships in Athletics, winning a silver in the 400 meters.He participated in the 2004 and 2006 Under-19 World Cups for Ireland.He participated in the 2004, and 2008 Summer Olympics.He participated in the 2004 Athens Games, but did not win any medals at that competition.He participated in the 2004 Summer Olympics for his native North African country.He participated in the 2004 Summer Olympics in Athens, Greece.He participated in the 2004 Tour de France and finished in 119th.He participated in the 2006 Olympics, finishing 10th in the first run, but failing to finish the second.He participated in the 2006 Tour de France and finished in 131st place.He participated in the 2006 Whitney Biennial.He participated in the 2006 World Championship finishing tied for ninth.He participated in the 2007 comedy show The Great Indian Laughter Challenge III in 2007.He participated in the 2007 Illinois Shrine Bowl.He participated in the 2008 Summer Olympics and was choked unconscious with a clock choke by Sherali Bozorov in the preliminaries.He participated in the 2008 Summer Olympics, both individually and with the Canadian Men's Team.He participated in the 2008 Summer Olympics, where he was placed 7th overall in the synchronized 3m springboard event with his diving partner Ben Swain.He participated in the 2008 Washington Conference as a side event moderator and is a member of the group organizing the 2010 Delhi Conference.He participated in the 2008 Whitney Biennial.He participated in the 2009 Copenhagen Conference where he chaired a joint side event with the International Renewable Energy Alliance (REN Alliance) and the newly formed International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA).He participated in the 2010, 2014 and 2018 Winter Paralympics and won 8 medals in total, including gold in the men's 15km biathlon standing at the 2018 games.He participated in the 2010 "Hollywood Calling" film scoring workshop with Christopher Young, the 2010 Rick Baitz "Composing for the Screen" Film Scoring Workshop, and the 2009 "The Art of Orchestration" Workshop with Steven Scott Smalley.
