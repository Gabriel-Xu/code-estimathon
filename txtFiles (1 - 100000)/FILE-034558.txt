He was a member of the Missouri House of Representatives, serving from 2000 to 2008 and 1988 to 1992.He was a member of the MIT Corporation and, until September 2017, the Board of ISO New England.He was a member of the MLB Safety and Health Advisory Committee and served as Chairman of the MLB Rules and Administration Committee during his tenure as the Brewers' general manager.He was a member of the Modest Jazz Trio with Red Mitchell and Jim Hall, who recorded an album in 1960, and worked with Harry James for most of the 1960s.He was a member of the Molecular Biology Institute, a division of University of California, Los Angeles.
