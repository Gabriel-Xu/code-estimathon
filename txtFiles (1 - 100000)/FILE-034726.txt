He was chairman or director of many business in the area including Cammell Laird shipbuilders, Brodsworth Colliery Staverly Coal and Iron Company and Leeds Forge Company Ltd and many more.He was Chairman, President, and CEO of UAL Corporation from 2002-2010.He was chairman, president and chief executive of the Fortune 500 company Columbia Energy Group; chairman, president and chief executive of the Fortune 1000 company New Jersey Resources; president and chief executive of Northern Natural Gas Pipeline; and vice president of Tenngasco.He was chairman, Security and Intelligence Committee of Ayodele Fayose Campaign Organization for the 21 June 2014 gubernatorial election in which Ayodele Fayose emerged as winner in all the sixteen local government in the state.He was chairman until 2015.He was chairman until his death in 2004, when the theater also subsequently closed down.He was chair of ACM SIGPLAN and founding chair of ACM SIGecom.He was Chair of Alder Hey Children's NHS Foundation Trust from 2011 to 2014, after chairing NHS North West.He was chair of an Arts venue in Stratford Upon Avon from 2012 to 2016, The Stratford ArtsHouse.He was chair of both the Michigan Legislative Black Caucus and Detroit Caucus.He was chair of BYU's Department of Spanish and Portuguese from 1993-1997.He was Chair of Committees from 1982 to 1989 but did not hold ministerial office.He was Chair of Governors at King's Ford Junior School in Colchester, he was followed into this lofty position by Bruce Tuxford.He was Chair of Hydro Tasmania from September 2004 until his resignation in 2014.He was chair of Morocco's local government association and vice president of the African branch of the United Cities and Local Governments organization.
