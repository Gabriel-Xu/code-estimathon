He was collated, inducted and installed Dean of Portsmouth at Portsmouth Cathedral on 16 November 2002.He was collated on 28 October 2015.He was collector from 1897 until 1911.He was collector of the subsidy for Plympton Erle in 1523.He was college roommates with Bill Morrissey, who encouraged him to perform his music in public, and co-wrote the song "Marigold Hall" with Morrissey.He was college roommates with Rob Cummings aka Rob Zombie at Parson School of Design in New York City during the 1980s.He was College Sailor of the Year in 1977.He was College Sailor of the Year in 1983 and 1984.He was Collingwood fan as a child.He was Collingwood's back pocket player in the 1910 Grand Final win over Carlton.
