He was the heir presumptive to the headship of the deposed House of Hohenzollern from the death of his father to 20 January 2013, when Georg Friedrich fathered twin sons, relegating Christian-Sigismund to a more remote place in the Line of succession to the former German throne.He was the heir to the throne during the reign of the pharaoh Ay though he never became a pharaoh.He was the heir to The Vyne estate near Basingstoke, Hampshire.He was the helmsman of the French boat Gwendoline 2, which won a bronze medal in the first race of the 2 to 3 ton class.He was the hereditary zamindar of Pappanad in Tanjore district.He was the Hereford Diocesan Ecumenical Secretary from 1969 to 1983; a Prebendary of Hereford Cathedral from 1979 to 1997; and a Canon Residentiary there from 1991 until 1997.He was the Herman Dana Professor of Medicine at Harvard Medical School and the director of the Harvard-Thorndike Electrophysiology Institute and Arrhythmia Service.He was the hero in the final, his partnership with Ian Bradshaw saw them home at The Oval.He was the High Commissioner for Bangladesh to Australia from 2008 to 2014.He was the High Commissioner to Ceylon from 1943 to July 1947.He was the highest compensated pick for the Indians in the 2006 draft agreeing to terms on $1,000,000 contract.He was the highest goalscorer for JK Narva Trans in 2013.He was the highest goal scorer in the 2016 CAF champions league with 9goals and was all scored in the group stage of the competition.He was the highest goalscorer in the nation and also recorded a record 5 hat-tricks in a single season.He was the highest in rank of the seven U.S. military police personnel who have been charged with torturing prisoners at Abu Ghraib prison in Iraq.He was the highest paid footballer in I-league earning INR 1.4 crore per year in 2011.He was the highest-paid university president in the United States, receiving $897,786 in 2004, and regained the title in 2007.He was the highest performing British athlete at the 1968 Summer Paralympics in Tel Aviv, winning two golds and a silver.He was the highest ranked Irish tennis player during his career.He was the highest-ranking African American in the American Expeditionary Forces at the end of World War I, serving as a lieutenant colonel in the 370th Infantry Regiment.He was the highest-ranking elected official in state history ever to work undercover for the FBI.He was the highest ranking elected official of the newly formed party at the time.He was the highest ranking Jewish American (Lt. Col.) to serve in the American Revolutionary Army.He was the highest-ranking officer of foreign birth in the British Army at that time.He was the highest-ranking uniformed attorney in the Air Force, and led over 4400 uniformed and civilian lawyers, paralegals and legal support personnel in the United States Air Force.He was the highest-rated European two-year-old of 1985 when he won the William Hill Futurity and the Chesham Stakes as well as finishing second in the Dewhurst Stakes.He was the highest-rated French two-year-old of 1994, when his wins included the Prix de la Salamandre and the Dewhurst Stakes.He was the highest-rated two-year-old in the 2002 European flat racing season and won the Cartier Racing Award for European Champion Sprinter in 2003.He was the highest-rated two-year-old trained in France in 1974, when he was unbeaten in three race including the Prix des Chenes and the Grand Criterium.He was the highest scoring batsman during the Test series, and also took the highest number of wickets for Sri Lanka in the ODI series.He was the highest wicket taker for the USA in Italy in Div 4 and in Malaysia in Div 3.He was the highly popular mayor of Bergen from 2003 to 2007.Before the 2003 election, a survey found that 64% of the inhabitants of Bergen wanted him to become the mayor after the election.He was the High Performance Director for Rowing New Zealand from 2008 to 2018.He was the hitting coach for the Chattanooga Lookouts in 2013.He was the hitting coach for the Cleveland Indians from 2005 until 2009, and the Tampa Bay Rays from 2010 until 2016.
