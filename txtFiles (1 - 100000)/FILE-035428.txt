Hilary Krzysztofiak (born 28 October 1926 in Szopienice, now a part of Katowice, died 30 September 1979 in Falls Church, Virginia near Washington) was a Polish painter, graphic artist and set designer.Hilary "Larry" Angelo Gomes (born 13 July 1953) is a former Trinidad & Tobago and West Indian cricketer.Hilary Lawson is an English post-realist philosopher.Hilary Liftin is an American author, born in New York City in 1969.Hilary Little is Professor of Addiction Science at St George's, University of London and the Institute of Psychiatry.
