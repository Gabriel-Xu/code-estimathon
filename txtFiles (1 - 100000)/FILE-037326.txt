League.In 1967, he co-founded the original ABA and the Indiana Pacers franchise, serving for two years as President of the ABA Board of Trustees.In 1967, he contested as the ALP candidate for the seat of Rodney but was not elected.In 1967 he co-wrote and directed the feature film Waiting for Caroline, as a commemoration of Canada's centennial year.In 1967 he created the master plan for the Metropolitan Museum of Art, and since then has designed all of the new wings and installation of many collections including the recently reopened American and Islamic wings.In 1967 he designed the directed three movies, the comedy I Do Not Ever Say Goodbye, the spy film Danger!In 1967, he formed jazz record label BYG Records, which collapsed in the mid-1970s.In 1967 he fought one professional bout and won.In 1967, he founded a hedge fund, Steinhardt Partners, that averaged an annualized return for its clients of 24.5% from 1967 to 1978.In 1967 he founded Gary Walker and the Rain, which consisted of Joey Molland (guitar and vocals); Charles "Paul" Crane (lead vocals, guitar); and John Lawson (bass guitar).In 1967 he founded one of the first vintage motor clubs in Austria and for a short period he was president of the Austrian Vintage Club Association in 1976.In 1967 he founded the Arab Music Company (renamed the Abdel Halim Nowera Ensemble for Arabic Music after his death), a the National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR) as a staff scientist and attained Senior Scientist status in 1973.In 1967, he led the Bermuda national football team to a silver medal at the Pan
