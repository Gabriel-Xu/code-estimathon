In 2000, she joined the firm of Peter Pennoyer Architects in New York City and has since collaborated with Peter Pennoyer on several books regarding the history of twentieth-century American architecture.In 2000 she joined the Konstantin Tachkin` Saint Petersburg Ballet Theater, becoming a principal dancer in the 2003/04 season.In 2000, she launched All About Buford, an award-winning pop-funk band that seemed to comfortably fit in that funny niche between a cappella and folk.In 2000, she launched a second series, Anything I Can Do, a workshop show.In 2000 she left the group, citing personal differences, and decided to pursue a solo career.In 2000, she made a cameo appearance in the music video for Blink-182's single "Josie".In 2000, she made a failed attempt to make the U. S. Olympic team in the marathon, then disappeared from the scene.In 2000 she moved to Australia and was a research fellow at ANU before she joined the University of Tasmania as an academic staff member in 2007.In 2000 she moved to Queen Mary, University of London, where she is Professor of Cancer Biology and Director of the Center for Translational Oncology in the Cancer Research UK Clinical Cancer Center.
