He was made Lord Brechin and Navar at the same time, also in the Peerage of Scotland.It was created in 1647 for Charles Lambart, 2nd Baron Lambart.It was created in 1647 for Sir John Hamilton, 2nd for Thomas Ruthven.It was created in 1658 for John Berkeley, 1st Baron Berkeley of Stratton, a Royalist supporter during the English Civil War, of the Bruton branch of the Berkeley family.It was
