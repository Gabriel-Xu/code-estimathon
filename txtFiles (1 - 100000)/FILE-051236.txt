McClain (formerly 3McClainGirls and McClain Sisters) is an American pop and R&B girl group.McClain graduated from the U.S. Naval Test Pilot School at Naval Air Station Patuxent River in June 2013 at the same time she was selected as a NASA astronaut.McClain has opened for the Commodores and James Brown.McClain has said that she would jump at the chance of going to Mars.McClain has since been a backing vocalist for Denise Williams, Hall and Oates, George Clinton and Linda Ronstadt.McClain has spent most of his professional career bouncing around the minor leagues for numerous Major League Baseball organizations.McClain is an unincorporated community in Jackson County, West Virginia.McClain later married producer Skip Scarborough and continued working in the music industry.McClain played for the Atlanta Falcons of the National Football League from 1976 through 1980.McClain played the bass guitar on the band's early albums during the 1980s and on Poletown in 1997.
