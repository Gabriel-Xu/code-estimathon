Pupils come from as far away as the village of Ashland to the west, where the defunct Ashland High School, closed in 1981.Pupils do not only come from Bad Harzburg, but also from the city of Vienenburg and Goslar (and the surrounding area).Pupils during the school's brief existence included two future prime ministers, William McMahon and Harold Holt.Pupils enter Fairlands from first schools in Cheddar and the nearby villages of Draycott, Shipham and Axbridge.Pupils enter Year 9 primarily from three catchment middle schools in Bury St Edmunds but pupils are drawn widely from across the villages and towns of West Suffolk.Pupils follow a curriculum based on National Curriculum core subjects together with activities that offer education for pupils having special moderate to severe learning difficulties that include a wide range of disabilities from autism, behavioral, emotional and social problems.Pupils follow the National Curriculum.Pupils from Alvechurch Middle School tend to feed into either South Bromsgrove High School or North Bromsgrove High School.Pupils from Bristol also attend the school.Pupils from Helston Community College take part in the annual Furry Dance, and ancient Cornish custom which is held on the Feast of St. Michael.Pupils from Lillyhall, Moorclose, Newlands School, either went to Southfield or Stainburn School.Pupils from New Farm Primary School, Silverwood Primary School and Onthank Primary School would have normally transitioned at the end of Primary 7 to become pupils of S1 in James Hamilton Academy.Pupils from St John's Middle School tend to feed into either South Bromsgrove High School or North Bromsgrove High School.Pupils from the school were chosen to form part of a "guard of honor" for athletes at the opening ceremony of the 2012 Olympic Games, displaying artistic creations their school made to celebrate the event.Pupils from Upper Sprotbrough who previously attended Outwood (formerly Adwick Secondary School), are now in the catchment area of Ridgewood.Pupils gain entry to the full-time school through auditions in drama, dance, singing and a personal interview.Pupils generally go on to other independent schools around the county, mainly the nearby Sherborne School and Sherborne Girls as well as other schools in South West England.Pupils had to walk between the lower and upper schools for various lessons until they reached their 4th year (reach the age of 14).Pupils in each year were split between four 'houses'; Castle (Yellow), Abbey (Red), Priory (Blue), and Minster (Green).Pupils in England and Wales are able to undertake BTEC and OCR national certificates and diplomas instead.
