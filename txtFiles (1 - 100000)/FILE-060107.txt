She has been a winner of several literary prizes for science fiction, including the Prix Aurora Award.She has been a Woodrow Wilson Fellow, a Fellow of the National Endowment for the Humanities and the National Humanities Center, and has received a Guggenheim Fellowship.She has been a World Bank Robert McNamara fellow, a Fulbright senior scholar at New York University, a visiting professor at the Ecole des Hautes Etudes en Sciences Sociales in Paris, and more recently British Academy Fellow at St Antony's College, Oxford.She has been a writer for Late Night with Seth Meyers since 2014.She has been a writer for the entirety her professional life.She has been a Writer in Residence at Georgia College & State University, Milledgeville, GA; Armstrong Atlantic State University, Savannah, GA; Pitzer College, Claremont, CA; and The National Writer's Voice, Tampa.She has been based in Canberra as a political correspondent since 1998.She has been based in Germany since 2007, where she is coached by Jonny Hilberath.She has been based in Havana the last two fall semesters (2003 and 2004) as Director of SLC in Cuba.She has been based in London for most of her career.She has been based in Melbourne, Australia since 2015.She has been based in New Orleans since 1976.She has been based in Paris since 1989, covering a range of subjects across Europe and elsewhere.She has been beatified by the Catholic Church, the last step prior to canonization.She has been Best FIFA Women's Player finalist.She has been best known for presenting the critically acclaimed program Foreign Exchange also on PBS and the United Nations Television produced 21st Century documentary seen on CUNY cable television.
