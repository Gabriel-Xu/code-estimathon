as Wayne Shorter, Herbie Hancock, Kenny Barron, Esperanza Spalding, Terence Blanchard, Marcus Miller and Lionel Loueke.She has performed and recorded with various musicians including Richie Havens, Boz Scaggs, Al Jarreau, Christopher Cross, and Tracy Chapman.She has performed annually with the Metropolitan Opera since her debut with the company in 1989, and as of April 2011 has appeared in a Blues.She has performed as a back-up vocalist for Steely Dan on several tours and recordings.She has performed as a drummer and backup singer for the Melbourne band Empra and performed her first solo
