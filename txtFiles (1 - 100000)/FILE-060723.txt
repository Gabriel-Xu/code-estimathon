She was a member of the DSM-IV work group on Anxiety Disorders and the DSM-5 work group on Anxiety, Obsessive Compulsive Spectrum, Posttraumatic, and Dissociative Disorders, while chairing the sub-work group on Anxiety Disorders.She was a member of the Dublin team that won the All-Ireland title in 2010 and she scored two goals in the 2014 final.She was a member of the Dutch national team and played 22 matches between her debut on 5 February 2006 against Finland and her last match on 8 August 2009 against Poland.She was a member of the Dutch National Women's Team that won the gold medal at the 2007 FIVB World Grand Prix in Ningbo, PR China.
