She was very successful as a teenager, as she won the gold medal at the 2000 World Junior Championships and the silver medal at the 2002 World Junior Championships.She was very unhappy as a teenager and tried to run away when she was sixteen, but was stopped by her mother and a policeman.She was vice-captain of the England side that beat Australia in the female version of the Ashes in 2013 and 2013-4.She was vice-chairman of the BBC Trust, the governing body of the British Broadcasting Corporation, and was a member of the UK Competition Commission until its termination in April 2014.She was Vice Chair of the Board from 1996 to 1998.She was Vice Chair of the Delegation to the ACP-EU Joint Parliamentary Assembly and a member of the Employment and Social Affairs committee.She was vice chair of the health and science institute of the Kalinga-Apayao State College.She was Vice-Chancellor and Chief Executive of Staffordshire University from 1995 to 2011.King has published extensively on the Third Reich and is considered an expert on Nazi Germany.She was Vice-Chancellor and Professor of Social Relations at Keele University, and has held a number of other public appointments in the UK.She was Vice-Chancellor of the University of Western Sydney from 1998 to 2013.She was vice-president at public relations firm Mackenzie Mccheyne for eight years.She was Vice President of Global Education and Research before becoming Vice President of Global Education, Government and Health Sciences in 2006 and CEO of Sun UK in 2007.She was Vice President of R&D for Logo Computer Systems, Inc. when Apple Logo was developed and was the Director of the Atari Cambridge Research Laboratory.She was vice-president of the American Political Science Association in 2006.She was Vice President of the Confederation in 2006 and 2010 and President in 2007 and 2011.
