The album is considered to be a departure from Roxy Music's sound, because it consists entirely of cover versions, mainly of standard songs.The album is considered to be a further shift in Kid Rock's sound toward rock music, as well as solidifying his rap rock sound and shaping the redneck image he would become known for.The album is considered to be among the band's best work: according to Mark Deming of AllMusic, "Liar isn't quite the wildest or weirdest album the Jesus Lizard ever made, but it may well be the strongest, and perhaps the best."The album is considered to be a Soviet rock classics and the best LP of the band.
