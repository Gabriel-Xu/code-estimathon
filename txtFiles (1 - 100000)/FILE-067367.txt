The college offers Bachelor of Arts degrees with concentrations in Africana studies, English language and literature (religious and philosophical studies or foreign language), history, or mass communication (print and online journalism, public relations and advertising, and radio and television) and the Bachelor of Fine Arts degree with a concentration in visual and performing arts.The college offers Bachelor of Arts (General and Honors) Course.The college offers Bachelor of Divinity degrees.The college offers Bachelor of Education and Master of Education degrees.
