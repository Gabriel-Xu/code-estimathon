The GCBDP was founded in 2006, after a split in the Greater Cooch Behar People's Association (GCPA).The GC box is upstream of the TATA box and approximately 110 bases upstream from the transcription initiation site.The GCC countries, for example, are classified as developing high-income countries.The GCCF also co-ordinates its own show: the Supreme Cat Show, which is famous for being the world's largest cat show and often referred to as the feline equivalent of Crufts.The GCCF became an incorporated company on 5 November 2010.The GCCF is a member of the Cat Group and the World Cat Congress.
