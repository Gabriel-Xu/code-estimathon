and strange story-songs or character sketches.Their songs were often played on college radio stations.Their songs were previously sampled by Chumbawamba on the album Readymades and they contributed vocals to the previous album A Singsong and a Scrap.Their songs were said to have "contempt for the cliches of contemporary sexual politics" and their music has been compared to the Gang of Four and the Young Marble Giants.Their songs were utilized in various commercials, movie and television show themes.Their songs, which are sung in Spanish, are focused on Satanism, anti-Christianity, sex,
