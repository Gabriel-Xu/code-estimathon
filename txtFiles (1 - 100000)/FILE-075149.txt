The paper was one of only a few to discuss racial inequality in the islands during the highly publicized Massie Trial of 1932.An English section, called "the Bee" for its sting, was introduced in 1925 in order to appeal to Nisei who were not fluent in Japanese.The paper was one of the earliest European free newspapers, being second after Metro Sweden.The paper was one of the few Indonesian English-language dailies to survive the 1997 Asian financial crisis and currently has a circulation of about 40,000.The paper was one of the first in the world to launch an online version of the publication Nando.net in 1994.The paper was one of the first to publish political satire, especially protesting British rule, in India.The paper was originally an evening paper called the Evening Mercantile Journal.The paper was originally called the Constitutional Telegraph.
