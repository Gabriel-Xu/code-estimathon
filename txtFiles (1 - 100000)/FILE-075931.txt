The program is divided into three phases: nominations, voting, and winners announcements.The program is divided into two branches: the Writing Program (with several advanced tiers for mentorship-driven workshops) and the Executive Program, which trains students in the development and production side of the business.The program is divided into two, one-hour programs, with each hour ending with a complete sign-off.The program is driven by viewer questions and requests for help in regards to a wide range of subjects.The program is eclectic by design, intended to provoke cross-pollination of ideas and generate new thinking, in 25 or more presentations over two days.The program is educational in focus, and makes no political claims.The program is emitted on Saturdays (from 17:00 to midnight) and on Sundays (from 15:30 to 23:30 PM), covering all the football (soccer) games of the Spanish La Liga BBVA and analyzing the most important sports news of the day.
