There are three NCAA affiliated ski conferences: the Central Collegiate Ski Association, Eastern Intercollegiate Ski Association and Rocky Mountain Intercollegiate Ski Association.There are three neighboring housing projects which lie just outside the town boundaries, Craigens, Logan and Netherthird, with the former ironworks settlement of Lugar also just outside the town, contributing to a population of around 13,000 in the immediate locale.There are three newsagent Shops (one of them has a post office), and a Fish & Chip and Pizzeria take-away shop.There are three new teams participating the league: Jumbo Plastic Linoleum Giants, Hog's Breath Cafe Razorbacks and EA Regen Med.
