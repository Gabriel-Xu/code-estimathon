The station has production centers in London (Broadcasting House) and Birmingham (The Mailbox).The station has proved popular with over 320,000 passengers using the station in the 2008-9 period.The station has put together a fairly good library of music, including many doo-opp hits of the 50's.The station has received many awards for its folk music programming.The station has received many industry awards and won the Radio Academy Nations and Regions award for 'Station of the Year' in the South region in 2007 and 2008.
