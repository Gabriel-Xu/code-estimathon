The unit was drawn mostly from medical personnel from Cleveland, Ohio from University Hospitals and the Medical School of Case Western Reserve University.The unit was embodied during both world wars, fulfilling its role within the garrison, and also sending contingents overseas to more active theaters of the wars.The unit was engaged in the Central Hupei Operation during the Second Sino-Japanese War.The unit was enlisted for three years, and most members left the unit in the end of 1863.The unit was equipped with an Avro York named 'Endeavor', an Avro Anson and a Percival Proctor.The unit was equipped with the Leopard 1V from 1986 - 1992.The unit was established as the 299th Instructor-Research Shipborne Aviation Regiment of Soviet Naval Aviation in September 1976.The unit was established by the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) in 1998.The unit was established during April 1940 by the renaming of a Coastal Command unit and disbanded during October 1943.The unit was established following the merger of Riverine Group 1 and the Maritime Expeditionary Security Group 1 & 2 (MESG-1 / MESG 2) on June 1, 2012.The unit was established in 1933, taking on responsibilities of the Empire Marketing Board Film Unit.The unit was established in 1942 and dissolved in 1945, during its service it provided crucial logistical support to the Allied war effort during the Guadalcanal Campaign.The unit was established in 1947 by executive order as the Defense Signals Bureau within the Department of Defense, and underwent several name changes until its current name ASD was adopted in 2013.
