Tuttle and his wife are both avid collectors of contemporary art.Tuttle attended St Joseph's College, Nudgee and represented Queensland and Australia at schoolboy rugby in 2013.Tuttle Farm, now known as Tender Crop, has often been referred to as the oldest known family-owned farm in the United States; however, this claim has been challenged.Tuttle graduated from Sanford High School in 1970.Tuttle is a city in Kidder County, North Dakota, United States.Tuttle is a former Clemson football standout in the early 1980s.Tuttle is a partner in the Tuttle-Click Automotive Group based in Irvine, California, and the Jim Click Automotive Group based out of Tucson, Arizona.Tuttle is known for founding the conservation organization, Bat Conservation International (BCI), and his research on gray bat population ecology migration, and frog-eating bats,.Tuttle is the son of Holmes Tuttle, founder of the Southern California chain of auto dealerships and, in the 1960s and 1970s, a prominent force behind the political rise of actor Ronald Reagan.Tuttle learned to play the guitar and became an accomplished singer while at high school.Tuttle previously worked in the White House during the Reagan administration as an assistant to the President in 1982, and director of presidential personnel in 1985.Tuttle Publishing, originally the Charles E. Tuttle Company, is a book publishing company that includes Tuttle, Periplus Editions, and Journey Editions.Tuttle served as a Democratic State Senator from Maine's 3rd District, representing part of York County including his residence of Sanford.Tuttles of Tahiti is a 1942 film directed by Charles Vidor and starring Charles Laughton and Jon Hall.
