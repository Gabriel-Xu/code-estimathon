The jury deliberated almost seven hours Thursday before convicting Christopher Kenyon Simpson, of Spiro, in the death of 20-year-old Ka'loni Flynn, of Fort Smith, Arkansas. The jurors recommended the maximum sentence of life in prison without parole. Simpson is scheduled to be sentenced November 8.
