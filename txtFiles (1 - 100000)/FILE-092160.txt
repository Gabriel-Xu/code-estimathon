Portugal for example has decriminalised drug use in 2001.The report also says that any regulation of cannabis should reflect the supremacy of human rights laws.For example, someone growing a small number of cannabis plants for modest personal use, could deploy their human right to "a private and family life" to avoid prosecution.The report argues the Human Rights Act could be invoked to support the argument that the possession or purchase of small amounts of cannabis does not harm other people's rights and therefore should not be criminalised.
