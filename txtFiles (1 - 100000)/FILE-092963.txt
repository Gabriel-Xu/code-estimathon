An airport security worker has been suspended after she failed to spot a fake bomb designed to look like explosives as it passed through the X-ray machine. The guard did not detect the dummy explosive as it passed through Manchester Airport’s ‘air side’ meaning it could have been taken on board a flight by a passenger. It is believed that the woman missed the fake bomb because she was looking at her work rota at the time rather than watching the screen. Didn't detect dummy: A security worker at Manchester Airport (pictured) has been suspended after she failed to spot a fake bomb designed to look like explosives as it passed through X-ray machines . The airport confirmed that a female security officer had been suspended and an investigation launched into the incident which happened last Thursday. The fake device was carried through security in an item of hand luggage by an official posing as a passenger, sent into the airport by the Department for Transport.
