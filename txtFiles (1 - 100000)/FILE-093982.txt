The family of an unarmed Mexican orchard worker shot dead by police after allegedly throwing rocks at cars have requested a fresh autopsy of his body by an independent examiner. Antonio Zambrano-Montes, 35, was killed last week when three officers opened fire on him at a busy intersection in Pasco, Washington. His death has become a new flashpoint in the debate over police violence in the wake of Ferguson after video of the killing appeared to show Montes running away from officers with his hands raised when he was hit.
