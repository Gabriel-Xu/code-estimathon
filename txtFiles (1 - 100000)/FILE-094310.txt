Georgia was England's top scorer during the U17 Women's World Cup, with three goals in four games as they reached the quarter finals.She also played every minute of England's games at the Uefa Women's U17 Championship finals, scoring two goals and making one assist.Sixteen-year-old Jess Stretton won individual gold in the Q1 archery at the Rio Paralympic Games.As the youngest competitor in the archery event she set a new Paralympic record, with compatriots Jo Frith and Vicky Jenkins taking silver and bronze position to make it a clean sweep for Great Britain.She also claimed silver at the Fazaa International Para Championships, breaking a world record in an earlier round.
