Neuroendocrine tumours are cancers that grow in hormone producing tissues, usually in the digestive system. They are rare. The endocrine system is a network of glands and organs in the body that produce hormones. The tumours usually develop slowly over a number of years. Many people don’t have symptoms at first. It is not unusual for people to find that a neuroendocine cancer has already spread to another part of the body when they are diagnosed. There is some debate among doctors about how neuroendocrine tumours should be grouped and what they should be called. Although they develop from the same types of cell, they develop in different organs in the body.
