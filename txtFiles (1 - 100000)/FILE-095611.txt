Manchester United's famous Class of '92 are set to take to the field once again this week - to take on the team some of them own. Ryan Giggs, Paul Scholes, Nicky Butt and the Neville brothers, Gary and Phil, are expected to play a part as the '92 vintage take on Salford City on Thursday at the AJ Bell Stadium. The quintet bought the club earlier this year and intend to get them from the Evo-Stik League Division One North - the eighth tier - to the Football League .
