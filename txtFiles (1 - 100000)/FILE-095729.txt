A painting that has been gathering dust in storage for decades has been identified as a £1m piece by 17th-century master, Sir Anthony van Dyck. The portrait of a woman with spectacular pearl earrings and a red flowers in her hair was thought to have been an undistinguished copy, and lay languishing in a storage room at Bowes Museum in Durham. But after being photographed for a project to put all the UK's oil paintings on one website, it was spotted by an art historian, who realised the significance of the work. Historians say a previously unknown painting that has been stored at Bowes Museum in Durham is by Van Dyck and could be worth up to £1million.
