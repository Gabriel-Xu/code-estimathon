A lioness in Zimbabwe has broken the world record by giving birth to eight cubs in one litter, despite being on a contraceptive pill. Named Pasha, she gave birth on . November 4 at the Lion and Cheetah Park on the outskirts of Harare, Zimbabwe. She had already given birth to two cubs earlier this . year, and had been immediately put on the pill to control her breeding, so the arrival of eight cubs was a surprise to staff. Parks officials only announced the extraordinary birth this week and the young cubs are being looked after by staff at the zoo.
