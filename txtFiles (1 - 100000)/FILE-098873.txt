Francis Wilson from Albuquerque, New Mexico unwittingly put her $235 rent check for her landlord in an envelope along with her $20 Comcast subscription payment and accidentally sent it off to the company. But when she realised her mistake and asked for it back, the Comcast admitted that they had already cashed the check and said they would only pay it back by putting credit on her account.
