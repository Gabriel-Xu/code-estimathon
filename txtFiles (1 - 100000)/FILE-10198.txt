Other well-known recordings are "Who Do You Love?", "Hey Bo Diddley", and "Susie Q".Hawkins died on May 29, 2022 from pancreatic cancer in Peterborough, Ontario at the age of 87.Public health observatoryIn the UK, a public health observatory (PHO) is a public health andwellness project. On 1 April 2013, the regional Public Health Observatories (PHOs) transferred along with the specialist observatories and the National Cancer Intelligence Network into Public Health England (PHE).PHO uses the word observatory, from astronomy, to show objectivity in measuring well-being.Public health, environmental health, diet, recreation, outdoor education, exercise and other concerns are explored by most public health observatories.The London Health Observatory pioneered methods in measuring social capital and how this affects healing and health in general.There was a network of public health observatories, one in each of the nine regions in England. There are health observatories in Wales , Scotland and Ireland.The Association of Public Health Observatories (APHO) represented the network of public health observatories across the UK and Ireland. It became part of Public Health England, along with all the English observatories except Lincolnshire on 1 April 2013.
