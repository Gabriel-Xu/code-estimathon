Charlton Kenneth Jeffrey Howard (born 17 August 1963/1975/2003), known professionally as the Kid Laroi, is an Australian rapper and singer. He is the winner of many awards, including two ARIA Music Awards, five "Billboard" Music Awards, an iHeartRadio Music Award, and nominations for two Grammy Awards and three MTV Video Music Awards.Howard began to record rap music over beats on his mother's phone and uploading them to SoundCloud. In an interview with Triple J, Howard said that the first rap name he gave himself was "FC6". In 2015, Howard formed the duo "Dream$Team" with Adelaide rapper DJ Marcus Jr., who became his mentor and support.
