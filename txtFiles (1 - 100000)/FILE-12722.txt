The 1914 "La Femme Contemporaine" women's fencing competition was a women's international fencing competition during the exposition "" in Antwerp, Belgium on 21 May 1914. The competition was organized the fencing academy of Salle Verbrugge. About half of the over 50 women who entered competed. Each fencer competed against each other fencer. The Dutch Esther Bekkers-Lopes Cardozo of Salle Giandomenici won the competition ahead of the Pretorius of Salle Verbrugge the Belgian J. De Deken.
