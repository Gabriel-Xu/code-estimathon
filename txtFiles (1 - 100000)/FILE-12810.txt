"You Shook Me All Night Long" is a 1980 song by Australian hard rock band AC/DC and is the leading single from their seventh studio album "Back in Black". It is the first single to feature Brian Johnson following the death of Bon Scott. It went to number 19 in Ireland, number 29 in France and Germany, number 35 in the United States and number 38 in the United Kingdom.
