At the 1988 Claxton Shield, Buffalo teenager Scott McCormack pitched 10 strong innings against Victoria, only to lose the game in extra innings 3 - 2.Bill Hutcheson, a catcher from originally Tennant Creek and later Darwin, was the only player to play in all 8 Claxton Shields for the Territory.At the 1988 Games he finished fourth with the British boat in the men's eight contest.At the 1988, Games he was a member of the Brazilian team which finished fourth in the Olympic tournament.At the 1988 Games, Les Autres sportspeople were not eligible to participate in wheelchair basketball.At the 1988 general election Lovelee defeated Owen in a rematch, but the seat was abolished in 1991 and Lovelee retired.
