Cuthbert Gordon Greenidge MBE (born 1 May 1951) is a Barbadian former first-class cricketer, who played Tests and One Day Internationals for 17 years for West Indies.Cuthbert himself had been elected in a special election in 1813.Cuthbert in the Mines is a 1984 computer game for the Dragon 32 home computer.Cuthbert is a former copper mining town in the locality of Three Rivers, Shire of Cloncurry, Queensland, Australia.Cuthbert is a ghost town in Mitchell County, Texas, United States.Cuthbert is an unincorporated community in Sanborn County, in the U.S. state of South Dakota.
