Eaton Corporation Plc is a multinational power management company with 2017 sales of $20.4 billion, founded in the United States with corporate headquarters in Dublin, Ireland.Eaton County is a county in the U.S. state of Michigan.Eaton County is included in the Lansing-East Lansing, Metropolitan Statistical area in Central Michigan.Eaton Elementary School, also known as Third Ward School, is located at 1105 McInnis Ave.Eaton Estates is a census-designated place (CDP) in Lorain County, Ohio, United States.Eaton Ford is a district of St Neots (wherein the population is included) and is in Cambridgeshire, England.Eaton Ford lies on the west bank of the River Great Ouse, on the western side of St Neots.Eaton Green (born 1967) was a Yardie gang member involved in armed robbery, drug dealing and extortion in South London.Eaton Hall, built in 1908 as Eaton Memorial Library, used to be the main library building at Tufts University in Medford, Massachusetts.
