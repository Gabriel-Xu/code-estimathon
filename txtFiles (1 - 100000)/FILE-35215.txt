He was Treasurer of Christ Church Cathedral, Waterford from 1684 to 1686; and Chancellor from then until 1890.He was Treasurer of Cloyne Cathedral from 1935 to 1942 and then of Cork until his appointment as Archdeacon.He was treasurer of Cloyne Cathedral from 1959 until 1968.He was Treasurer of Glamorgan County Cricket Club for nine years, and is a director and former Chairman of Llanelli Scarlets, the leading rugby union club.He was treasurer of Gray's Inn in 1628.
