His work is concerned with an ongoing examination of the transformation of modes of historical avant-gardism into the present.His work is concerned with light as both a physical phenomenon and metaphorical figure.His work is concerned with the social and political debate in Albania and Kosovo.His work is considered an exemplar of a particular contemporary South-East Queensland "style".His work is considered as representative of Socialist realism in art in France.His work is considered part of the 'Neo -Realism' trend of Spanish art.His work is considered part of the "New Nordic Movement", a term to describe 21st century design in various media as practiced by designers from Northern European countries.His work is considered pioneering in social reform, especially in the field of sanitation and hygiene.His work is considered to be at the leading edge in urban hydrology and the treatment of waste and stormwater, realizing sustainable infrastructure which combines city resource needs with space for nature and people.His work is considered to be illustrative of 3rd generation Ghanaian artists, who freely express themselves as artists in a modern world, without succumbing to restrictive notions of African Art.
