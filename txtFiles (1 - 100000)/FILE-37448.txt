In 1984, she graduated from Grinnell College with an independently designed major that combined economics, history, sociology, and Spanish, with a year off in Zagreb learning Croatian.In 1984 she joined B'not Esh, a Jewish feminist community, and early on, during one of their annual retreats, shared her first poems.In 1984 she joined Jay Thomas as co-host of the WKTU/New York morning show.In 1984, she landed a national tour of Pippin with Ben Vereen in the role of Fastrada.In 1984, she married Dan Franklin and they have two daughters.In 1984 she married the actor Bill Paterson; they have two children.In 1984 she moved to Arizona where she won the title of Miss Arizona Teen USA in 1992.In 1984 she moved to New Haven, Connecticut.In 1984 she moved to New York City, playing piano in jazz clubs.In 1984 she moved to Philadelphia where she exhibited her work at various venues.In 1984, she obtained her PhD in social Anthropology at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro.In 1984 she participated in the foundation of the Green Party.In 1984 she placed 7th overall, in 1988 18th.In 1984, she ran against Republican Susan Golding for San Diego County Supervisor.In 1984, she ran for the office of state representative, and served two terms in the state legislature prior to running for Congress in 1988.In 1984, she ran for United States Senate, losing to the Republican incumbent, John Warner.In 1984, she received a BSc in planning from the University of Toronto.In 1984, she recorded her first album at the age of 18, and continued to release albums into the 1990s.In 1984, she re-emerged as Dalbello, with an edgier brand of alternative rock.In 1984, she released her international debut album produced in collaboration with jazz pianist Bobby Enriquez, who led the Cal Tjader Trio for Amapola's debut jazz album, Sophisticated Lady.In 1984, she retired from teaching and dedicated herself to painting and writing.In 1984, she sang on the ABC network campaign, "We're With You on ABC" (a campaign using character actors playing the roles of people watching ABC shows throughout the day).In 1984 she set up the women's nutritional advisory Service, which has helped tens of thousands of women all around the world to improve their health and wellbeing.In 1984, she starred as "Laurel Chapin Wolek" in One Life to Live, which she left in 1986.In 1984, she took third place in the Swiss final with Emporte-moi.In 1984 she transferred to London and two years later covered the aftermath of the Chernobyl disaster for Business Week and Nucleonics Week.
