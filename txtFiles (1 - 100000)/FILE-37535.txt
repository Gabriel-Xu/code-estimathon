In 1992, its parent company was bought by Little, Brown & Co., at that stage part of the Time Warner Book Group.In 1992, it started its Urdu news service and hence became the first news agency to provide Urdu news.In 1992 it was acquired by Saft Groupe S.A. and is now headquartered in France.In 1992 it was adapted for television.In 1992, it was added to the List of World Heritage in Danger due to illegal poaching, extensive grazing, unplanned road construction and encroachment of the park's perimeter.
