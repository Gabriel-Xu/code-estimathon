In December 2017, he became a Director of ADS Investment Solutions Limited (ADSI), which is incorporated in the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM).In December 2017, he became the 2nd fastest Indian to reach 4000 ODI runs.In December 2017 he covered the Olympia Horse Show for the BBC.In December 2017 he played his first game for Bristol in his new position of Hooker, having transitioned from the back row.In December 2017 he pleaded guilty to one count of lying to the FBI (a felony) about contacts he had with the Russian government during Trump's presidential transition.In December 2017, he recorded his first tour win in the Joburg Open and followed this with a second win at the Maybank Championship in February 2018.In December 2017, he released his fifth EP A Ghetto Christmas Carol.In December 2017 he returned to Cheltenham to win the International Hurdle on his seasonal debut after a protracted battle with The New One.In December 2017 he signed a one-year with York for 2018 having played for them on loan during the 2017 season, he eventually earned a full-time contract with the Toronto Wolfpack.In December 2017, he signed to compete in the 2018 Superbike World Championship series aboard a Honda Fireblade SP2 for Ten Kate Racing as teammate to Leon Camier.In December 2017 he signed with Wakefield for the 2018 season.In December 2017 he started to present the Italian version of the American game show The Wall.In December 2017, he was appointed as the 10th Bishop of Christchurch, New Zealand.In December 2017, he was awarded Australian Institute of Sport Emerging Athlete of the Year.In December 2017, he was ESPN's number seven recruit in the nation and 247Sports.com's number four recruit.In December 2017, he was hired by the San Francisco Giants organization as an assistant director of player development.
