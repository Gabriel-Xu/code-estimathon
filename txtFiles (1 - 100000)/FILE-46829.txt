It was renamed "Lynn" in 1637 in honor of King's Lynn in England.It was renamed Macaulay on 1 May 1909.The station is located beside the Moonee Ponds Creek, to its west, and surrounded mainly by factories and warehouses.It was renamed Madison Hall and used for classrooms, offices, laboratories, the gymnasium, and assembly hall until 1975.It was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1978.It was renamed Mahakoshal Mahavidyalaya in 1947 after independence.It was renamed Mama Campisi's in 1982, and continued under that name until 2005, when it was closed down.It was renamed Mannerim on 28 October 1890.It was renamed Manson, My Name is Evil after its initial release.
