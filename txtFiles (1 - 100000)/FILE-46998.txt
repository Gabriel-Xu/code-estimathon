It was the home of John Barton Gilbert (1891-1953), a prominent Hartsville manufacturer and businessman.It was the home of John B. Harper, a blacksmith and farmer who also served as a Crawford County judge.It was the home of John D. Rockefeller, Jr. and his wife Abby Aldrich Rockefeller during the restoration of Colonial Williamsburg.It was the home of John FitzGibbon, 1st Earl of Clare.It was the home of John H. Bowen, local attorney and member of the United States House of Representatives, and of governor of Tennessee William Trousdale.It was the home of John Lowndes McLaurin (1860-1934), a former United States Congressman and Senator in the early-20th century.It was the home of John Napier, the 8th Laird of Merchiston and the inventor of logarithms, who was born there in 1550.It was the home of John Wood, the Younger.It was the home of Joseph Edward Birch and his descendants.It was the home of Josephus Daniels (1862-1948) from about 1894 to 1913.It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1979.It was the home of Judge Frank Cox, a prominent lawyer who served as prosecuting attorney and Judge on the Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia.It was the home of J.W. White, a long-time resident who had an electrical company in Casa Grande.
