Riverview lies at the intersection of River Road and Riverview Drive, southeast of Junction City, near the Willamette River.Riverview Manor is an unincorporated community located within Piscataway Township in Middlesex County, New Jersey, United States.Riverview Medical Center is a 476-bed acute care community hospital located in Red Bank, New Jersey, United States.Riverview Medical Center provides assisted living nursing home care and home care.Riverview Mounds Archeological Site (40MT44), also known as the Rinehart Acres, is an archeological site of the Mississippian culture located south of Clarksville in Montgomery County, Tennessee, on the eastern shore of the Cumberland River.
