She was named a regular co-host of the afternoon talk program Outnumbered on November 14, 2016.She was named Arizona's Gatorade Player of the Year.She was named Art Director of New Media at Atlantic Records in 2000 and remained there until 2003, creating online content and overseeing websites for all Atlantic artists including Lil' Kim, T.I., Trick Daddy, Jewel, Brandy and many others.She was named as 1st runner-up for the Quality of Life Award at the Miss America 2012 pageant.She was named as Adelaide's inaugural co-vice-captain alongside Angela Foley in January 2017.She was named as an Adjunct professor attached to the Governance Institute at the University of Canberra, in 2000.She was named as an alternate at the 1996 Summer Olympics, but did not participate.She was named as a new millennium it girl by Harper's Bazaar along with Kate Moss and Amber Valletta.She was named as an outstanding performing artist.She was named as Antigua and Barbuda's sportswoman of the year in 2011.She was named as a Purpose Prize winner in 2009 and a top 10 CNN Hero in 2012.She was named Asian Young Footballer of the Year in 2013.She was named Asia's Best Female Chef in 2017, and has used this as a platform to promote LGBT issues and help women find work in professional kitchens.She was named Asia's Best Pastry Chef 2016 by The World's 50 Best Restaurants.She was named as New Zealand sportswoman of the year for 1990 at the Halberg Awards.She was named as one of Australian Art Collector magazine's 50 Most Collectible artists in 2008; her works are held by the National Gallery of Australia and the Art Gallery of New South Wales.She was named as one of Canada's 100 Most Powerful Women in 2011.She was named as one of the 100 "Greatest Sportswomen of the Century" by Sports Illustrated for Women.She was named as one of the members of the Sri Lankan contingent at the 2018 Commonwealth Games and claimed a bronze medal in the women's 48kg event on the opening day of the competition on 5 April 2018, which was also the second medal earned by Sri Lanka at the 2018 Gold Coast Commonwealth Games and was also the second medal secured by the Sri Lankan contingent in weightlifting category.She was named as one of the most popular women by the BBC in its special multi format series, the 100 Women in 2015.She was named as "One To Watch" by Time Out in 2015.She was named as player of the match when England beat New Zealand in the 2009 Women's Cricket World Cup Final, taking a career-best four wickets for 34 runs.She was named as president of Marlboro College in 2003 and began her duties in April 2004.She later joined the Board of Trustees at Windham Foundation.She was named ASP's Rookie of the Year in 1987.She was named as the best-selling female recording artist of 2009 by the Guillermo Mendoza Memorial Scholarship Foundation.She was named as the Chief Justice of the Nunavut Court of Appeal on March 24, 1999.She was named as the coach of the Tanzania National Netball Team in 2012.She was named as the goal keeper of the New Zealand squad for the 2018 Commonwealth Games and was part of the Silver Ferns team which had a disastrous campaign claiming fourth place in the women's team event after losing to Jamaica in the bronze medal clash.She was named a TED Fellow twice and gave a TED Talk at the 2016 conference.She was named a Thomson Reuters Highly Cited Researcher in the report on "The World's Most Influential Scientific Minds."
