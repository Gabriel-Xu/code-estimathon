Singh was elected to the Assembly for nine out of twelve terms (1957, 1962, 1967, 1972, 1977, 1985, 1990, 1998, 2003), six times out of which from the Jhunjhunu constituency.Singh was elected to the Bihar Legislative Assembly in an 18 March 1979 by-election in the Matihani seat.Singh was featured in 'Chords of Change' TV series.Singh was killed in an automobile accident in 2003, and was succeeded as the Tavua Open MP by Damodran Nair, also of the FLP.Singh was killed on 1 July 2018 in a suicide bombing in Jalalabad which killed 18 other people, mostly from Afghanistan's Sikh and Hindu minority.Singh was named 11th Lieutenant Governor of Andaman and Nicobar Islands in July 2013 and took office on 8 July 2013.
