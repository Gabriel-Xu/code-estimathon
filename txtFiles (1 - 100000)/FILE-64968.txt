The album was inspired by a Japanese folk tale, and centers on two song cycles, The Crane Wife and The Island, the latter inspired by William Shakespeare's The Tempest.The album was inspired by an Anne Briggs EP titled The Hazards of Love.The album was inspired by and dedicated to Virginia Clinton Kelley.The album was inspired by, and documents, the disintegration of Sylvian's relationship with his wife, Ingrid Chavez, marking a turning point in Sylvian's lyrics as they became more personal and open and less oblique.
