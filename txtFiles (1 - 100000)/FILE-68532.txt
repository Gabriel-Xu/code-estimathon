The Diogenes Club is a fictional gentleman's club created by Sir Arthur Conan Doyle and featured in several Sherlock Holmes stories, such as "The Greek Interpreter".The Diogenidae are a family of hermit crabs, sometimes known as "left-handed hermit crabs" because in contrast to most other hermit crabs, its left chela (claw) is enlarged instead of the right.The Diogo Bernardes Theater (Portuguese: Teatro Diogo Bernardes) is a theater in the civil parish of Arca e Ponte de Lima, in the municipality of Ponte de Lima, in the Portuguese district of Porto.The DIO is an agency of the Australian Intelligence Community and is part of the Defense Strategic Policy and Intelligence Group with the Australian Geospatial-Intelligence Organization (AGO) and the Australian Signals Directorate (ASD).
