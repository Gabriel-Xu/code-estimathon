The Durand Express was a weekly newspaper published in Durand, Michigan from 1888 until 2003.It was owned by a number of different individuals until being sold to the Argus-Press of Owosso, Michigan.The Durand Mk V was a two-seat sports biplane aircraft developed in the United States in the 1970s and marketed for home building.The Durango and Silverton Narrow Gage Railroad, driven by a historical steam locomotive, runs through the canyon of the Animas River.The Durango chipmunk (Neotamias durangae) is a species of rodent in the family Sciuridae.The Durango class is an offshore patrol vessel class in service with the Mexican Navy.The Durango Kid is a 1940 American Western directed by Lambert Hillyer, starring Charles Starrett, Luana Walters and Kenneth MacDonald.The Durango mud turtle (Kinosternon durangoense) is a species of mud turtle in the Kinosternidae family.The durangonella de Coahuila snail, scientific name Durangonella coahuilae, is a species is a genus of minute freshwater snails with an operculum, aquatic gastropod molluscs or micromolluscs in the family Hydrobiidae.The Durango night lizard is a diminutive lizard found in the Mexican state of Durango.The Durango salamander or pine woods salamander, Ambystoma silvense, is a mole salamander.The Durango shiner (Notropis aulidion) is an extinct species of freshwater fish of the Cyprinidae family.The Durango shiner was native to the Rio Tunal, which forms the headwaters of the San Pedro Mezquital River, a Pacific slope river rising near Durango City, Durango, Mexico (Chernoff and Miller 1986).The Duranos also helped in the early years in establishing the company.The Durans would move to Mississippi later in 2016 and the Stars would officially fold.The Durant Affair is a 1962 British drama film directed by Godfrey Grayson and starring Jane Griffiths, Conrad Phillips and Nigel Green.The Durant automobile is considered to be an example of an "assembled" automobile because so many of its components were obtained from outside suppliers.
