Their 1968 single "Do the Reggay", was the first song to first use the word "reggae", naming the genre and introducing it to a global audience.Their 1972 song "Brandy (You're a Fine Girl)" was a #1 hit on both the Billboard Hot 100 and Cash Box Top 100 charts, remaining in the top position for one week.Their 1973 song "Leaving Me" reached #21 on the Billboard Hot 100, and remained on the chart for 14 weeks.Their 1975 single, "Bohemian Rhapsody", was number 1 for nine weeks (and a further five weeks in 1991) and is the third biggest selling single of all time in the UK.Their 1977 album Blank Generation influenced many other punk bands.Their 1977 song, "Riding High", has been sampled by Hip Hop artists.Their 1978 single, She's Gonna Win, made #42 on the UK Singles Chart.Their 1979 debut album, Cut, has been called one of the defining releases of the post-punk era.Their 1979 debut single On the Beach featured Joey Ramone on vocals.Their 1979 hit "Rapper's Delight" was the first rap single to become a Top 40 hit on the Billboard Hot 100.
