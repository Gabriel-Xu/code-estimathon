W. Kennedy Junior High School and served as a junior high school from 1989-1991.The school was renamed the Milwaukee Campus for Trades, Technology and Media in 2011 to focus on vocational and technology programs.The school was renamed the University of Chicago Booth School of Business.The school was renamed the USC Suzanne Dworak-Peck School of Social Work in 2016, after USC alumnus Suzanne Dworak-Peck donated $60 million to the school.The school was renamed three times, Stellenbosch Boys High, Victoria College (after Queen Victoria) and in 1940 Paul Roos Gymnasium.The school was renamed to SMK Cochrane as it is no longer situated on Jalan Cochrane.The school was renamed to Union Institute Academy in 1841, Normal College in 1851, and to Trinity College in 1859.The school was renamed Upper Batley High School in September 2015, and converted to academy status in April 2016.The school was renamed Wigston College in 2015.The school was renamed Worden Sports College when it became a specialist Sports College.The school was renovated and was expanded soon after St. Mary's College moved locations.The school was renovated by replacing the existing buildings with new ones.The school was renovated in 1989 and won a local historic preservation award.The school was renovated in 2009 and is rented out for conferences and meetings.
