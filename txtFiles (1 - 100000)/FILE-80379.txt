The town is famous for being the location where Pat Garrett captured Billy the Kid and his associates on December 23, 1880.The town is famous for having the first agricultural university of India which was established on 17 November 1960.The town is famous for its ancient temple Sri Meenakshi Sundareswarar temple also known as Nagara Sivan Kovil.The town is famous for its annual Viking Games as well as for the J.F. Willumsen museum.The town is famous for its Blanca Aurora Falls near Barangay Blanca Aurora along the Blanca Aurora river.The town is famous for its Carabao Festival where carabaos are paraded and kneel as they pass through San Isidro Labrador Parish Church, in honor to the town's patron saint, San Isidro Labrador.The town is famous for its fort built in the late 18th century when Arki was the capital of the erstwhile hill state of Baghal.The town is famous for its Pleistocene sites which possesses rhino bones, tools, deer bones, turtle remains, and stegodon bones.The town is famous for its pottery in the pre-Columbian Chorotega style.The town is famous for its specialized crab dishes and its Alimango festival, which is dedicated to its crab produce.
