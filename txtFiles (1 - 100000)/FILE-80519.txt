The tree was first introduced to the West in 1995, at the Morton Arboretum, Illinois, as part of an evaluation of Chinese elms for landscape use.The tree was fitted with climbing pegs, a platform, and a telephone line which connected to a ranger station.The tree was formerly known as Oreodaphne californica.The tree was in Boscobel Wood, which was part of the park of Boscobel House.The tree was introduced to Florida in 1911 by the USDA, and is also now commonly grown in Suriname, Guyana and Trinidad and Tobago.The tree was introduced to the UK in 1752, but has never prospered there, being prone to dieback.The tree was located about 100m from the Perak Darul Ridzuan building which houses the state assembly.The tree was marked by surveyor Banning Austin in 1893 during a road survey of the region; the mountain pass Austin surveyed, which was originally known as Wild Goose Pass, was renamed Austin Pass in his honor.The tree was most commonly found on the flood plains, indicating a tolerance of periodic inundation.The tree was named both Welsh and British tree of the year for 2016 and was runner up in the European Tree of the Year awards of 2017.The tree was not recognized as a valid cultivar by some authorities.The tree was of great economic importance from the 17th century to the 19th century, when it was commonly logged and exported to Europe for use in dyeing fabrics.The tree was once common throughout West Africa and parts of Central Africa, with O. aubrevillei being the primary West African species, and O. michelsonii found in Central Africa.The tree was one of several cultivars prepared for release in 1970, but delayed by the outbreak of the second, far more aggressive strain of Dutch elm disease.The tree was originally identified as 'Pendula' by Loddiges (London), in his catalog of 1836, a name adopted by Loudon two years later in Arboretum et Fruticetum Britannicum, 3: 1398, 1838, but later sunk as a synonym for 'Horizontalis'.The tree wasp (Dolichovespula sylvestris) is a species of eusocial wasp in the family Vespidae, found in the temperate regions of Eurasia, particularly in western Europe.The tree wasp is sometimes a victim of the nest parasite Dolichovespula omissa, who lays their eggs in the nest of D. sylvestris, as well as individual parasites including roundworms.
