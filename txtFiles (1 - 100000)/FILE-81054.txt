The village is administrative center for the Sandy Lake Band of Mississippi Chippewa, though the administration of the Mille Lacs Indian Reservation, District II, is located in the nearby East Lake.The village is adopted as a model village under the Sansad Adarsh Gram Yojana by Guwahati MP Bijoya Chakrabarty.The village is adopted by honorable Vinod Kumar Boianapalli who is a Member of Parliament (MP) of Karimnagar.The village is a dormitory village.The village is a few miles west of Exmoor.The village is affected by illegal immigrants from Zimbabwe, and resulting crime.
