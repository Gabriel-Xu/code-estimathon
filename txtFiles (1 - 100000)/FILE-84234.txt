Tipperary Racecourse is a horse racing venue in County Tipperary, Ireland which stages both National Hunt and Flat racing.Tipperary Racecourse is owned by Horse Racing Ireland.Tipperary, represented by Thurles Sarsfields, won the inaugural championship, beating Galway, represented by Meelick, in the 1887 final.Tipperary responded with another goal by Dinny Murphy to finally end the game as a contest.Tipperary retained the title with an easy win.Tipperary's All-Ireland defeat was their fourth ever and their second in succession.Tipperary's All-Ireland defeat was their third ever and their first since last winning the title in 1981.Tipperary's All-Ireland victory was their first since 1930.Tipperary's All-Ireland victory was their fourth since the start of the decade.Tipperary's All-Ireland victory was their second in four years.Tipperary's Darragh Egan was the championship's top scorer with 3-23.Tipperary's first Championship game came against Limerick on 9 June, where they were defeated 1-18 to 1-15 at the Gaelic Grounds.Tipperary's home ground is Semple Stadium and Wexford's home ground is Innovate Wexford Park, however, all bar one of their championship meetings have been held at neutral venues, usually Croke Park.Tipperary's Michael Cleary was the championship's top scorer with 3-35.
