The show was originally on television in the United Kingdom from 28 September 1981 to 19 March 1992. It was produced by Cosgrove Hall Films for Thames Television. The series also had a spin-off show, "Count Duckula"."Danger Mouse" was a parody of British spy fiction, particularly the "Danger Man" series and James Bond.A continuation of the original series, under the same name, began airing in September 2015 on CBBC.
