Ryanair is going to great lengths to improve its image and win over customers, but it has topped a list of the ten least client-friendly airlines, according to a new consumer survey. The list, which places British Airways joint sixth with four other major international carriers, is based on how passengers are treated when making complaints. Raclamador.es, a Spanish consumer organisation which conducted the survey, claims Ryanair 'does not accept to resolve complaints amicably.
