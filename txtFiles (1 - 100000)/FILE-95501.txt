Two firefighters lost their lives responding to a nine-alarm fire at a brick brownstone in Boston's Back Bay neighborhood Wednesday. More than a dozen other firefighters were injured. "In 30 years, I've never seen a fire travel that fast, escalate that quickly, and create such havoc in such a short period of time," Deputy Fire Chief Joe Finn told reporters. He identified those killed as Lt. Edward Walsh, 43, and Firefighter Michael Kennedy, 33. Walsh had worked with the Boston Fire Department for close to 10 yeas, while Kennedy had 6 1/2 years on the job. Walsh was married with three kids, all under the age of 10. Kennedy was a veteran of the U.S. Marine Corps. "These two heroes ran into a burning building -- got people out of the building," said Boston Mayor Martin Walsh. "We lost two heroes here today."
