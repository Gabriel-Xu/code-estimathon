'Most people my age can't feel anything,’ one of the oldest licensed physicians practicing medicine in the U.S. told a crowd of friends and family gathered for his 100th birthday party. ‘They're dead.’ Dr Fred Goldman, of Cincinnati, Ohio, has been a doctor since 1935 when Sigmund Freud was still practising - and still sees 12 patients a day in his computer-free suite which he calls ‘the dump’. The 100-year-old physician still makes house calls and has no plans to give up his work - although he did cut back from five eight-hour days a week to three at the age of 96.
