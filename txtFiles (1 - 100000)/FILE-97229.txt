Bernie Kozen was pictured today talking to sheriff's deputies as the victims authorities say were killed by scorned resident Rockne Newell, 59, were identified as Chestnuthill Township Supervisor David Fleetwood, Gerard Kozic, 53, of Saylorsburg and James LaGuardia, 64, of Saylorsburg.
