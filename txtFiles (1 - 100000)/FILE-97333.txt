A 61-year-old man has been arrested and charged after allegedly groping a sleeping woman while touching himself on a Newark-bound flight on Sunday. Indian national, Devender Singh, who lives in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, has been charged with one count of abusive sexual contact and appeared in federal court in New Jersey on Monday.
