"Should I tell him where I live? I want to film you doing that in the studio and really get a sense of who you are.'"By a stroke of luck, the film-maker turned up on the day Haim recorded the "perfect take" of Right Now. The version in the video forms the basis of the album track, with overdubs and harmonies added later.But that was in November.
