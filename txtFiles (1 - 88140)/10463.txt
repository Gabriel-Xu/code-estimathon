The constitution also gives them a right to name themselves under a third gender. Homosexuality was never illegal in ancient Indian and traditional codes. It was made a crime by the British during their rule in India.Though LGBT rights have been spoken for in recent political movements, much homophobia is still present among the people. A poll from 2019 says that around half of Indians object to same-sex relationships. Hindu respondents accept the LGBT community the highest. In 2010s, LGBT people in India gained tolerance and acceptance, especially in large cities.
