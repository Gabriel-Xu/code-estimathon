Canadian Premier League (CPL) is a Canadian soccer league that started in 2017. As of the current 2023 season, 8 teams play in the league. The commissioner of the league (the person in charge) is Mark Noonan.The CPL is the highest level on the Canadian soccer league system. The CPL was formed on May 26, 2017 as a professional league by the Canadian Soccer Association.The CPL regular season starts in April and ends in October, with each team playing 28 games.
