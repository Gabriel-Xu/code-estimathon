Denzel Rae Don Curry (born February 16, 1995) is an American rapper and singer. He was born in Miami Gardens, Florida. He made his first mixtape in 2011. SpaceGhostPurrp put the mixtape on social media. This made Curry more popular. Curry joined Purrps' hip-hop group, Raider Klan.Curry left Raider Klan in 2013. He made his first studio album. It was named "Nostalgic 64". He made many extended plays (EPs). Curry has made many albums that have been on the "Billboard" 200.
