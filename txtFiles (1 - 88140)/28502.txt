First published by Macmillan of Canada in 1975, this novel focuses on the life-story of the fictional conjuror Magnus Eisengrim.First published by Macmillan of Canada in 1981, The Rebel Angels is the first of the three connected novels of Davies' Cornish Trilogy.First published by McClelland and Stewart in September 1994, the novel won the Lambda Literary Award for gay male fiction and the Books in Canada First Novel Award.First published by McSweeney's in November 2003, it was nominated for the National Book Critics Circle Award.First published by Michael Joseph, it was reprinted by Persephone Books in 1999 and is the second in their collection.First published by Oxford University Press Canada in 1998, it became a well-known reference for Canadian English.
