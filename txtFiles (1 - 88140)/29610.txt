Friedrich Hillegeist (born 21 February 1895 in Vienna, died 3 December 1973 in Vienna) was an Austrian politician.Friedrich Huch (Braunschweig, 19 June 1873 - Munich, 12 May 1913) was a German writer.Friedrich Huldreich Erismann, or Fyodor Fyodorovich Erismann (14 November 1842 - 13 November 1915) was a Swiss ophthalmologist and hygienist born in Gontenschwil, canton of Aargau.
