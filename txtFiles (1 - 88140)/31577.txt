He attended the gymnasium at Bruchsal and in 1814 entered Heidelberg University, where in 1817 he was appointed a lecturer (privatdozent) in history, in 1818 a secretary at the university library, in 1819 an associate professor, in 1822 a full professor, and in 1825 head of the university library.He attended The Haverford School in Haverford, Pennsylvania, and Tufts University.He attended the Hebrew Institute of Long Island through 9th grade, then moved to Hewlett, Long Island where he graduated from Woodmere H.S. in 1954.He attended the High School for the Performing and Visual Arts in Houston, a magnet school of the Houston Independent School District.He attended the Honiara Technical Institute from 1981 to 1985, followed by management courses, before working as a heavy plant mechanic.He attended The Hutchins School, in Hobart.He attended the Ilya Repin Leningrad Institute for Painting, Sculpture and Architecture and was a student of Oleg Eremeev.He attended the Indonesian Air Force Academy in 1967-69.He attended the Industrial College of the Armed Forces from August 1971 to July 1972.He "attended the Institute of American Indian Arts, and studied fine arts with Oscar Howe at the University of South Dakota at Vermillion."He attended the Institute of Musical Art, then studied under Fritz Busch in Germany.He attended the Interlochen Arts Academy, earned his B.A. at the Cleveland Institute of Music in 1977, and his M.A. in 1978.He attended the Interlochen Arts Academy in Interlochen, Michigan.He attended the Intermediate School Killorglin.He attended the International Congress of Mathematician in Beijing in 2002.He attended the International Ornithological Congress at Berlin where he spoke on the rationale for international bird protection.He attended the International School Ibadan.He attended the International School of Geneva.He attended the Iowa Writer's Workshop and Case Western Reserve University, and currently is an associate professor of English at the University of Southern California.
