He created and developed two cable television networks: The A&E Network and The History Channel.He created and directed a cartoon for Nickelodeon and Frederator Studios called Flavio which will be seen as part of the animated series Random Cartoons in 2008.He created and directed the children's series Rubbish, King of the Jumble for ITV and was nominated for a BAFTA in 2002-03 for the BBC pre-school animation series Boo! which he directed.He created and directed the Developmental Behavioral Biology Program at the NICHD where he initiated NICHD-supported research programs to study the relationship between mother-child bonding and the development of social abilities in adult life.He created and directed two shorts Bloo's Gang and "Ignoramooses" for Cartoon Network and went on to direct numerous TV shows (some of which he had worked on earlier in his career) such as Animaniacs, Pinky and the Brain, and Xiaolin Showdown.He created and edited the monthly periodical The Truthseeker (1863-1887).He created and has maintained for 22 years an annual meeting of that community with the President of the United States.He created and hosted a daily free market podcast, Coffee and Markets, until 2014.He created and hosts the program Computer America, the longest running, nationally syndicated radio talk show about computers.He created and marketed a number of effects, including "Dean's Box," "Blizzard", and "A New World" (created with magic innovator Michael Weber).He created and owns the Towler Institute in Vallico di Sotto in Tuscany, Italy.He created and produced a wide array of shows and TV series and specials with his brother, Marty, beginning in the 1960s.He created and produced Strategy Room.He created and produced the animated "Virtual Shabbat" CD-ROM for The National Jewish Outreach Program, and later developed several Jewish and Israel-oriented cable shows, including one for the World Zionist Organization.He created and produced The Days and Nights of Molly Dodd and The Slap Maxwell Story, co-created Buffalo Bill (with Tom Patchett), and was an executive producer for The Bob Newhart Show.
