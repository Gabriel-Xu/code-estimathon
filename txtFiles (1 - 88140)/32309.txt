Religions and Conservation and as a consultant for World Wildlife Fund.He has served as director since October 2010.He has served as District Attorney for Clatsop County since 1994.He has served as district councilor in Tsuen Wan since 1985.He has served as Editor and now is Deputy Editor-in-Chief of the international astronomy journal Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society.He has served as editor for many of his and his spouse's works.He has served as editor for the following scholarly journals: IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management, the Journal of Applied Management Studies, the Journal of Information Technology Management, the Journal of Management Systems, and the Communications of the ACM.He has served as Editor-in-Chief of the McGill Law Journal and has been called one of the most important Constitutional and criminal law experts in Canada.He has served as Editor of the Energy, Environment and National Resources area of the Operations Research Journal.He has served as either co-head writer, associate head writer, or a breakdown writer on the shows he has worked.He has served as Essendon captain since the 2017 season.He has served as executive director acquisition by Amazon in 1998.He has served as General Secretary of Administration and Organization at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (1999-2000).He has served as general secretary of the Cotton
