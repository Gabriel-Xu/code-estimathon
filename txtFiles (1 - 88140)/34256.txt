He spent the 1974 season with the Birmingham Americans and 1975 with the Birmingham Vulcans, both of the World Football League.He spent the 1977 season in the West Australian National Football League (WANFL), playing for Claremont, having been involved in a trade which saw Claremont star Kevin Worthington arrive at Collingwood.He spent the 1977 season with Cooee in Tasmania, meaning he had played at highest level in four different states.He spent the 1979 and 1980 seasons with the Surf.He spent the 1980 season with the Sacramento Gold, ranking only behind Scott Manning in GAA.He spent the 1981 and 1982 seasons at Footscray before playing at East Ballarat in 1983.He spent the 1982 season out of professional football before returning to the game via the USFL; he played in the 1983 & 1984 seasons as a member of the Birmingham Stallions and Jacksonville Bulls, respectively.He spent the 1982 season with the Denver Bears and Burlington Rangers.He spent the 1982 VFL season at Essendon and then signed with Coburg, where he played in 1983.He spent the 1982 VFL season playing for Collingwood before being traded to Richmond the following season.He spent the 1988 season in the SANFL as a full-forward with West Torrens and kicked 38 goals to finish as their leading goal kicker.He spent the 1991 season with the Burnie Hawks in the TFL Statewide League and returned to the AFL the following year, joining Brisbane.He spent the 1992 season primarily up forward and kicked 37 goals for the year.He spent the 1994-95 and 1995-96 seasons with Stafford Rangers.He spent the 1994 season playing for Swan Districts, before being named as a foundation selection with the new Fremantle Football Club.He spent the 1996-7 school year at the University of Cambridge Computer Laboratory and at the University of Glasgow.He spent the 1996 season on injured reserve, and was released in training camp a year later.He spent the 2000/2001 season at Worcester Rugby, in England, then returned to Italy.He spent the 2003-04 and 2004-05 seasons once again with HC Dynamo Moscow, and has been with Metallurg Magnitogorsk every season since.He spent the 2003 season as a consultant for the Los Angeles Dodgers before being named as Assistant General Manager of the Cincinnati Reds in December of that year.He spent the 2005 and 2006 seasons as the 49ers' quarterbacks coach.He spent the 2005 pre-season with AFC Wimbledon, but returned to Rugby Town in August 2005.In February 2006 Nicholls moved to London for work and joined Wingate & Finchley.He spent the 2005 season and the start of the 2006 season with the Long Beach Armada of the Golden Baseball League before spending time again with the Athletics as well as the Seattle Mariners organizations.He spent the 2005 season with the San Diego Padres organization, appearing in 10 games for the Padres, and finishing with an 8.18 ERA.He spent the 2006, 2005, and 2004 seasons as the offensive coordinator of the Colorado Crush.He spent the 2006/2007 season in England where he led the British Playoff Champions with 16 ppg and 4 assist, and 2007/2008 season in Giessen Germany where he averaged 3.1 ppg in limited action.He spent the 2006 close season coaching in the USA at the Charlton Athletic US Soccer Academy, returning to Rushall in September 2006.He spent the 2006 NFL preseason with the Houston Texans before being released at the conclusion of training camp.He spent the 2006 season in the San Diego Padres organization, making the Triple-A All-Star team that year, in which he also participated in the home run derby.He spent the 2006 season with the Battle Creek Crunch of the Great Lakes Indoor Football League.He spent the 2007-08 NFL season as a defensive assistant with the Miami Dolphins.He spent the 2007 CFL season with the Lions and participated in all 18 regular season games.He spent the 2007 season playing for the Cincinnati Reds Triple-A club, the Louisville Bats.He spent the 2007 season with Double-A Trenton and Triple-A Scranton.He spent the 2007 season with the Cologne Centurions of the NFL Europe and came back with the Colts for training camp in 2008.He spent the 2008-2009 season with the Rockford Icehogs of the American Hockey League.He spent the 2008 season at North Adelaide Football Club in the South Australian National Football League, before returning to Peel Thunder in 2009.He spent the 2008 season on Injured Reserve after not recovering from knee surgery.He spent the 2009 season playing for Woodville-West Torrens in the South Australian National Football League (SANFL), but made an impressive pre-season debut for Port Adelaide in February 2010, scoring two goals and being named as one of his team's best players.He spent the 2011-12 season as an assistant coach at Miami (Ohio).He spent the 2011 and 2012 offseasons with the Eagles.He spent the 2011 season as the Raiders' cornerbacks coach.He spent the 2012-13 winter playing for Mid West Rhinos in Zimbabwe, with limited success, looking to reinvigorate his game after three years as a first-team regular while averaging in the twenties.He spent the 2012 preseason with the Oakland Raiders.He spent the 2012 season at Queensborough Community College before being hired as head coach of the Bears in 2013.He spent the 2012 season with the State College Spikes.
