He was a finalist in the third series of the Finnish Idols in 2007.He was a finalist of the 2011 Archibald Prize.He was a finalist of the Archibald Prize in 2015, 2016 and 2017.He was a finalist of the BBC Radio 2 Young Folk Award 2011, as well as winning the 'Highly Commended' prize at the Young Storyteller of the Year Awards the same year.He was a finalist on Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Challenge 2007, along with Aneek Dhar and Amanat Ali, in which he finished as runner-up to Aneek Dhar.He was a finalist on SpikeTV's The Ultimate Fighter 7.He was a finalist on the Fox reality show On the Lot.He was a finalist on the NBC reality-talent show Last Comic Standing (seasons 2 and 3).He was a finalist on the ninth season of America's Got Talent placing fifth in the competition.He was a finalist on the seventh series of Britain's Got Talent.He was a finalist on The Ultimate Fighter: Team Lesnar vs. Team dos Santos.He was a finalist to become Obama's running mate, losing, in a close decision, to Delaware Senator Joe Biden.He was a finance minister under the Austrian rule that became the Austrian Empire in 1818.He was a financial writer for The New York Times from 1937 - 1963.He was a financier of NADECO and was a close associate of Obafemi Awolowo.He was a fine attacking batsman who had the potential to be one of the greats.He was a Fine Gael candidate for Dublin North-East in the 2007 general election, he came third last with only 6.5% of the electorate voting for him, and was beaten by Terence Flanagan for the FG seat.He was a "Finland Distinguished Professor" with the University of Jyvaskyla, Finland, and "Changjiang Distinguished Professor" with the Northeastern University, China.He was a Finnish Tatar.He was a firefighter.He was a firefighter by profession.He was a fireman for the MFB and died on duty.He was a firm opponent of the Council of Chalcedon and held communion with those who accepted the Henotikon of Emperor Zeno without imposing a formal anathema on Chalcedon.He was a firm supporter of early Muslim League.He was a firm supporter of Mary, Queen of Scots whom he accompanied to Carberry Hill on 14 June 1567, and fought for at the battle of Langside.He was a first baseman and outfielder for the 1882 Pittsburgh Alleghenys of the American Association and the 1884 Toledo Blue Stockings of the Union Association.He was a first baseman with the Montreal Expos and San Francisco Giants and Chicago White Sox third base coach and is also known for his skill as a pinch hitter.He was a first cousin of Claus Bille, and a member of the Bille family, which became the most politically powerful noble family of Denmark during his lifetime.He was a first cousin of poet Emily Dickinson.He was a first cousin to the previous Earl and had served under him in the royalist forces during the Irish Confederate Wars.He was a First Lieutenant by 1829, when he was appointed adjutant, and purchased a Captaincy in 1832.
