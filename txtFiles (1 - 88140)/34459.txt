Hall and was part of Hall's comedy writing team at Cinesound Productions in the late 1930s.He was a friend of George Bernard Shaw.He was a friend of Hal Jordan's, and a member of the Green Lantern Corps of the Kylminade.He was a friend of Jimmy Bryant, the famous guitar player, with whom he is often confused.
