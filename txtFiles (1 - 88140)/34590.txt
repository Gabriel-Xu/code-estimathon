He was a papal commissioner enquiring into the Knights Templar, 1309 to 1311.He was created cardinal in 1320, as cardinal-priest of Ss.He was a papal legate in France in the 1170s, and was created cardinal in 1182, as cardinal-deacon of Ss.He was a papal master of ceremonies from 1998 to 2013.He was a papal notary and a member of the influential Fieschi family, who later became Bishop of Vercelli.He was a Paramacharya of King Bukka II.He was a paramedic alongside Sam Aleni (Rene Naufahu) and worked at Shortland Street clinic, where his wife Marj (Elizabeth McRae) fronted reception.He was a paratrooper and was honorably discharged in 1968 after serving in the 101st Airborne Division.He was a parish priest from then until 1952 when he was appointed Archdeacon of Owerri.He was a parish priest from then until his appointment as Archdeacon of Aba in 1949.He was a parish priest, publisher, and author or translator of religious and missals in Cebuano.He was a parliamentary candidate at the 1931 general election at Tottenham North.
