He was appointed the "Dean of Academic Affairs" in IIT Guwahati in 2008, and held the post until 2012.He was appointed the Department's Chief upon the death in office of his predecessor, David S. Scannell.He was appointed the Deputy Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation after United National Party won the 2015 general elections.He was appointed the Director of General Election Resources for the party in 2007.He was appointed the District President of the Samajwadi Party from District Moradabad in 2005 and also contested the 2007 Vidhan Sabha elections from Thakurdwara , Uttar Pradesh.He was appointed the eighth Director of the Australian Institute of Sport in December 2011.He was appointed the England forwards coach in December 2015.He was appointed the first Biologist to the Welland & Nene River Division of the Anglian Water Authority in order to develop a monitoring program for their large new reservoir, now called Rutland Water.He was appointed the first Bishop of Espoo in 2004.He was appointed the first Chief Scientist in Western Australia in 2003 but resigned over fundamental differences with the Carpenter Government in 2006.He was appointed the first Chief Trade Adviser to the Pacific Islands Forum in 2009, and resigned from that position on September 2011 and was succeeded by Edwini Kessie, a legal practitioner.He was appointed the first commander of the Institute for Co-operation of Defense and Partnership-Hospitals in 2003, Commander of the Health Care Agency Group in 2004, and Surgeon General in 2007.He was appointed the first general of artillery by King Frederick II in 1744.He was appointed the founding member of the airline on 18 July 2011.He was appointed the fourth Bishop of Parramatta, Australia, by Pope Francis on 5 May 2016.He was appointed the governor of Olderfleet Castle and knighted in 1603.He was appointed the Home Secretary of India on 31 August 2015.He was appointed the inaugural Franklin A. Thomas Professor in Policing Equity at the John Jay College of Criminal Justice in 2016, the college's first endowed professorship.He was appointed the Interim Chair of the Asian, Australasian and Pacific Islands Regional At-Large Organization (APRALO) of ICANN in Sep 2016, elected as Chair in Nov 2016, and re-elected in May 2018.
