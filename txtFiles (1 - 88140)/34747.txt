He was consul ordinarius in AD 204 with Lucius Fabius Cilo as his senior colleague.He was consul ordinarius in AD 54 with Marcus Asinius Marcellus as his colleague.He was consul ordinarius in the year 127 with Marcus Gavius Squilla Gallicanus as his colleague.He was consul ordinarius in the year 50 with Gaius Antistius Vetus as his colleague.He was consul posterior in 124 with Manius Acilius Glabrio as his colleague.He was consul posterior in 148 as the colleague of Lucius Octavius Cornelius Publius Salvius Julianus Aemilianus.He was consul suffect in the last three months of 127 with Sextus Julius Severus as his colleague.He was consul suffectus in an uncertain year.He was Consul Suffectus in the nundinium of April-June 116 as the colleague of Tiberius Julius Secundus.He was consul suffectus with Tiberius Claudius Julianus for the nundinium of September-October 154.He was consultant to the World Bank and Italian government.He was consulted by judges of many tourneys and also organized and/or judged several endgame study tourneys himself.He was consulted by the government on forestry matters, and wrote several memoirs on forestry, appending a list of South African trees, shrubs, and arborescent herbs, to his annual report for 1866.He was contemporary of Pope Alexander II of Alexandria (704 AD - 729 AD).He was contemporary to Pradyota and Bimbisara.He was contemporary to Suryavanshi King Trayarunya who was the ancestor of Lord Rama.He was contestant at the second season of Indian Idol in 2005 and 2006.He was contested as an MLA four times from Uravakonda Assembly Constituency in Anantapur district.He was continued in his judgeship on each subsequent Demise of the Crown, and under Edward V became chief justice of Lancaster.He was continued in the office in 1641, and was one of the commissioners for a treaty of peace with England in 1640-1.He was continued on this post till 2009.He was contracted to St. Helens and played for Leigh Centurions in Super League.He was contracted with Gloucestershire from the 2014 season, but was released at the end of the 2015 season after not playing any first-class cricket that summer.He was contributing editor for The Weekly Standard and was a research associate at the Project for the New American Century.
