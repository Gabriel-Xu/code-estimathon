He was Lord Deputy of Ireland for the Duke of Clarence from 1463 to his death, and in 1464 founded the College of Youghal.He was Lord Florey Professor of Molecular Medicine at the University of Sheffield from 1991 to 2014.He was Lord High Treasurer from 15 April 1407 to 14 July 1408.He was Lord Lieutenant of Antrim from 1994 to 2008.He was Lord-Lieutenant of County Meath and a Senator of the Irish Free State from 1922 to 1928.He was Lord Lieutenant of Dunbartonshire from 1990 to 2007.He was Lord Lieutenant of Huntingdonshire from about 1587 until his death.He was Lord-Lieutenant of Ireland, Lord Privy Seal, Secretary of State for both the Northern and Southern Departments, Lord President of the Council and First Lord of the Treasury.He was Lord Lieutenant of Linlithgowshire.He was Lord-Lieutenant of Renfrewshire from 8 August 1838 until his death.He was Lord-Lieutenant of Suffolk and Cambridgeshire.He was lord-lieutenant of the county of Caithness and lieutenant-colonel of the Ross-shire militia.He was Lord Lieutenant of West Lothian from 1964 to 1985.He was Lord Lieutenant of Wiltshire from 2004 to 2012, having previously served as the county's High Sheriff.He was Lord Lyon King of Arms from 1630 to 1654.He was Lord Mayor of Birmingham for 1939-40.He was Lord Mayor of Bradford in 1924 - 1925.He was Lord Mayor of Cardiff 1982-83 and leader of Cardiff City Council for five years.He was Lord Mayor of Cardiff, Wales for the year 2011 to 2012 and was an elected Cardiff councilor for 17 years.He was Lord Mayor of Dublin during the reign of Charles II of England.He was Lord Mayor of Dublin for the year 1890.He was Lord Mayor of London from 1854 to 1855.He was Lord Mayor of London from 1857 to 1858 and also represented Gloucester and Barnstaple in the House of Commons.He was Lord Mayor of London from 1868 to 1869 and also represented Lambeth in the House of Commons.
