He was selected to play for Australia and was a member of the 1948 Kangaroo Tour.He was selected to play for England as Graham Gooch's opening partner on tours of Australia and the West Indies.He was selected to play for New South Wales Origin in all three games of the 2002 drawn series.He was selected to play for NSW Firsts on one occasion in 1970.He was selected to play for Paralympics GB in the 2012 Summer Paralympics in London.He was selected to play for Quins in the 2009 Middlesex Sevens and in November that year made his first XV debut against Newcastle Falcons in the LV Cup.He was selected to play for the Eels as a reserve in the 1984 NSWRL season's grand final which was lost to the Canterbury-Bankstown Bulldogs.He was selected to play for the New South Wales team against France and Queensland in 1955.He was selected to play for The North of England v NZ All Blacks.He was selected to play for the Third Division South representative side in 1954/55.He was selected to play for the United States in the 2010 Winter Olympics in Vancouver as the team's starting goaltender.He was selected to play in the 1975 and 1976 NHL All-Star Games.He was selected to play in the 1988 Senior Bowl.He was selected to play in the 1992 NHL All-Star Game and still holds many offensive records for the St. Louis Blues.He was selected to play in the 2000 Tri Nations Series, in which he played in five of South Africa's matches during the tournament.He was selected to play in the Combined Universities team to play in the 1985 Benson & Hedges Cup, making his List A debut in the tournament against Surrey and making three further appearances in the competition.He was selected to play in the England Under-19 tour to play against Bangladesh in 2005-06.
