He was the athletic director of the Portland Pilots from 2004 to 2011 and the Marquette Golden Eagles from 2012 to 2013.He was the athletic trainer for the Minnesota Vikings in the National Football League.He was theatrical director at Delmar Garden in St. Louis, Missouri, in 1910.Stammers died of typhoid pneumonia at Roosevelt Hospital and was buried at his summer home in North Brookfield.He was the attendant of William the Lion and was one of the hostages for his release named in the Treaty of Falaise in 1174.Valognes was captured by William Marshal during a tournament at Le Mans, Duchy of Maine, France in 1175 and was ransomed.He was the attorney for the Sisseton Wapheton Oyate and a tribal judge.He was the Attorney General and Commissioner of Justice in the state.He was the Attorney-General for Australia between 1993 and 1996, and since 2004 has been dean and professor of law at Queensland University of Technology (QUT), his alma mater.He was the Attorney General for Nova Scotia from 1878 to 1882 and was briefly the Premier of Nova Scotia in the run-up to the general election of 1882.He was the Attorney-General Minister for Treaty of Waitangi Negotiations and, for a shorter period, Minister of Culture and Heritage.He was the Attorney General of Georgia from 1945 to 1965, and a Justice of the Georgia Supreme Court.He was the Attorney General of Malaysia from 1994 to 2000 and also served as a judge in the High Court and Federal Court.He was the Attorney General of the state of Nevada, United States.He was the Australian Commonwealth Ombudsman from 2012 to 2017.He was the Australian High Commissioner to the United Kingdom from 2008 to 2013.He was the Australian Horse Of The Year in 2009.He was the Australian national captain in 47 matches from 1969 to 1972 and led the Wallaby side on three overseas tours.He was the Australian national swimming champion, an Australian and New South Wales record holder and a bronze medalist in the British Empire Games in 1938.
