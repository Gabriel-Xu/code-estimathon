In 1898 Flatlands was a station on the Intercolonial Railway and had one post office, three stores, a shingle mill, a church and a population of about 400.In 1898 for his revolutionary activity Zolotaryov was exiled to Kherson Governorate and later Poltava Governorate.In 1898 Frank A. Seiberling acquired an old strawboard factory in Akron, Ohio and founded the Goodyear Tire & Rubber Company (naming it after Charles Goodyear).In 1898 Giesl von Gieslingen became head of the Evidenzbureau (1898-1903).In 1898, Gillespie and Jacob Myers formed the American Voting Machines Company.In 1898 Gladestone had 3 hotels, 3 stores, a livery stable among other businesses.In 1898, he began his legal practice in St. Joseph.
