In 1968, he was an outspoken opponent of the city of Whitehorse installing parking meters, even hiring a lawyer to represent all citizens of the city in challenging their parking tickets.In 1968 he was appointed a Canon of Westminster and in 1974 Archdeacon of Westminster, his last appointment before the deanery.In 1968 he was appointed county captain with Jim Parks junior, and was then sole captain for four years (1969-1972).In 1968, he was appointed full professor of the University of Vienna from which he retired in 1992.In 1968, he was appointed Lagos State Commissioner of Finance, in a pioneer cabinet that included Shafi Edu, Adeniran Ogunsanya and Ganiyu Dawodu.In 1968 he was appointed Minister of Water and Electricity.In 1968 he was appointed to the Bench of the Supreme Court, holding the post until his retirement in 1972.In 1968, he was appointed to the University of Michigan Board of Regents, then won election, and served from 1969 to 1975.In 1968, he was a reporter and photographer for the 1st Infantry Division newspaper in Vietnam.In 1968, he was a student at the Victoria University of Wellington and while in Mexico at the Summer Olympics, he was awarded VUW Sportsman of the Year, which was presented to his father.In 1968 he was awarded a one-year Fulbright Scholarship to undertake postdoctoral studies with Takeru Higuchi at the University of Kansas, USA in the field of solution thermodynamics.In 1968, he was commissioned into the 1st Reconnaissance Regiment, Ceylon Armored Corps as a second lieutenant.In 1968, he was elected as Chairman of the Derry Citizens' Action Committee, and was subsequently prominent in the Derry Citizens' Defense Association and chaired the Derry Citizens' Central Council.In 1968 he was elected as member of Agra Municipal Corporation as BJS candidate and become leader of the party in the house.In 1968, he was elected district court judge for the 7th Judicial District of NC and appointed chief judge by Chief Justice R. Hunt Parker.In 1968 he was elected to the Tasmanian Legislative Council as the independent member for Macquarie.In 1968, he was found dead from a gunshot wound in his cabin by a friend.In 1968 he was in a duo with Gosney Thornton called Tom and Billy.In 1968 he was joined by young Scottish banjo player Davey Johnstone; they toured as Murf & Shaggis for two years until they added double bass player Ron Chesterman (formerly of Strawbs) to the line-up and changed their name to Draft Porridge.In 1968, he was loaned to Lecce and he remained there for two years, then he was loaned again to Savona and Spezia.In 1968, he was named eastern vice-president for the Progressive Conservative party and president of the party association and became interim leader for the party in 1969.In 1968, he was named Poet Laureate of New Hampshire.In 1968 he was ordained to the episcopate as the Bishop of the Northern Territory, a position he held until 1983.In 1968 he was paroled from prison.In 1968, he was part of the British men's wheelchair basketball team which finished third.In 1968, he was part of the cross-arts team which created the Christophe Plantin Prize.In 1968, he was singing in Washington Square Park, New York City, when he was heard one day by Bernard Stollman, the head of ESP-Disk.In 1968, he was sold to Leicester City and appeared in their 1969 FA Cup Final 1-0 defeat against Manchester City.In 1968 he was the coxswain of the East German boat which won the silver medal in the coxed fours event.
