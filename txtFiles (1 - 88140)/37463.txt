In 1986, he was appointed to the Canadian Cabinet as Minister of Labor, and in 1989, was moved in a cabinet shuffle to Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development.In 1986, he was appointed to the High Court.In 1986 he was appointed to The Wall Street Journal's editorial board.In 1986, he was arrested in Portugal for drug trafficking and jailed for four years.In 1986, he was awarded an Honorary Doctor of Music degree from Berklee College of Music, where he currently serves on the Board of Trustees.In 1986 he was awarded Doctor of Sciences degree in "Theoretical and Mathematical Physics".In 1986, he was awarded the Francqui Prize on Human Sciences.In 1986 he was awarded the Lester Randolph Ford Award.In 1986, he was awarded the Padma Bhushan, India's third highest civilian award, given Government of India.In 1986, he was co-curator of "Falls the Shadow" with Jon Thompson at the Hayward Gallery.In 1986, he was conferred the Arjuna Award for his contribution to the Indian volleyball.In 1986 he was conferred with an honorary Doctor of Divinity Degree by Pillsbury Baptist Bible College, Owatonna, Minnesota.In 1986, he was constructing an album with a select group of musicians to work on his project.In 1986, he was convicted of accepting $57,000 in bribes.In 1986 he was Davenport Visiting Professor at Yale University.In 1986, he was designated Master Artist by the Five Civilized Tribes Museum.
