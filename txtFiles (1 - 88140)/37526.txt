In 1991, she appeared in Ron Howard's Backdraft, and in 1992 she acted in the drama-thriller Single White Female.In 1991, she appeared with Dawn French in Ben Elton's Silly Cow, at the Theater Royal Haymarket.In 1991, she became a diplomat with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Estonia.In 1991, she became an assistant professor at Ohio State University.In 1991, she became a Police Services Advisor for the Ontario Ministry of the Solicitor General and Correctional Services.In 1991, she became a presenter for the second series of the Saturday morning children's TV program, The 8:15 from Manchester.In 1991 she became only the third woman to become a fellow of the Institution of Civil Engineers and in 2002 she was elected a fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering.In 1991, she became president of the Washington Bullets team, a member of the National Basketball Association (NBA); then 29 years old, she was the first female president of an NBA franchise, and one of the first women to hold the top front-office position for a major league sports team in North America.In 1991 she became secretary general of the Young Socialists.In 1991, she became the first black woman elected to Dayton's city commission, on which she served until 2004.In 1991, she became the first Hispanic woman to be elected to the Texas House of Representatives; she was reelected in 1993, 1995 and 1997.In 1991, she became the first woman to manage a USAP station when she was appointed manager of the Palmer Station on Anvers Island in 1991.In 1998, Peoples Rocks off the coast of Anvers Island was named after Ann Peoples.In 1991, she began another work, The Eyes of the Goddess, but it was unfinished at the time of her death.In 1991 she began using her mother's maiden name, Ashton, when she recorded her first single, in order to distinguish herself from CCM singer Kim Hill.In 1991, she broke the 50-meter record and received a gold medal.In 1991, she co-edited a work entitled Aboriginal Resource Use in Canada: Historical and Legal Aspects.In 1991, she collaborated with British choreographer Siobhan Davies, writing and recording a poem cycle for a ballet that opened in the Southbank Center in London.In 1991, she completed her Ph.D at Liverpool with lexicographical studies of Egyptian hieroglyphs.In 1991 she established a research center at Monash University, which later moved to La Trobe University.In 1991, she exhibited at the American International Toy Fair in New York City, where her doll "Pansy" won a Dolls Award for Excellence.In 1991 she formed her own band - The Deirdre Cartwright Group.In 1991, she founded Pacific Actuarial Consultants, of which she served as president until 2001.In 1991 she founded the Chicago Interfaith Committee on Worker Issues.In 1991, she founded the City of Refuge under the United Church of Christ, "in order to unite a gospel ministry with a social ministry".In 1991, she founded the Egg Donor Program, to help infertile couples.In 1991 she founded the Emirates Environmental Group, which she continues to chair.
