In the 15th century, Sultan Husayn Mirza Bayqarah built the current Blue Mosque here.In the 15th Century, the canons of Oseney Abbey attended lectures at Oxford University.In the 15th century the church was lengthened to the west and heightened for the second time with Brick.In the 15th century, the Gothic church Beatae Mariae Virginis was built on the former castle site.In the 15th century the heavy losses from the Wars of the Roses at the top of society, followed in the 16th by much more rapid economic growth and the English Reformation, after which fewer clergy worked in administrative roles, and which liberated in the Dissolution of the Monasteries vast quantities of assets for the agile nobility and gentry to acquire, all worked to greatly increase upwards mobility at the top of society.In the 15th century, the house Limburg-Styrum was divided into Limburg-Broich, controlling Limburg-Hohenlimburg, and Limburg-Styrum who kept the lordship Styrum.In the 15th century, the King's Inch site was rebuilt as Inch Castle by Sir John Ross.In the 15th century, the manor was held by the Catesby family who were probably responsible for clearing the settlement, for by 1505, the records show that there were no tenants.In the 15th century the marquesses remained substantially autonomous, thanks to the support of the Visconti and later the Sforza of Milan.In the 15th century the nave was given a new roof and the north aisle was added around the same time.In the 15th century the term mtavari was applied only to the five ruling princes of western Georgia (Samtskhe, Mingrelia, Guria, Svaneti, and Abkhazia), whose autonomous powers were finally eliminated under Imperial Russia.
