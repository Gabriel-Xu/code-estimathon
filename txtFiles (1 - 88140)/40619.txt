In the past, he represented a number of political parties; currently he was with Manipur People's Party .In the past he sat on the boards of AdMob, Clearwell Systems, eMeter, Karma, and PeakStream.In the past, he served as a mechanic for [Mansion Motorsports ], Spears Motorsports, and Travis Carter Motorsports.In the past, he served as an aide to Senator Gerald Cardinale on insurance and economic matters and was a teacher for the North Bergen School District from 2004 to 2006.In the past, he served as a Professor of History at the University of Southern California, Executive Director of the USC Shoah Foundation Institute for Visual History and Education, and President of the Chicago Historical Society.In the past, he served as a regular columnist for Discipleship Journal, and is the general editor of the Zondervan Exegetical Commentary Series.In the past he served as conductor of the Israel Chamber Orchestra.In the past, he served on the Budget and Finance Committee, Administration Committee and Human Services Board.In the past, he served on the council of the American College of Neuropsychopharmacology and has chaired the NIMH Psychopathology and Psychobiology Review Committee.In the past he signed deals with major labels such as Universal Music Group, CNR Records in Belgium & ZYX Music records in Germany.In the past, he was a board member of the Song Room, board member of the Zoos Victoria Foundation, board member of the Petstock Foundation, and chairman of the board of CAPRA (Child Abuse Prevention Research Australia) @ Monash University, and a member of The Rees Advisory Group.In the past, he was a contributor to the Daily Kos.In the past he was a Goldwater Scholar, a Marshall Scholar and a Chandra Fellow.In the past he was a lecturer and economics theory researcher at AUBG, Justus Liebig University, Central Michigan University and Middle Tennessee State University.In the past, he was also known in the music scene for his works as a freelance graphic designer under Soundmind Graphics, and until his 2006 move to Canada, Jeff owned and ran his own home-based recording studio, Vision Studio, in Jakarta.In the past, he was a member of the Vlaams Belang party.In the past, he was among others chair of the Reformed Political Party election committee in The Hague, a member of the board of directors of the reformed teachers' college Driestar Hogeschool at Gouda, and an elder as well as chair of the diaconate in the Dutch Reformed Bethlehemkerk at The Hague.
