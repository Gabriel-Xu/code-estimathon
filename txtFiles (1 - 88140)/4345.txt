The name came from the December to Dismember event which was held by the original Extreme Championship Wrestling in 1995. Lashley won the match and the championship after he hit Big Show with a spear and pinned him.The pay-per-view had an attendance of 4,800 and 90,000 people payed to see the event, with 55,000 of the buys being domestic.
