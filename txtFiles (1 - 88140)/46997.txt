It was the home of James Mitchell Rogers, a prominent Winston-Salem businessman, and his son, Francis Mitchell Rogers, chief chemist of Standard Oil Company of Indiana.It was the home of James Willis Hamer, farmer, state representative, and state senator of Dillon County during its first half-century.It was the home of Jessamyn West during her childhood.It was the home of Joel Smith, who was an influential planter, merchant, banker, and supporter of industries and railroads.It was the home of John A. Lentz (1860-1925), who served as Hickory's mayor in 1911-1912.It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1985.
