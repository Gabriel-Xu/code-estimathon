Jabobson began his political career as Mayor of Tuktoyaktuk, during his tenure he was forced to deal with problems caused by erosion of the coast line and damage to the community from the Beaufort Sea.Jabodetabek have been steadily extended, especially since Jokowi became governor, then replaced by Ahok, his deputy who made more improvement of the TransJakarta system with adding more busses and new feeder lines.Jabodetabek refers to Jakarta metropolitan area, which is formed by combining the first syllables of Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, (Greater) Tangerang and Bekasi, which also represent Commuter line's operational area.Jabodon is a historic vacation resort in the Town of Washington, Vilas County, Wisconsin.Jabodon's main house, an American Craftsman house, was constructed in 1924; its boathouse was likely built in the same year.Jabodon was added to the National Register of Historic Places on March 25, 2009.
