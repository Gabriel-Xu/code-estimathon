Kalinga Bharati Resudential College (KBRC) is a residential institute in Pirabazzar, Cuttack, India.Kalinga Ethnoarchaeological Project (KEP), based in the Cordillera Mountains of the Philippines, was one of the longest-running ethnoarchaeological projects in the world.Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT), formerly KIIT University is an institute of higher education located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India.Kalinga Institute of Social Sciences (KISS) is a residential institute for tribal people based in Bhubaneswar, India.
