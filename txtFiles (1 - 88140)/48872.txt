Kuku Nyungkal (also spelled Kuku Njunggal, Guugu Nyungkul, Gugu Njunggal) is an Australian Aboriginal language and the language of the Kuku Nyungkal people of Far North Queensland.Kukuom is a town in the Brong Ahafo Region of Ghana.Kukup became more prosperous when the famous Singapore Arab Syed Mohamed bin Ahmed Alsagoff (also known as Nong Chik) obtained the approval of the Sultan of Johor to develop the southwest coast of Johor.Kukup District was replaced with "Pontian District" when the main road linking Pontian and Johor Bahru was completed in 1900.Kukup is a small fishing village in Pontian District, Johor, Malaysia.Kukup Island (Malay: Pulau Kukup) is an island in Pontian District, Johor, Malaysia.Kukup was rapidly developed under Constantinople Estate owned by Tuan Syed Mohamed Alsagoff.Kukurahtsevo is a village in Petrich Municipality, in Blagoevgrad Province, Bulgaria.Kukurantumi is a town in the East Akim District of the Eastern Region of Ghana, noted for Adonten Senior High School and also the St. Paul's Technical Institute.Kukurha is a village in West Champaran district in the Indian state of Bihar.Kukur is a village and a former municipality in the Elbasan County, central Albania.Kukurna is a settlement in Kenya's Rift Valley Province.Kukuryak is a village in Kirkovo Municipality, Kardzhali Province, southern Bulgaria.Kukutesvara Tank is associated with the Kukutesvara Siva temple of Bhubaneswar.Kuku-Thaypan is an extinct Paman language spoken on the southwestern part of the Cape York Peninsula, Queensland in Australia, by the Kuku-Thaypan people.Kuku was one of two major Kabyle kingdoms, the other being the Kingdom of Ait Abbas.Kukwa is Lake Erie's first ever student-athlete to sign an NFL contract.
