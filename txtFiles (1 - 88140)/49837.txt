Literally, "Mahamandaleshwar" means "Superior of great and/or numerous monasteries" or "Superior of a religious district or province" (maha = great, mandala = district, ishwara = head, sovereign).Literally, makan bajamba has a good philosophy; it brings a sense of togetherness without seeing the status of its people.Literally meaning "do it yourself", the DIY ethic promotes the idea that anyone is capable of performing a variety of tasks rather than relying on paid specialists.Literally meaning "falling off", the concept is described first in chapter 9 of the classical Jain text, Tattvartha Sutra (True nature of Reality) written by Acharya Umasvati, in 2nd century CE, the only text authoritative in both Svetambara and Digambara sects of Jainism.Literally meaning Grand River, this channel is now part of Kali Krukut (Krukut river) in Tambora subdistrict.Literally meaning "heather island" in Scottish Gaelic, it is a common Scottish island name.
