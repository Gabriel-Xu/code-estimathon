Nauru House (also called 80 Collins Street) is a landmark 52-storey building located in the Melbourne CBD, Victoria, Australia.Nauru International Airport currently serves 8 international destinations, with Nauru's national Airline, Nauru Airlines.Nauru is an island nation in Micronesia.Nauru is a small island country in the South Pacific.Nauru is one of 35 countries where Scouting exists (be it embryonic or widespread) but where there is no national Scout organization which is yet a member of the World Organization of the Scout Movement.Nauru is part of the Commonwealth of Nations, but Queen Elizabeth II is not Nauru's head of state.Nauru is renowned particularly for weightlifting; all its medals in the Commonwealth Games have been achieved in that field.Nauru is scheduled to participate at the 2018 Summer Youth Olympics in Buenos Aires, Argentina from 6 October to 18 October 2018.Nauru itself is symbolized by a white 12-pointed star.Nauru listed 130 competitors as of 4 July 2015.
