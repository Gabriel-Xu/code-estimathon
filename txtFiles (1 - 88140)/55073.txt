Originally released January 10, 1990, it features a wide array of guest musicians, including then-former Black Sabbath band member Ozzy Osbourne.Originally released locally on Alberta-based independent label Western Famine, the album was later reissued across Canada with distribution by Arts & Crafts.Originally Released March 2010 by Streetcleaner Records/Six Feet Under Records and later rereleased by Southern Lord Records.Originally released November 1, 2005, as a part of the Hipeponymous box-set, it was released separately on November 8, 2005.Originally released on 12 July 1999 as the second single from his fourth album, White Ladder, it was re-released as the fourth single on 12 June 2000.Originally released on 14 September 2009 in the UK on SL Records, it was re-released on Absolutely Kosher Records in 2011 in the US.
