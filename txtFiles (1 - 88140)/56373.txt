Pillaiyar Theru Kadaisi Veedu (English: The Last House of Pillayar Street) is a 2011 Tamil-language romantic drama film written and directed by Thirumalai Kishore and starring Githan Ramesh as a T. Rajendar fan.Pillalamarri is an 700-year-old banyan tree located in Mahabubnagar (Old name Palamuru), Telangana, India.Pillanallur is a panchayat town in Namakkal district in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu.
