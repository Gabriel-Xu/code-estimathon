She has participated representing Spain in the Chess Olympics once, in 1990 in Novi Sad.She has participated three times at the European Figure Skating Championships.She has participated three times at the Sanremo Music Festival, in 1995, in 1996 and in 2001, resulting at the first, third and second place respectively.She has Pashtoon, Indian and Russian origin, and she is the first Afghan/Russian model to be featured in Miss England quarter finals.She has penned numerous full-length plays, including Summer Cyclone, Farm Boys, and Nothing Revolutionary, as well as having written a good number of one-acts as well.She has penned over 700 songs, and worked with Carl Weathersby and Dave Specter.She has penned songs for artists such as Shakira, Pitbull, Chris Brown, Jennifer Lopez, LeToya Luckett and Keyshia Cole.She has performed across most of the world, including the United States, United Kingdom, Italy, Japan, Germany, Colombia, Greece, The Netherlands and Poland, and has associated with the Philharmonia, the London Philharmonic and Romanian Philharmonic orchestras as well as all six major Australian symphonies.She has performed across the United States and in France, Germany, Italy, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.She has performed all over Africa from Selam festival in Ethiopia to Big brother Africa numerous times in South Africa.She has performed all over New England including the Hard Rock Cafe and the Fox Theater at Foxwoods Casino.She has performed all over the world, from Puerto Rico to Egypt, and Costa Rica to Greece.She has performed along rap acts such as The Roots, Jay-Z, and Blackalicious.She has performed alongside her husband in various Mike Hammer-related television shows, including Mickey Spillane's Mike Hammer, The New Mike Hammer and, most recently, Mike Hammer, Private Eye as the yoga instructor Maya Ricci.She has performed and choreographed for film and television and presented hundreds of concerts throughout India, North America, Asia, Africa, the United Kingdom.She has performed and recorded as a solo artist and as one half of Minor Alps with Matthew Caws of Nada Surf.She has performed and recorded for many record labels, under many aliases.
