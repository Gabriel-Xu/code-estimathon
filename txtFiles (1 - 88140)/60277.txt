She is best known as a fell runner in Great Britain, but better known as a sky runner elsewhere after her victory in the 2016 Skyrunner World Series (Sky Extreme) and bronze medal at the 2016 Skyrunning World Championships (Sky Ultra).She is best known as a first-generation feminist, and author of the novel Up The Sandbox (1970), which was filmed as a starring vehicle for Barbra Streisand in 1972.She is best known as a former interviewer for TNA Wrestling, Maxim Magazine, the reality show Beauty and the Geek and her Playboy magazine appearance in July 2008.She is best known as a former member of a British four-piece girl group Neon Jungle.She is best known as a former member of the American pop/R&B girl group and dance ensemble the Pussycat Dolls.
