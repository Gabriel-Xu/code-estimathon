She is the twin sister of Peter Beale (Thomas Law, Ben Hardy), and has three half siblings, older brother Steven Beale (Edward Savage, Aaron Sidwell), younger sister Cindy Williams (Eva Sayer, Mimi Keene) and younger brother Bobby Beale (Kevin Curran, Alex Francis, Rory Stroud, Eliot Carrington).She is the twin sister of Phillipa Towler-Green and the daughter of Diane Towler, who also coached her.She is the twin sister of Prince Vincent.She is the twin sister of singer Jill Vidal.She is the twin sister of Victoria Reznik.She is the two-time winner of the International Music Festival in Bucharest, Romania where she also served as an English speaking television commentator.She is the Uganda Ambassador to Italy.She is the UK Government's advisor on obesity in the United Kingdom.She is the UK Manager overseeing the Slough and Swindon branches of the fictitious paper merchant, Wernham-Hogg.She is the UK representative of the International Pole Federation (IPF), the European representative of the International Pole Fitness Federation (IPFF) and a member of the International Dance Council (CID UNESCO).
