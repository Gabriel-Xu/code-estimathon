She was an underworld Titan-goddess who assisted Jove in the Titanomachy and was therefore able to keep her powers.She was an unpaid advisor to the Hillary for America campaign.She was an unpledged superdelegate in the 2016 Democratic Presidential primary, and announced her support for Hillary Clinton.She was an unsuccessful 2012 Democratic nominee for U.S. Representative for Iowa's 4th congressional district.She was an unsuccessful candidate for a seat in the provincial assembly in 1986 before being elected in 1989.She was an unsuccessful candidate for Congress in 2016.She was an unsuccessful candidate for Governor of Tennessee in the 2018 Tennessee gubernatorial election.She was an unsuccessful candidate for Multnomah County commissioner in 2008.She was an unsuccessful candidate for the Michigan State University Board of Trustees in 2002.Emmons is not related to State Senator Judy Emmons.She was an unsuccessful candidate for the Senate seat held by Hollis French in 2012 and for the Anchorage Assembly in 2010.She was an unsuccessful candidate for United States House of Representatives in Virginia's 2nd congressional district in 2000.She was an unsuccessful candidate for Washington County commissioner in 2006.She was an unsuccessful candidate in the Rochester and Strood by-election, 2014, and the London Assembly election, 2016.She was an unsuccessful City Council candidate on mayoral candidate Louis Manzo's ticket in the 2009 Jersey City municipal election.She was an unsuccessful senate candidate for Victoria in the 2016 Australian federal election.She was a nurse and hospital matron in the Civil War.She was anxious to send word about her plans to Pretich.She was a one-day specialist but has managed smaller stage races.She was a one time editor of the Badminton Gazette and a well known Lawn Tennis player and became a professional tennis coach.She was a onetime trustee of the Guggenheim Museum.
