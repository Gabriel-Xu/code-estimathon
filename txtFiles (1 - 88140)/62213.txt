South Norwood has been the home of Spurgeon's College, a world-famous Baptist theological college, since 1923; Spurgeon's is located on South Norwood Hill and currently has some 1000 students.South Norwood is a ward in the London Borough of Croydon in the United Kingdom.South Norwood Leisure Center is a leisure center located on Portland Road in South Norwood in Croydon, London.South Norwood Library is a public library in South Norwood, South London.South Nottinghamshire Academy is a specialist school for both sports and mathematics.South Nottinghamshire, formally the "Southern Division of Nottinghamshire" was a county constituency represented in the House of Commons of the Parliament of the United Kingdom.South Notts Bus Company was a bus company operating in Nottinghamshire from 1926 until 1991 when sold to Nottingham City Transport who continue to use the brand name today.South Nowra is a suburb of Nowra in the City of Shoalhaven in New South Wales, Australia.South Nyack is a village incorporated in 1878 in the town of Orangetown in Rockland County, New York, in the United States.South Oak Bay is a neighborhood located in the Municipality of Oak Bay, British Columbia, to the south of Oak Bay Avenue and lying east of the boundary between Oak Bay and Victoria, British Columbia.
