St. Peter's has many historical associations, with the Early Christian Church, the Papacy, the Protestant Reformation and Catholic Counter-reformation and numerous artists, especially Michelangelo.St Peter's has six bells.St Peter's Hospital is a former hospital in Henrietta Street, Covent Garden, London, which is a grade II listed building.St Peter's in the City is a Church of England parish church in the city of Derby, UK.St. Peter's is affiliated with the Christian Brothers of St. John Baptist de la Salle, and is a member of the CHSAA.St Peter's is an area of Broadstairs, a town on the Isle of Thanet in Kent.St. Peter's is an Ottawa landmark.St. Peter's is a parish of the Episcopal Diocese of Tennessee.St Peter's is a secondary school in Huntingdon, Cambridgeshire.St Peters is a suburb in the Inner West of Sydney, in the state of New South Wales, Australia.St. Peter's is known for raising the most money for the Terry Fox Campaign, reaching 76, 000 $ in 2015.St. Peter's is one of the four churches in the world that hold the rank of Major Basilica, all four of which are in Rome.St Peter's is the home of The Sir Tom Cowie Campus at the University of Sunderland on the north bank of the River Wear.St. Peter's is the oldest of the 36 Roman Catholic parishes on Staten Island, having been established in 1839, before the second-oldest St. Mary's (1852) and the third-oldest St. Joseph's (1855).St. Peter's Kierch was used regularly until 1879 when a large church was completed.
