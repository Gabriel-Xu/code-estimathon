The 15th European Badminton Championships were held in Herning, Denmark, between 13 and 20 April 1996, and hosted by the European Badminton Union and the Danmarks Badminton Forbund.The 15th European Cross Country Championships were held at Brussels in Belgium on 14 December 2008.The 15th European Film Awards were presented on December 7, 2002 in Rome, Italy.The 15th European Women's Artistic Gymnastics Championships were held in Helsinki.The 15th fashion show featured some of the new Angels and also the returning Angels.The 15th festival in 2011 reached 35,000.To reduce the time in between the festivals the organizer started the Wintercircus in 2001 and 2002 which took place in February.The 15th Festival ran from 29 November to 7 December 2014.
