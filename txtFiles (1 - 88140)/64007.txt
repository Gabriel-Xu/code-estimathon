The 1994 European motorcycle Grand Prix was the last round of the 1994 Grand Prix motorcycle racing season.The 1994 European Parliamentary Election was a European election held across the 12 European Union member states in June 1994.The 1994 European Parliament election in Ireland was the Irish component of the 1994 European Parliament election.The 1994 European Parliament election in Spain was held on Sunday, 12 June 1994, as part of the EU-wide election to elect the 4th European Parliament.The 1994 European Promotion Cup for Men was the 4th edition of this tournament.The 1994 European Seniors Tour was the 3rd season of the European Seniors Tour, the professional golf tour for men aged 50 and above operated by the PGA European Tour.The 1994 European Tour was the 23rd official season of golf tournaments known as the PGA European Tour.The 1994 event in particular had a lot of talent as Susan Tedeschi, Michael Burks (who won the Albert King Guitar Award) and a 16 year old Sean Costello competed, although none of them were the eventual winner.The 1994 FA Cup Final was the 49th FA Cup final to be held since the Second World War and was contested between Manchester United and Chelsea.
