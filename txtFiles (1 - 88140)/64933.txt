The album's touring cycle lasted two years, ending in late 2009, and visited several countries and music festivals worldwide.The album's tour was documented in Jonathan Demme's film Stop Making Sense, which generated a live album of the same name.The album's track list features few instrumentals, a trait of most P-Square albums.The album's track listing and artwork were revealed on 14 October 2010.The album's track listing and cover art was released on April 1, 2015.
