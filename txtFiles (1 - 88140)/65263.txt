The Apples in Stereo, styled as The Apples in stereo, are an American rock band associated with Elephant Six Collective, a group of bands also including Neutral Milk Hotel, The Olivia Tremor Control, Elf Power, Of Montreal, and Circulatory System.The Apples were an indie-dance band from Scotland in the early 1990s.The Apple symbol is a morphed into an "O", which is followed by a logo for Barack Obama's presidential campaign website.The apple takes its name from the village of Sturmer, Essex.The apple tentiform leafminer (Phyllonorycter malella) is a moth of the Gracillariidae family.The Apple Thunderbolt Display is a 27-inch flat panel computer monitor sold by Apple Inc. from July 2011 to June 2016.The Appleton Area School District serves 16,281 students in 15 elementary schools, 3 middle schools, 3 high schools and 14 recognized charter schools.The Appleton Baronetcy, of South Benfleet in the County of Essex, was a title in the Baronetage of England.The Appleton Farm is a historic farmstead on Hancock Road in Dublin, New Hampshire.The Appleton Institute is a multidisciplinary research hub residing within the Adelaide campus of CQUniversity Australia, located south of the Adelaide CBD, in Wayville, South Australia.The Appleton Institute was formed from the research group Professor Dawson first established at the University of Adelaide in 1992.The Appleton Log Hall is a meeting hall in Appleton, a community in Klickitat County, Washington, United States.The Appleton Museum of Art houses a permanent collection of around 18,000 works including art and artifacts representative of European, American, Asian, African, Contemporary and pre-Columbian styles.The Appleton Museum of Art is an art museum located in Ocala, Florida.The Appleton Papermakers were a minor league baseball team located in Appleton, Wisconsin.The Appleton School is a secondary school and sixth form with academy status located in Croft Road, Benfleet, Essex, England.The Appleton series was issued as "two volumes or four divisions"; it was reprinted in London by J.S. Virtue & Co., simply published as four volumes.The Appleton Stakes is an American Thoroughbred horse race held annually near the end of March at Gulfstream Park in Hallandale Beach, Florida.The Appleton Transit Center is a bus terminus in downtown Appleton, Wisconsin, located at 100 East Washington Street (on the corner of Washington Street and Oneida Street).The Appleton Water Tower is a Victorian water tower located in Sandringham, Norfolk.
