The book was first published through Shaye Areheart Books on September 26, 2006, and has subsequently been re-printed through Broadway Books.The book was first published under this title in 1933 by Macmillan and Co., containing color plates and pen illustrations by Stuart Tresilian.The book was first released as a hardcover in the US on January 6, 2004.The book was first released at the New Delhi World Book Fair in February 2014.The book was first released in 1942 at Maharaja College.The book was first released in 1987 through Troll Associates, later winning the 1988 Scott O'Dell Award for Historical Fiction.The book was first released in 2005, was later released as an "updated and expanded" edition in 2006, and was yet again released with additional updates in 2007 as "further updated and expanded: Release 3.0".
