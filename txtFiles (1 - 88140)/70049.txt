The first book, Cinderella Stays Late, was published in 2014.The first book containing monsters to be published was the Heroic Tier adventure Keep on the Shadowfell, followed closely with the release of the first set of "core" rulebooks.The first book contains the reasons why he became a half-vampire and what he had to give up in order to save his ex-best friend, as well as introducing the character.The first book covers notable events such as World War I, the Russian Revolution, and the struggle for women's suffrage.
