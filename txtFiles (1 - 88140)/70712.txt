The game features mental problems, such as math questions, in order to keep the user's brain active; in order to answer the questions, the player must perform various physical motions.The game features Michael Jordan in his first official appearance in the series.The game features more than 100 different options to customize a boat, and features 4 player mode.The game features more than 400 levels, and players are able to download new levels and upload their own custom-created levels to share online.The game features more than 40 popular licensed songs, many of them cover versions recorded for the game, spanning five decades (from the 1960s to the 2000s).The game features more than 50 prehistoric animals, especially dinosaurs and Pleistocene mammals.
