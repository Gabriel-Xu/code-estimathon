The Gulf Labor Coalition or Gulf Labor (also Gulf Labor) is the name of a coalition of artists and activists founded in 2011 and based in New York, United States, organized to bring awareness to issues surrounding the living and working conditions of migrant laborers responsible for building the Guggenheim Abu Dhabi, Louver Abu Dhabi, and Sheik Zayed Palace Museum on Abu Dhabi's Saadiyat Island, United Arab Emirates, along with other buildings on the island including a New York University Abu Dhabi campus.The Gulflander is an Australian passenger train operated by Queensland Rail on the isolated Normanton to Croydon line in the Gulf Country of northern Queensland.The Gulf Line Railway was a railroad in the U.S. state of Georgia, connecting Hawkinsville and Camilla.The Gulf logperch (Percina suttkusi) is a small freshwater fish located in North America.The Gulf, Mobile and Northern Railroad (reporting mark GMN) was a railroad in the Southern United States.
