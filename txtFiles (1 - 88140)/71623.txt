The house was declared a national momument in 1972 by the National Commission for Museums and Monuments.The house was declared an Historic Landmark Property by the City of Independence in 1994.The house was declared as a heritage house by the National Historical Commission on July 10, 2010.The house was declared a U.S. National Historic Landmark in 1965.The house was deconstructed and rebuilt on Old Sudbury Road in Lincoln in 2005.It was built in 1845 and added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1986.The house was deeded for use as a library in 1956, and served that purpose into the late-1970s when it again became a residence.
