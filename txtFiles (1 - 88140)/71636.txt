The Howard Baronetcy, of Great Rissington in the County of Gloucester, was created in the Baronetage of the United Kingdom on 1 December 1955 for Seymour Howard, Lord Mayor of London in 1954.The Howard Bison are the intercollegiate athletic teams representing Howard University in Washington, DC.The Howard Bison football team represents Howard University in college football.The Howard Bison women's basketball team represents Howard University in women's basketball.The Howard Bison won their second national title (their first title in 1971 was vacated by the NCAA) by defeating the two-time defending champion Saint Louis Billikens, 2-1 after four overtimes.The Howard Block, also known as the Central Block, is a commercial building located at 201-205 Huron Avenue in Port Huron, Michigan.The Howard Center for Family, Religion and Society is an socially conservative American think-tank and advocacy group that opposes abortion, divorce, and homosexuality, promoting instead the "child-rich, married parent" family..The Howard Center is an independent, non-sectarian organization.The Howard College campus in Durban was strung out along the Berea, a ridge to the north of the Durban city center.The Howard County Center of African American Culture is located in Columbia, Maryland.
