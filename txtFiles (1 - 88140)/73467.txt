The Men's combined road race / time trial cycling events at the 2004 Summer Paralympics were held at Vouliagmeni between 24 & 27 September.The men's combined was an artistic gymnastics event held as part of the Gymnastics at the 1904 Summer Olympics program.The men's competition and women's competition were merged and held in the same venue from 1987 onwards.The men's competition, held from 15 to 26 February, was won by Sweden, and the women's competition, held from 11 to 20 February, was won by Canada.The men's competition in the bantamweight (56 kg) division was staged on 10 November 1998.The men's competition in the light-heavyweight (85 kg) division was staged on 13 November 1998.The men's competition in the lightweight (69 kg) division was staged on 12 November 1998.The men's competition in the middle-heavyweight (94 kg) division was staged on 14 November 1998.The men's competition in the middleweight (77 kg) division was staged on 12 November 1998.The men's competition in the super heavyweight division (+105 kg) was staged on 15 November 1998.The men's competition is an under-22 level competition (born on or after January 1, 1997), with each team allowed up to three overage players.The men's competition of the High diving events at the 2013 World Aquatics Championships was held on 29 and 31 July 2013.The men's competition of the high diving events at the 2015 World Aquatics Championships was held on 3 and 5 August 2015.The men's competitions consist of three major divisions across the State of Tasmania.The men's competitions were held on Thursday, February 4, 1932, Friday, February 5, 1932, Saturday, February 6, 1932, and on Monday, February 8, 1932.The men's competition was an under-22 event, with each team supplemented with up to three over age players.
