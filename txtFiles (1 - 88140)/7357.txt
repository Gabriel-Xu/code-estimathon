Edwin "Ed" Meese, III (born December 2, 1931) is an American attorney, law professor, and author.Meese was born on December 2, 1931 in Oakland, California. He studied at Yale University and at the University of California-Berkeley.He served under Ronald Reagan's administration back when Reagan was Governor (1967–1974), the Reagan Presidential Transition Team (1980), and under Reagan's presidential administration (1981–1985).
