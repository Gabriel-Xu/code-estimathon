The motherhouse and novitiate are located in Round Top, New York, in the Catskill Mountains, with additional houses in Melville, New York, and White Bear Lake, Minnesota, where they operate schools and are involved in various forms of charity work.The motherhouse at Paderborn was reopened and the activities of the Sisters was extended with their former success throughout Germany.The Motherhouse currently houses more than 200 sisters, more than 100 of whom require supportive care.The motherhouse for the Eastern province is located in Mendham, New Jersey, and the motherhouse of the Western province is located in Wilmette, Illinois.
