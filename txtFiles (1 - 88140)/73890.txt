The movie was remade in Tamil as Veetula Raman Veliyila Krishnan, Kannada as Maneli Ramanna Beedheeli Kamanna (1983) and in Hindi as Ghar Mein Ram Gali Mein Shyam (1988) with Govinda, Neelam Kothari and Anupam Kher.The movie was remade in Tamil in 2000 as Sudhandhiram.The movie was remade in Tamil in 2004 as Kuththu starring Silambarasan and Divya Spandana.The movie was remade in Telugu as Jeevana Teeralu.The movie was remade in Telugu as Nagavalli with Venkatesh Daggubati.
