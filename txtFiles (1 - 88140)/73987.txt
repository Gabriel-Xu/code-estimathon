perceived as an anti-war piece at a time when the US war in Vietnam was sinking in popularity.The musical was performed at the La Jolla Playhouse in San Diego in September 1995, and the Goodman Comunale Florence.The musical was performed at the Tennessee Performing Arts Center in Nashville to some very positive reviews.The musical was performed between July 3 and August 10, 2014, at Stage 773 in Chicago, Illinois.The musical was performed between July 6 and August 7, 2016, at Stage 773 in Chicago, Illinois.The musical was performed between March 11 and April 3, 2016, in Los Angeles, California.The musical was performed February 11-23rd, 2011, at the Hoover-Leppen Theater in Chicago, Illinois (this was the first StarKid stage production to not be performed on the University of Michigan campus).The musical was performed May 14-16, 2010, on the University of Michigan campus.The musical was popular with amateur theater groups, particularly in Britain, from 1911 through the 1950s.The musical was praised by critics for the cast performances, particularly York, and the innovative use of stilts and live dogs.The musical was premiered for the Royal National Theater in London in 2013.The musical was presented by Jacobsen Entertainment and David Atkins Enterprises.The musical was presented from March 31 to May 20, 2007 at the Kirk Douglas Theater of the Center Theater Group in Los Angeles, California as a co-production with Deaf West Theater.The musical was presented Off-Broadway in 1997.The musical was produced at the Arena Stage in Washington, D.C. in May to June 1981, again directed by Ashman and starring Coffin.The musical was produced by George Grossmith, Jr. and Edward Laurillard.The musical was produced by Lore Noto.The musical was produced by Michael Frazier and Fred Walker, and the subsequent musical cast album was produced by Quincy Jones.The musical was produced by Ray Stark, who was Brice's son-in-law via his marriage to her daughter Frances, and starred Barbra Streisand.The musical was produced in London in 1933 and received its US premiere in 1982.The musical was produced in Philadelphia by the Philadelphia Drama Guild in 1986 and has been
