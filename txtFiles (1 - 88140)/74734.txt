The older lighthouses at Punta Cumplida and Fuencaliente are situated at the northern and southern points respectively.The older line began running trains in 1803, and was a plateway of about 4 feet gage, with horse traction, for the purpose of bringing minerals from the Mynydd Mawr to the sea for onward shipment at Llanelly Docks.The older line of the counts of Nassau-Hadamar existed from 1303 to 1394; the younger line existed from 1607 to 1711 and received the hereditary title of prince in 1652.The older, longer Gaelic name is Lagan Achaidh Droma, "hollow at the field of the ridge".The older Macedo is best known as a slave owner whose cruelty provoked some of his slaves to murder him in his baths.The older main road to Skien, Larvik, and Oslo, passes through Siljan.
