The plumage of the males includes characteristic grossly elongated flank plumes (which emerge from beneath the wings and strictly speaking are flank plumes pectoral plumes), and a pair of wire-like feathers emerging from the end of the tail.The plumage of the Rennell starling is blackish with a green-blue gloss.The plumage of the sexes is similar, but the casque of the male is larger than that of the female.
