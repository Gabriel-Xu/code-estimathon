The population of Opitsat Indian Reserve No. 1, which is named after the village and is an official land status used by Statistics Canada as a census area, was 174 at the Census of 2006.The population of Opua West and Opua East combined was 612 in the 2006 Census, an increase of 60 from 2001.The car ferry across the Bay of Islands, the main tourist access to Russell, runs between Opua and Okiato.The population of Orford greatly increases during the summer months due to its flourishing sailing club.The population of Oru is over 5,200, and Lude over 4,700.The population of Paihia was 1770 in the 2006 Census, a decrease of 69 from 2001.The population of Pakistanis in Jordan, according to the Overseas Pakistanis Foundation, is estimated to be up to 8,000.The population of Pakistanis in Oman, according to the Overseas Pakistanis Foundation, is estimated to be around 85,000.The population of Palmer Heights was 3,762 at the 2010 census.The population of Palmer Township was 20,691 at the 2010 census.The population of Palominas as of the 2010 U.S. Census was 212.The population of Pandamatenga was 1,545 in the 2001 census.
