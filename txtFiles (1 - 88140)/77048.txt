heavily eroded by subsequent impacts to the point where the edge is little more than a curving rise in the surface.The rim of Alhazen is nearly circular, but appears highly oblong when viewed from the north and southwest.The rim of Amici has been eroded and distorted by subsequent impacts, so that is now has a somewhat polygonal shape.The rim of Amundsen is slightly distended along the southern
