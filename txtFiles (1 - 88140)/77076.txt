The River Isbourne is 14 mile (22km) long tributary of the River Avon which flows through Gloucestershire and Worcestershire in the Midlands of England.The River Isbourne valley divides Hinton into the "East Village", near to the church, and the "West Village".The river is bridged by Alberta Highway 2 and flows past the hamlet of Assineau, Alberta.The river is bridged by Alberta Highway 2 and flows past the hamlet of Driftpile, Alberta, as well as the Driftpile River First Nation Indian Reserve.The river is bridged by both the B4358 and the A483 roads.The river is brown and murky.The river is by a valley and a garden equipped with a children's play area.The river is called Nant Cerrig-y-groes at its source near Moel y Llyn.The river is called the Suam River from its source to the border with the West Pokot County of Kenya.The river is carefully navigable in its upper reaches past the Chignetco bridge during high tides.The river is characterized by long meandering sections through clay and sand valleys alternated by sections through eroded bedrock and marshes.The river is completely covered on a reach of 0.9 kilometers within the city of Slatina.The river is considered a flood risk due to several damaging flash floods in history.The river is crossed by a stone bridge, which leads to the hamlet of Crew Green about a mile east of the village, and very close to English border.The river is crossed by three bridges: one carrying the A830 trunk road, an older bridge for the B8008 road, and one for the West Highland Line railway.The river is crossed by two historically significant bridges, the curved Manuherikia Bridge No.1 (number 70 on the Otago Central Railway line), a concrete pier bridge completed in 1903, and a stone pier bridge at Ophir built in 1880.The river is currently designated as catch and release for Salmon.The river is currently shown on maps as Otututu (Rough) River, although this orthography is no longer common for New Zealand Rivers.The river is dammed at Lake Mutirikwe, which is recognized as an important wetland, and at Bangala Dam near Renco.The river is dammed at the nearby Secord Dam, providing water frontage for many vacation homes.The river is dammed to form Lake Summit, in Tuxedo, North Carolina, and Lake Adger near Mill Spring, NC.The river is dammed to form the Kalammawadi reservoir in the west of Kohlapur district.
