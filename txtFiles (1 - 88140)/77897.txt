The seminary used to have a high school department which was phased out at some time.The seminary was a founding member of the Graduate Theological Union (GTU) in Berkeley, the largest consortium of graduate schools and seminaries in the United States.The seminary was canonically established in 1988 by Pope John Paul II in accordance with his letter Ecclesia Dei adflicta and opened in 2000.The seminary was established in 1850 by German pastors in what was then the American frontier.The seminary was established in 1942 as ministerial training institution by three sponsoring denomination.The Seminary was established in 1970 through the efforts of Imus Bishop Felix Paz Perez.The seminary was established in 1978, originally located at Luther House, Petaling Jaya, Selangor.The seminary was established in 1992 by Norman Geisler and Ross Rhoads.The Seminary was established in 2007, it is located along Maguey Road in Barangay San Luis in Antipolo City, which is part of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Antipolo under its diocesan bishop, Most Rev. Francisco M. de Leon, DD.
