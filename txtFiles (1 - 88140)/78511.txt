The single was one of the highest digital debuts in the United States at the time and remained at the top of the US Billboard Hot 100 for seven consecutive weeks.The single was one of the most successful of Warwick's international hits, selling over 3,500,000 copies worldwide.The single was one of the year's biggest radio hits, after being played first in the UK on the Dermot O'Leary Show on Radio 2, along with their first live radio session.The single was one of two new tracks which appeared on the re-issue of the album in 2002.The single was only available for sale through Prince's online retail outlet and during concerts.The single was only available via an offer from Misfits Records where proofs of purchase had to be sent in from Osaka Popstar's Osaka Popstar and the American Legends of Punk, Balzac's Beyond the Darkness and The Misfits' Project 1950.The single was only ever released in Japan and Australia as a "Souvenir EP" to coincide with the "Dirty Honeymoon Tour" in Australia and the Southern Hemisphere.The single was only issued in Belgium, France, Germany, Luxembourg and The Netherlands.The single was only moderately successful in the U.S., where it peaked at number 19 on the Modern Rock Tracks chart.
