The song was also the final recording by the massively successful 1975-84 Styx lineup of DeYoung, Shaw, Young, and both Panozzo twins; by the time Shaw returned to the band in 1995, John Panozzo's declining health prevented his participation.The song was also the final single featuring Ron Welty on drums.The song was also the first by AC/DC to chart on Billboard Alternative Songs chart.The song was also the first by the band to chart in Canada, reaching number 8 in the Canadian Rock-Alternative chart.
