The township's name refers to its creation from the northern part of Center Township and the southern part of Owen.The townships of Artemesia, Osprey, Proton and Melancthon were excluded from the south riding.The townships of Coleshill Fawr and Coleshill Fechan were eventually merged into the Borough of Flint in 1934.The townships of Halesowen, Cakemore, Hasbury, Hawne, Hill, Hunnington, Illey, Lapal, Oldbury, Ridgacre, Romsley and Warley Salop formed a detached part of Shropshire; the rest of the parish, consisting of the chapelries of Cradley and Frankley, and the hamlets of Lutley and Warley Wigorn, was part of Worcestershire.
