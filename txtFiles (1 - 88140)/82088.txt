They signed to Asian Man Records, who released their eleventh studio album, Munki Brain (2007), and also reissued all of their Lookout!They signed to Atco Records in 1974, a division of Atlantic Records at that time, now a division of Warner Brothers.They signed to Bailey Way Entertainment for the release of their next single, "Giddy Up".They signed to Big Phat Adelaide Records and Modern Music in 2006 and released their first EP, Salvaging What's Left.They signed to Blood & Ink Records, where they released, Turning a Blind Eye, another studio album, in 2013.They signed to Bronco Records in 1975, and planned a second album to be titled Street Level.They signed to Columbia Records in 2010.They signed to Columbia Records label Startime International and released their first album in 2015.They signed to Come&Live!They signed to Cooking Vinyl for their final two albums in 1999 and 2000.They signed to Crusade Records late that year and released their debut extended play, A Small Misunderstanding Leads to Disaster, in February 2006.They signed to Deathwish Inc. in 2010, and released their debut album Early Frost in 2013.They signed to Dolton Records in 1959 and had several songs on the Billboard Hot 100.They signed to Don Giovanni Records in 2012.They signed to EMI Parlophone and had a hit single in Europe (Top 10 in France) with "Turkish Delight".They signed to Future 80's Records and released the album Planet Of Storms in 2015.
