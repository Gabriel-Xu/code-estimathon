World Rugby has reset the tournament on a new four-year cycle to avoid conflict with the Olympics and Women's World Cup Sevens; World Cups will thus be held every four years after 2017.The first Women's Rugby World Cup was held in 1991 and won by the United States.World Rugby is a sports management video game developed by Brendan O'Brien and David Taylor.World Rugby is the world governing body for the sport of rugby union and rugby sevens.World Rugby Junior Player of the Year is an accolade awarded annually by the World Rugby.World Rugby League is a organization which organizes rugby league.World Rugby organizes the Rugby World Cup every four years, the sport's most recognized and most profitable competition.World Rugby's headquarters are in Dublin, Ireland.
