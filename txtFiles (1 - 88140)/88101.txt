Zimmermann rejected the assumption of fixity.Zimmermann represented the Mannheimer ERC and was coached by Peter Sczypa.Zimmermann started skating when he was ten years old in 1996 and turned professional in 2003.Zimmermann studied composition in Germany with Werner Heider and Mauricio Kagel, the theory of musical intelligence at the Institute of Sonology in Utrecht (now located in The Hague), and computer music at Colgate University in New York.Zimmermann was founded in 1991 by sisters Nicky and Simone Zimmermann.Zimmerman played college baseball at the University of Virginia and graduated from Kellam High School.Zimmerman played for the Minnesota Vikings from 1986 to 1992 and for the Denver Broncos from 1993 to 1997.Zimmerman ran twice for mayor of Vancouver, both times unsuccessfully.Zimmerman received a Saturn Award nomination as Best Director for the film, a predecessor to more well-known modern parodies of the horror genre.
