The Innsbruck-Land District is a district in Tyrol, Austria. It encloses the statutory city Innsbruck, and borders Bavaria (Germany) in the north, the district Schwaz in the east, South Tyrol in Italy to the south, and the district of Imst in the west.It has an area of 1,990.17 km². As of 2021, 181,698 people lived there.
