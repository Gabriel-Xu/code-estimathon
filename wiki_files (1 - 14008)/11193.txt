Brazil is one of the most biodiverse countries in the world. Brazil is home to many hummingbirds, toucans, parrots, macaws, waterfowl and birds of prey. Mammals living in Brazil are anteaters, pumas, jaguars, armadillos, otters and dolphins. Caimans, turtles and anacondas are reptiles found in Brazil. Brazil happens to have some neat flora including the very tall Brazil Nut Trees, Brazilwood, Myriad Palms, Kapok bearing Ceiba Trees, Rosewood, and Water Lilies.
