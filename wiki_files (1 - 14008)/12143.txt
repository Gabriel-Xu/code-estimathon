The site is part of East Tennessee State University, and the Gray Fossil Site & Museum opened on the site in 2007.Once, the land around the Gray Fossil Site was a pond inside a sinkhole surrounded by a warm, wet forest. The fossils found in the area show what lived and died in and around the sinkhole pond.As the first site of its age known from the Appalachian region, the Gray Fossil Site is a unique window into the past.
