Shen Lyu-shun (; 12 November 1949 – 6 January 2023) was a Taiwanese diplomat. He was notably the representative of Taiwan to the United Kingdom from 2011 to 2014 and to the United States from 2014 to 2016. He was the representative to the European Union and Belgium from 2008 to 2009. He was also deputy minister of foreign affairs from 2009 to 2011. Shen was born in Taipei.Shen died on 6 January 2023 in New Taipei.
