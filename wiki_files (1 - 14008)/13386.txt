"Motley Crew" is a song by American rapper Post Malone. The song was released on July 9, 2021. The song was able to make it to #13 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 and #3 on the "Billboard" Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs. The title of the song is a reference to the American heavy metal band Mötley Crüe.On July 8, 2021, a music video for the song was released. It was produced by Cole Bennett. In the video, Malone is driving around the Auto Club Speedway in Fontana, California.
