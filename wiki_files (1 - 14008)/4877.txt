A Scantron test scoring system is a multiple-choice, fill-in-the-bubble form. Scantrons are then fed through a machine that grades them. The Scantron is a very progressive form of grading. It eliminates human error on the grader's behalf, and takes much less time.The technology of this system dates back to the nineteenth century using optical mark recognition, telegraph lines, and punch tape.
