The German defenders were finally driven from their positions, but with many losses for the Allies.The Allied landings in Italy in September 1943 were followed by an advance north in two parts. An advance was made on each side of the mountain range in the middle of Italy.On the western side, U.S. Fifth Army moved from Naples. In the east General Sir Bernard Montgomery's British Eighth Army moved up the Adriatic coast.The Fifth Army made slow progress because of difficult land and German defences. The Germans were protected in positions.
