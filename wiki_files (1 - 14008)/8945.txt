Jerry AdrianiJerry Adriani (Jair Alves de Sousa; 29 January 1947 – 23 April 2017) was a Brazilian singer, musician and actor. He began his career in television while working with Rede Tupi in São Paulo. His early works were as a vocalist for the group "Os Rebeldes".Adriani died at a hospital in Rio de Janeiro from cancer on 23 April 2017, aged 70.Jean PérimonyJean Périmony (2 March 1931 – 9 April 2017) was a French comedian, actor, educator, theater director, and costume designer. He was born in Dole, Jura. He was known for working with assistant director Robert Manuel and then with Henri Rollan. He created the "Cie du Villiers" (1969) and the Festival Molière at the Théâtre Trévise in Paris (1977).
