Yitzhak Rabin was one of the three recipients of the 1993 Nobel Peace Prize. The others were Yasser Arafat and Shimon Peres. All three got the prizes for trying to make peace in the Middle East.Yitzhak Rabin was shot dead in Tel Aviv on November 4, 1995.
