The hypothetical chain of events leads to a significant (usually negative) result. So unintended consequences follow the first step in the chain of events.An example of this is the domino effect theory. During the Vietnam War, many people in the United States believed that if Vietnam were to unite and become communist, then the rest of Southeast Asia would eventually become communist too, unless the United States got involved in actively stopping communism.A slippery slope argument can also be a slippery slope fallacy. Fallacies are statements that sound reasonable but are actually logically flawed or sometimes even dishonest. Whether or not a slippery slope argument is a fallacy depends on whether it becomes true "necessarily" or just in practice. In many cases it just acts as a warning, and readers have to use common sense as to its truth value.
