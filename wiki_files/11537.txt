Sulzbach-Rosenberg ( "Suizboch-Rosnberg" or "Suizbach-Roasnberg") is a municipality in Amberg-Sulzbach, a district ("Landkreis") in the Upper Palatinate. It is situated approximately northwest of Amberg, and east of Nuremberg.
