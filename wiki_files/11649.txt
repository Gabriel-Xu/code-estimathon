Going in Style is a 2017 American heist comedy movie directed by Zach Braff and written by Theodore Melfi. It is a remake of the 1979 movie "Going in Style". It stars Morgan Freeman, Michael Caine, Alan Arkin, Joey King, Matt Dillon, Christopher Lloyd, Ann-Margret, John Ortiz and Siobhan Fallon Hogan. It is about three elderly men who plan to rob a bank after their pensions are canceled.It was released in the United States on April 7, 2017.
