Chantal Haenen (born 22 April 1997) is a Dutch para cyclist. She competed at the 2020 Summer Paralympics.Before being impaired Haenen was involved in dressage. When she was 18 years old she was involved in a traffic accident that resulted in amputation of her legs.She started with para cycling in 2017.
