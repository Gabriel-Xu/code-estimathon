At the age of 16, Hans Bøgh-Sørensen assumed control of 30 percent of the family's juice business.In 1982, he returned from a job at Mærsk Data to take up the role of deputy director in the family's juice business. He held this position until he became owner and managing director in 1987.Hans Bøgh-Sørensen established the pharmaceutical company "Orifarm" in 1994.In 2019, he resigned as CEO of Orifarm after more than 25 years at the helm. He continued as chairman of the board and is still active in the company.In 2000, Hans Bøgh-Sørensen received the prize as "Entrepreneur of the Year", Denmark and in 2008 the "Tietgen" prize .
