William "Will" Turner is a blacksmith and self-taught swordsman who lives in Port Royal. He is played by Orlando Bloom.Will was found holding on to a piece of wood at sea as a boy and rescued by a ship traveling to Port Royal. He falls in love with Elizabeth Swann, the governor's daughter, and wants to marry her. However, because she is rich and he is poor, it does not seem possible. When Elizabeth is taken by Barbossa, Will teams up with Jack Sparrow to rescue her. Sparrow tells Will that Will's father, Bootstrap Turner was one of his crew on the "Black Pearl". Bootstrap Turner was the only one on the crew who did not want to mutiny against Sparrow.Will finally marries Elizabeth in "." They have a son, Henry. However, Will is made captain of the "Flying Dutchman", and is only allowed to return to port every ten years.James Norrington is a British Royal Navy Officer.
