The Nintendo Wii had Netflix starting on March 27, 2010. It was only for American and Canadian owners, but a Netflix disc was required to use the software. As of October 18, 2010, American and Canadian Wii owners could download Netflix as a channel without requiring a disc.Netflix has been discontinued for the Wii on January 30, 2019.The Nintendo Wii, DS and DSi can use the Internet with a downloadable web browser. The Nintendo Wii, DSi, and DS browsers runs on Opera. The Nintendo DS's web browser requires a DS card and a RAM expansion through the Game Boy Advance port.The "Forecast Channel" first became available on December 19, 2006, and was shut down on June 28, 2013, due to the discontinuation of WiiConnect24 which it required. People were allowed to use the channel to see the current forecast, the UV index, today's overall weather, tomorrow's forecast, a 5-day forecast (only for the country you live in), and a laundry check (Japan only). Certain games like "Madden NFL 07", "", and "Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games" could use the "Forecast Channel" to put custom weather conditions in-game.The "News Channel" allows people to access news headlines and current events from the Internet. News articles are available on a globe view similar to the "Forecast Channel", and on the sidebar. The "News Channel" became available in North America, Europe, and Australia on January 26, 2007.
