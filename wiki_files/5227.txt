Well-formed belemnite guards can be found in rocks dating from the Mississippian (or Lower Carboniferous).Belemnites were numerous during the Jurassic and Cretaceous periods, and their fossils are abundant in Mesozoic marine rocks, often with their cousins the ammonites.
