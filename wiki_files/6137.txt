It was a Second World War Allied attack on the German-occupied port of Dieppe. After less than 10 hours since the first landings, the last Allied troops had all been either killed, evacuated, or captured by the Germans.Instead of showing the British commitment, the terrible number of Allied deaths showed that the Allies were not ready to invade France for a long time.
