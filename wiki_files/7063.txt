He became known for his role in the balanced budget of the 1990s. He was an advocate of cutting spending to make government work better for less money. He also worked with Ralph Nader to make less tax loopholes for corporations.In 1998, he was the main force behind the balanced budget that was signed by President Bill Clinton. He ran for president in 2000, but did not succeed. He then hosted a TV show on Fox News for a time. The show was called "Heartland with John Kasich".He defeated Ted Strickland in the 2010 Ohio gubernatorial election.Kasich was re-elected in 2014. He got the majority of votes in 86 of the 88 Ohio counties.In April 2015, he announced that he made his New Day For America group. It was a 527 group before it became a super PAC in July.In May 2015, sources close to him said he was "virtually certain" to run for the Republican nomination for president.Kasich announced his candidacy for president on July 21 at Ohio State University.
