Kenneth Welsh, CM (March 30, 1942 – May 5, 2022) was a Canadian-American movie and television actor (sometimes credited as Ken Welsh). He was known for his roles in "Twin Peaks" and in "The Aviator". He also appeared in "The Day Reagan Was Shot" and in "The Day After Tomorrow".Welsh died on May 5, 2022 in Toronto, Ontario from cancer at the age of 80.Edwin Meese
