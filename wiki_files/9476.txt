Tom Murphy (23 February 1935 – 15 May 2018) was an Irish playwright. He worked closely with the Abbey Theatre in Dublin and with Druid Theatre, Galway. He was born in County Galway, Ireland and later lived in Dublin.Murphy's first successful play, "A Whistle in the Dark", was performed at the Theatre Royal Stratford East in London in 1961. It was very controversial. He also wrote "The Sanctuary Lamp", "Famine", "The Gigli Concert", and "Conversations on a Homecoming". He wrote one novel, "The Seduction of Morality" (1994).
